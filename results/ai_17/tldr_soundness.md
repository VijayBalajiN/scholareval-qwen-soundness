## Strengths 
The strongest support is for the core search-and-feedback idea: test-time MCTS over states with process-level AI feedback produced large gains in web-agent tasks [(Putta et al., 2024-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b393f619a87c5b6aa63c7abc7118263205b6aa62), and MCTS with LLM rubric feedback is a plausible RAG query-refinement analogue [(Hu et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/79fa21c6d9ee706c694475b56be8612f21a3f04b). The centralized-critic, decentralized-actor design is supported by multi-agent RL evidence [(Iqbal, and Sha, 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/929bef0066bad871ba971b673c053112d055d29f) and by critic-guided code execution feedback [(Gotmare et al., 2022-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6d994b4f5a46cd14e8f09f1e9e49120546b15e31). KB-side editing is supported by learned RAG knowledge-partition agents [(Wang et al., 2024-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8d0df3168870fd17b36ecd5575e406feb5a5a1b5) and by the directly comparable ARKS execution-feedback setting [(Su et al., 2024-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0874581dabb0af63971fa3026779d4e1e4ea5f61). The PROMPT AGENT-E baseline is also well grounded in MCTS prompt optimization [(Wang et al., 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1eb1a8c7f88de27af224153f43ecdd41774600f2).

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions concern the unvalidated MCTS budget: depth=3, width=3, 5 iterations, and c=2.5 are not supported by convergence evidence [(Kocsis, and Szepesvari, 2006-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e635d81a617d1239232a9c9a11a196c53dab8240) or by LLM-agent MCTS budgets [(Zhou et al., 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/700bd9681f1b9e9e2212e10415d27b11c7e6836b), and effective combinatorial MCTS can require far more simulations [(Chowdhury et al., 2024-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f31ab3e13618b7d1bf1fe7b695f54f7c27154fc2). Centralized critics can also have high variance and instability in multi-agent settings [(Lyu et al., 2023-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4509f46c875f48821d42f3c6a5a95a2c124f08f1). Document-level decomposition and 50-line chunking are risky because extracted sections or triplets can interfere with retrieval [(Wu et al., 2024-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5040acef429108015991dc5480f1cedf6851f0b7) [(Wu et al., 2023-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/440ca16479ece9ed61461032b9b2fcde36c8ae7a), external-memory editing degrades at scale [(Zhong et al., 2023-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/56e952fd463accff09cf2e35432aaabd7c7c57f3), and more retrieved context can reduce knowledge precision [(Shuster et al., 2021-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a2a7033a5a859e3a6e6f0a83018326400b4c5faa). Execution accuracy is also sensitive to test quality and false positives [(Li et al., 2022-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5cbe278b65a81602a864184bbca37de91448a5f5) [(Dong et al., 2023-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a95817df43516f5ff9cfc447209def1fcd569afc), and G-Eval with GPT-4 is not uniformly the best coherence judge [(Zhu et al., 2024-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5ffd59819bfd3bd908056e45c4ce2f932b70ecdf) [(Peng et al., 2024-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ff7457e8e389e7f20448cf33d0bc6205daa47a71).

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Replace the fixed MCTS configuration with a search-budget and component ablation: sweep iterations 5, 20, 50, and 100; depths 2-5; expansion widths 2-5; and exploration constants 1.0, 2.5, and 4.0, while comparing full MAC-CAFE against no MCTS, no centralized critic, single-actor, and PROMPT AGENT-E. Use the centralized critic to provide value estimates or learned guidance for document-edit selection, and report LLM calls, wall-clock time, and confidence intervals. This directly addresses the unvalidated shallow UCT setting [(Kocsis, and Szepesvari, 2006-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e635d81a617d1239232a9c9a11a196c53dab8240) [(Chowdhury et al., 2024-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f31ab3e13618b7d1bf1fe7b695f54f7c27154fc2) and isolates whether multi-actor centralized-critic MCTS is necessary [(Putta et al., 2024-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b393f619a87c5b6aa63c7abc7118263205b6aa62) [(Hu et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/79fa21c6d9ee706c694475b56be8612f21a3f04b) [(Wang et al., 2024-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8d0df3168870fd17b36ecd5575e406feb5a5a1b5) [(Wang et al., 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1eb1a8c7f88de27af224153f43ecdd41774600f2).

Add section-quality, retrieval-interference, and scaling controls before relying on MCTS: compare 50-line chunks against semantic or triple-validated sections, report section precision/recall, retrieval precision@k, false-section retrieval rate, and downstream RAG accuracy, and test edit scales such as 1, 10, 100, and 1000 edits. Include locality, retention, multi-hop, and leakage-aware probes so that gains are not caused by overlapping or noisy KB content. This addresses decomposition interference [(Wu et al., 2024-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5040acef429108015991dc5480f1cedf6851f0b7) [(Wu et al., 2023-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/440ca16479ece9ed61461032b9b2fcde36c8ae7a), scaling degradation [(Zhong et al., 2023-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/56e952fd463accff09cf2e35432aaabd7c7c57f3), retrieval noise [(Shuster et al., 2021-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a2a7033a5a859e3a6e6f0a83018326400b4c5faa), and leakage risk [(Borgeaud et al., 2021-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/002c256d30d6be4b23d365a8de8ae0e67e4c9641).

Validate the execution and coherence evaluation protocols: build a hidden-test execution harness for ARKS-Pony and ARKS-Ring with stratified 1:1:2 failure-case splits, report strict pass@1, average pass ratio, syntax/runtime/functional error rates, and bootstrap confidence intervals, and use the eval split only for MCTS selection or early stopping. For coherence, validate G-Eval with GPT-4 against human ratings and alternative coherence models, and add hallucination, locality, and multi-hop probes. This addresses execution-metric fragility [(Li et al., 2022-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5cbe278b65a81602a864184bbca37de91448a5f5) [(Dong et al., 2023-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a95817df43516f5ff9cfc447209def1fcd569afc) [(Inala et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/075b6fb7d3787953164eecc1bd2e13f97c9f3c44) and judge limitations [(Zhu et al., 2024-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5ffd59819bfd3bd908056e45c4ce2f932b70ecdf) [(Peng et al., 2024-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ff7457e8e389e7f20448cf33d0bc6205daa47a71) [(Yao et al., 2023-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f5c73d9e6641b018b633690102121f5605d34fb0).
