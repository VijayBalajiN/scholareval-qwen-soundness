## Strengths 
The strongest support is for regularized optimal transport on single-cell data, because FactoredOT improved scRNA-seq alignment over plain and entropic OT [(Forrow et al., 2018-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a851c9245c82a94ecac4eef87a196339eebf400c), and structured graph OT supports pathway-aware sender-receiver transport [(Vayer et al., 2018-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6f9de3cd77ac32ce00da3ba6b71e2360b5b2a81c) with entropic OT providing a practical distance primitive [(Cuturi, 2013-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0080118b0eb02af581ff32b85a1bb6aed7081f45). Pathway-aware intercellular signaling is supported by CytoTalk [(Hu et al., 2021-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ed238585b4dafdc6389eeac6f786e3ecd6c8d4d7), PathFinder [(Feng et al., 2024-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/14be81e003b6638090dd044b97488787a1583b98), MultiNicheNet [(Browaeys et al., 2023-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/bb4b22dada82dd5a7f953aad033d04ae305f3cdd), and scDiffCom [(Lagger et al., 2021-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cea7f51aed8b859097ba1c5dad41254fc0c7b793). LRP-KG construction is supported by GrowDAGs [(Köse et al., 2022-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ba3ddc4111caccdfab9958a2b093dc5a49fd7d61) and receptor-ligand schema work [(Efremova et al., 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6a3b3e4a2eebc3fad3b03ee6d8228264247abbc8). Benchmarking is supported by CellChat's 6 human and 5 mouse design [(Jin et al., 2020-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a3cd148f5795617b281a3ee1738abf19e2476bb0), biomedical KG baselines [(Krix et al., 2022-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/dd13cff173b970b24eba00aacb3e0209722f8b5b), and filtered KG evaluation [(Bonner et al., 2021-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9e712910ff1b81b6fe2a5e71515b68e91e679e79).

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions are that no supplied work quantitatively validates pathway-level KGOT over existing CCI methods, and plain or entropic OT can underperform regularized FactoredOT on scRNA-seq [(Forrow et al., 2018-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a851c9245c82a94ecac4eef87a196339eebf400c). Pathway graph reconstruction is sensitive to cost functions and prior interactomes, so a static KEGG/Reactome LRP-KG may be noisy or unstable [(Köse et al., 2022-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ba3ddc4111caccdfab9958a2b093dc5a49fd7d61). Simple ligand-receptor expression, correlation, or network crosstalk baselines may already capture much of the signal [(Zhou et al., 2017-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/95198116d59bed1aba8a68fa89db0f51301579f9), [(Hu et al., 2021-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ed238585b4dafdc6389eeac6f786e3ecd6c8d4d7). Evaluation is also mismatched: filtered MR and Hits@K are sensitive to negative sampling, filtering, and tie handling [(Trouillon et al., 2016-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2218e2e1df2c3adfb70e0def2e326a39928aacfc), [(Li et al., 2023-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f442378ead6282024cf5b9046daa10422fe9fc5f), while CCI methods are often validated qualitatively or with spatial/proteomic evidence rather than the proposed accuracy and percentile rank [(Browaeys et al., 2023-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/bb4b22dada82dd5a7f953aad033d04ae305f3cdd), [(Luo et al., 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/077e018171fde37914036ecd2c9d8da5a87a8e53). The 6 human and 5 mouse at-least-one-LR-pair criterion and 100 permutation iterations may be insufficient for stable significance [(Jin et al., 2020-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a3cd148f5795617b281a3ee1738abf19e2476bb0), [(Dimitrov et al., 2021-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/147b83171b2d5a6a05f7384af6cccd43de20df24).

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Build and benchmark the LRP-KG before applying OT: define node types for ligand, receptor, complex, gene, pathway, and regulator; define edge types for ligand-receptor binding, receptor-to-pathway, pathway-to-gene, and pathway-to-regulator interactions; use versioned KEGG and Reactome plus a receptor-ligand schema [(Efremova et al., 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6a3b3e4a2eebc3fad3b03ee6d8228264247abbc8); assign evidence weights and prune redundant edges using GrowDAGs-style cost functions [(Köse et al., 2022-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ba3ddc4111caccdfab9958a2b093dc5a49fd7d61); and report KEGG-only, Reactome-only, and combined graph metrics plus FactDrop and TypeDrop ablations.

Create a unified ground-truth and statistical evaluation protocol: curate LR-pathway positives from perturbation or pathway-activity labels and add spatial co-localization validation where available [(Browaeys et al., 2023-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/bb4b22dada82dd5a7f953aad033d04ae305f3cdd), [(Luo et al., 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/077e018171fde37914036ecd2c9d8da5a87a8e53); report AUC, precision, recall, F1, MR, Hits@K, accuracy, percentile rank, confidence intervals, and permutation p-values with more than 100 permutations or an exact null [(Dimitrov et al., 2021-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/147b83171b2d5a6a05f7384af6cccd43de20df24); and specify filtered negative sampling, tie handling, and hard-negative sensitivity [(Krix et al., 2022-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/dd13cff173b970b24eba00aacb3e0209722f8b5b), [(Li et al., 2023-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f442378ead6282024cf5b9046daa10422fe9fc5f).

Make the OT model and baselines directly comparable: implement entropic, fused-structure, and low-rank or transport-rank regularized OT with tuned lambda, alpha, gamma, and convergence checks [(Cuturi, 2013-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0080118b0eb02af581ff32b85a1bb6aed7081f45), [(Vayer et al., 2018-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6f9de3cd77ac32ce00da3ba6b71e2360b5b2a81c), [(Forrow et al., 2018-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a851c9245c82a94ecac4eef87a196339eebf400c); compare gene-abundance versus cell-count marginals and expression versus pathway-informed costs; and benchmark against CytoTalk or scDiffCom, NicheNet or MultiNicheNet, GraphComm, CellChat, CellPhoneDB, SingleCellSignalR, and CellCall with receptor-masked and spatial validation where available [(Hu et al., 2021-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ed238585b4dafdc6389eeac6f786e3ecd6c8d4d7), [(Lagger et al., 2021-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cea7f51aed8b859097ba1c5dad41254fc0c7b793), [(Browaeys et al., 2023-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/bb4b22dada82dd5a7f953aad033d04ae305f3cdd), [(So et al., 2024-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/93c62304b68e4dce4db66251bb28714c613a8b22), [(Jin et al., 2020-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a3cd148f5795617b281a3ee1738abf19e2476bb0).
