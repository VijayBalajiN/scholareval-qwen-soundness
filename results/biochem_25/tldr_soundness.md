## Strengths 
The proposal's core methods have the strongest literature support for (1) using Shield-1-dependent destabilizing domains to induce conditional protein stress, as shown by [(Miyazaki et al., 2016-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/700fb459fbe0b33f23713befdb1f424d7c97c524); (2) RNA-seq differential expression and time-course modeling with DESeq2-style negative-binomial GLMs, FDR control, and rlog-based clustering, supported by [(Love et al., 2014-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/817fc8de25f989b8229847fc7710750f273460b); (3) fluorescent degron reporters for proteasome/ubiquitin degradation readouts, supported by [(Beer et al., 2019-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b773b974d4ab13b96856205fe70133284f5e0198); and (4) CRISPR TP53 knockout plus transcriptomic validation to test p53 dependence, supported by [(Ihry et al., 2018-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a7b90250a4fc1ed8be4241a34709186d5a525d1e) and [(Kirschner et al., 2015-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5e7d1dbb4f2647152acdc21f93a616ce04e8cfb5). Integrated p53 RNA-seq/ChIP-seq and orthogonal validation in mouse fibroblasts is also supported by [(Broz et al., 2013-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/87470ea28edc3b5d3fc01d9cc49640fec3caa38d), while supervised transcriptomic separation and network enrichment are supported by [(Nassiri et al., 2019-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7a4fe93ed0c4eab5fe0f25724eec63f2084c1b2f).

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions concern interpretation rather than feasibility. First, DD withdrawal may produce aggregates rather than a clean soluble unfolded-protein signal, as noted for AgDD in [(Miyazaki et al., 2016-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/700fb459fbe0b33f23713befdb1f424d7c97c524), which could confound nuclear versus cytosolic PQC and p53 activation. Second, compartment-specific degron reporter accumulation may reflect membrane topology, organelle protection, or ubiquitin ligase/adaptor accessibility rather than proteasome capacity, as shown by [(Beer et al., 2019-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b773b974d4ab13b96856205fe70133284f5e0198). Third, the p53 knockout evidence is indirect: [(Ihry et al., 2018-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a7b90250a4fc1ed8be4241a34709186d5a525d1e) and [(Kirschner et al., 2015-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5e7d1dbb4f2647152acdc21f93a616ce04e8cfb5) do not test nuclear/cytosolic DD stress in NIH3T3 cells and suggest p53 outputs are context- and duration-dependent, so knockout may remove only a subset of the response. Fourth, DESeq2 supports differential expression but not pathway enrichment, as highlighted in [(Love et al., 2014-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/817fc8de25f989b8229847fc7710750f273460b), and the generic statistical plan lacks the explicit replication, thresholds, and functional readouts supported by [(Broz et al., 2013-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/87470ea28edc3b5d3fc01d9cc49640fec3caa38d) and [(Nassiri et al., 2019-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7a4fe93ed0c4eab5fe0f25724eec63f2084c1b2f).

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Before RNA-seq or p53 assays, validate that NucDD, CytoDD, and ntDD behave as soluble, proteasome-degraded unfolded proteins in the intended compartments: confirm localization, matched expression, and degradation kinetics by microscopy/flow cytometry; test aggregate formation by native PAGE, detergent-insoluble fractionation, or size-exclusion; and include Shield-1-present, non-targeted, and proteasome-inhibited controls to distinguish reversible unfolded-protein stress from aggregate or proteasome-overload phenotypes, using [(Miyazaki et al., 2016-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/700fb459fbe0b33f23713befdb1f424d7c97c524) as proof-of-concept rather than quantitative benchmark.

Make the proteasome-capacity readout interpretable by validating compartment-specific degron reporters and adding orthogonal controls: confirm NLS/NES/non-targeted localization and baseline degradation; use both ubiquitin-dependent and ubiquitin-independent reporters; normalize fluorescence to reporter and DD expression; measure at 0, 15, 30, 60, 120, and 240 minutes with flow cytometry or microscopy; and include proteasome inhibitors, nuclear/cytosolic fractionation, or direct proteasome activity assays to separate proteasome capacity from ligase/adaptor accessibility or membrane-topology effects, as cautioned by [(Beer et al., 2019-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b773b974d4ab13b96856205fe70133284f5e0198).

Specify a rigorous p53-dependence and statistical plan: generate multiple NIH3T3 TP53 knockout clones with indel/sequencing validation, p53 protein/mRNA loss confirmation, and rescue or empty-vector controls; run WT and KO lines with matched DD constructs in a DESeq2-style condition + time + condition:time design with FDR-controlled differential expression, separate pathway enrichment, clustering, and RT-qPCR/Western validation; add ChIP-seq or CUT&Tag for direct p53 targets; and replace or supplement cell-cycle analysis with apoptosis, viability, proliferation, and transformation assays, following [(Ihry et al., 2018-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a7b90250a4fc1ed8be4241a34709186d5a525d1e), [(Kirschner et al., 2015-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5e7d1dbb4f2647152acdc21f93a616ce04e8cfb5), [(Love et al., 2014-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/817fc8de25f989b8229847fc7710750f273460b), [(Broz et al., 2013-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/87470ea28edc3b5d3fc01d9cc49640fec3caa38d), and [(Nassiri et al., 2019-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7a4fe93ed0c4eab5fe0f25724eec63f2084c1b2f).
