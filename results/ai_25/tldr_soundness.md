## Strengths 
The best-supported components are inference-time candidate-path expansion and selection, learned decoding-order preferences, generation-oriented corruption/masking, and the evaluation protocol. Multi-path expansion with probability, validity, or utility-based selection is supported by [(Mao et al., unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/514627a1a81152f03de0c1e0e9e73525fb8da03c), [(Suzgun et al., 2022-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a4bdc300db297756f36bedee2859b62df8e268c2), [(Das et al., 2016-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d3d4273db96381330a98b1277fddc3907af95fbd), and [(Ding et al., unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f2396e95d152fb212d2f18102bac5c5453519bc2), while practical k-width search is supported by [(Miao et al., 2023-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f0c31511134abdd23f990310e8a2f2eb3a629b62). Learned path or order preferences for masked models are supported by [(Jiang, and Bansal, 2021-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e7f010513bebc23116f03cacf69290878d491f61), and iterative masked decoding by [(Ghazvininejad et al., 2019-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5efadc9019ce3378a0eb6c8f939cdde6c8918b1e) and [(Gao et al., 2019-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/36a3cfa6ed48988e23cf68b3abcbe097b29e594a). Modified pre-training corruption is supported by [(Lewis et al., 2019-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/395de0bd3837fdf4b4b5e5f04835bcc69c279481), [(Chang et al., 2022-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7c597874535c1537d7ddff3b3723015b4dc79d30), [(Chen et al., 2019-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/75352cc69a29bd5fc411e0e79737cb96b6309161), [(Joshi et al., 2019-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/81f5810fbbab9b7203b9556f4ce3c741875407bc), and [(Lan et al., 2019-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7a064df1aeada7e69e5173f7d4c8606f4470365b). The zero-shot and task-specific evaluation design is supported by [(Brown et al., 2020-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/90abbc2cf38462b954ae1b772fac9532e2ccd8b0), [(Li et al., 2023-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4cf7de6c10f871ef9bc4cde15b4417299da18b00), [(Liu et al., 2024-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f7310dac21abc6ba357bcd5e75fb2e6957a97303), [(Tuli et al., 2024-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/46b8e95c97d04ac3ddb782cf9a6421a45470fd59), [(Narayan et al., 2018-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/305b2cf37e5dece81e95c92883d5a6e28ac93b22), and [(Bao et al., 2022-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2ae0c69cac7bb2ba386faf6e9702db54b2ca594d). Ablation design is supported by [(Wan et al., 2023-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a4f116e63c30fb8ac11d90ed4d106cc6e9dc30cf), [(Lu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2c5bf29079cd958a2bef150077a02a1deb300652), and [(Post, and Vilar, 2018-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/823f335eee85b42502c8c6cb3ce38b4ae274ef89).

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions concern the central BERT-native generation claim and the selection rule. Hard highest-probability top-k selection may be weaker than soft aggregation, learned verification, or utility reranking, as shown by [(Das et al., 2016-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d3d4273db96381330a98b1277fddc3907af95fbd), [(Suzgun et al., 2022-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a4bdc300db297756f36bedee2859b62df8e268c2), [(Ding et al., unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f2396e95d152fb212d2f18102bac5c5453519bc2), and [(Mao et al., unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/514627a1a81152f03de0c1e0e9e73525fb8da03c). Learned path preferences are not uniformly better than simple heuristics, and DPO-style training is not directly validated, with length bottlenecks reported in [(Jiang, and Bansal, 2021-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e7f010513bebc23116f03cacf69290878d491f61) and instability under limited compute in [(Kim et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/744cd4de6b581ebb21bd4f2dc341a05b9d08f162). BERT-family generation may require distillation or more training, as indicated by [(Chen et al., 2019-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/75352cc69a29bd5fc411e0e79737cb96b6309161), [(Ketykó et al., 2021-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ab226ac6347b07f45d02bd7dda2d4d7e23a4478e), and [(Lewis et al., 2019-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/395de0bd3837fdf4b4b5e5f04835bcc69c279481), while the 150k-step budget may be underpowered relative to [(Dong et al., 2019-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1c71771c701aadfd72c5866170a9f5d71464bb88), [(Joshi et al., 2019-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/81f5810fbbab9b7203b9556f4ce3c741875407bc), and [(Lewis et al., 2019-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/395de0bd3837fdf4b4b5e5f04835bcc69c279481). Zero-shot BERT-family evaluation is also risky because prior BERT results are often fine-tuned, biased, or not directly comparable to AR baselines, as noted by [(Zellers et al., 2019-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8b0f27bb594b1eaaf493eaf1e2ee723a2b0a19ad), [(Sakaguchi et al., unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/92e121c6e114fe3cfb89370df03847c66a9b4e28), [(Hendrycks et al., 2020-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/814a4f680b9ba6baba23b93499f4b48af1a27678), and [(Yang et al., 2024-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f78c3d44cd27a3ccbe23cc3efd6fd13a120476f7). Finally, ROUGE/BLEU/METEOR may be insufficient, as shown by [(Chu et al., 2024-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a509397430eca1cd3970082ed57f9c490ef6e3ae), [(Zhang et al., 2019-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/295065d942abca0711300b2b4c39829551060578), and [(Lavie, and Agarwal, 2007-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/34d7a07c493ca6336c92156806a2947e115caadc), and beam/path ablations can show non-monotonic quality-speed tradeoffs, as in [(Wan et al., 2023-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a4f116e63c30fb8ac11d90ed4d106cc6e9dc30cf) and [(Holtzman et al., 2019-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cf4aa38ae31b43fd07abe13b4ffdb265babb7be1).

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Add a controlled path-selection ablation that reports oracle-to-top gaps: for each k and decoding budget, generate candidate paths, evaluate the oracle best path and the selected path on XSum and MSQG, and compare hard max, top-k average, LogSumExp, learned verifier, and BERTScore/BLEURT reranking under equal runtime, following [(Wan et al., 2023-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a4f116e63c30fb8ac11d90ed4d106cc6e9dc30cf), [(Das et al., 2016-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d3d4273db96381330a98b1277fddc3907af95fbd), [(Suzgun et al., 2022-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a4bdc300db297756f36bedee2859b62df8e268c2), [(Ding et al., unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f2396e95d152fb212d2f18102bac5c5453519bc2), and [(Miao et al., 2023-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f0c31511134abdd23f990310e8a2f2eb3a629b62).

Strengthen GeBERT and path selection* by adding matched pre-training and training ablations: compare prefix/suffix masking against random MLM, C-MLM, span/n-gram masking, and BART-style deletion/permutation; ablate DPO-only, DPO plus penalty plus MLM, lambda values, exact-match versus BLEU/BERTScore scoring, beam-derived negatives, verifier tokens, length-aware decoding, and frozen reference models, with convergence diagnostics and longer runs if needed, motivated by [(Lewis et al., 2019-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/395de0bd3837fdf4b4b5e5f04835bcc69c279481), [(Joshi et al., 2019-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/81f5810fbbab9b7203b9556f4ce3c741875407bc), [(Lan et al., 2019-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7a064df1aeada7e69e5173f7d4c8606f4470365b), [(Chen et al., 2019-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/75352cc69a29bd5fc411e0e79737cb96b6309161), [(Jiang, and Bansal, 2021-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e7f010513bebc23116f03cacf69290878d491f61), and [(Kim et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/744cd4de6b581ebb21bd4f2dc341a05b9d08f162).

Make evaluation fair and metric-robust: lock an lm-eval zero-shot protocol with templates, chance baselines, contamination checks, bias-controlled WinoGrande/HellaSwag analyses, multiple seeds, and confidence intervals; match AR baselines by parameters, tokens, data mix, and checkpoint; and supplement ROUGE/BLEU/METEOR with BERTScore, faithfulness metrics, keyword diagnostics, METEOR parameter sensitivity, and speed measurements, following [(Brown et al., 2020-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/90abbc2cf38462b954ae1b772fac9532e2ccd8b0), [(Li et al., 2023-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4cf7de6c10f871ef9bc4cde15b4417299da18b00), [(Liu et al., 2024-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f7310dac21abc6ba357bcd5e75fb2e6957a97303), [(Tuli et al., 2024-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/46b8e95c97d04ac3ddb782cf9a6421a45470fd59), [(Sakaguchi et al., unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/92e121c6e114fe3cfb89370df03847c66a9b4e28), [(Zellers et al., 2019-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8b0f27bb594b1eaaf493eaf1e2ee723a2b0a19ad), [(Chu et al., 2024-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a509397430eca1cd3970082ed57f9c490ef6e3ae), [(Zhang et al., 2019-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/295065d942abca0711300b2b4c39829551060578), and [(Lavie, and Agarwal, 2007-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/34d7a07c493ca6336c92156806a2947e115caadc).
