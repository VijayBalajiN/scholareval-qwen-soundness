## Strengths 
The strongest support is for the core causal-graph forecasting idea: a transfer-entropy matrix can be thresholded and used as a GNN adjacency matrix, as in [(Duan et al., 2020-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4fb598f7669a30ae783344de6bde02f14ba91117), and causal-graph GNN+LSTM can reduce MSE versus non-causal GNN+LSTM, as in [(Fatemi et al., 2023-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/21b1d3c9fa6f7dbae207a542a82ec27dad122fdd). TE-informed dynamic graph aggregation is further supported by [(Sriramulu et al., 2023-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/35c970fc96b86f719a303b1e02d63c52ec67b3ca) and [(Febrinanto et al., 2023-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/374376e2184f669322f842166a729f3bf54a22c7), while learned or heterogeneous adjacency matrices show that graph aggregation is broadly useful in [(Wu et al., 2020-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/75e924bd79d27a23f3f93d9b1ab62a779505c8d2), [(Wang et al., 2020-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/87e77ac0c054abddfef9cd8e579f10c8559217c9), and [(Cao et al., 2020-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/645054d31fa26b29bbfb0cf73b75f8906c359415). The Gaussian pTE estimator has methodological support from [(Silini, and Masoller, 2021-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c06602553082b3eecab4e85220eef3454bfb54cd) and [(Lizier, 2014-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/70b45b61d38bb3d44794b57fdd1dbf9d1212b18b). The patchified temporal embedding is supported by patch-based forecasting results in [(Liu et al., 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b8e57155bbcc1ce8a112482c85b3a3bb25f3fe52), [(Cheng et al., 2024-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ac7b15e0b7fc9c51624bfd1a2f5926059a7e0ed8), and [(Hong et al., 2024-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ca175146dfa9293e72c5d6fbb902be8565960d9e). The Sequence Enhancer is supported by patch-level attention that shares cross-patch, cross-variate, and cross-time information in [(Liu et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9a1f121e4fc3b2ade9237ded9a4d91822e09714d), auto-regressive and channel attention evidence in [(Yu et al., 2024-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/52a4c435945dd38a430de2904fe9e38e3c1429f6) and [(Duong-Trung et al., 2022-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a4549a05610f799246c5c1cb2510e84b121c9763), and variate-attention evidence in [(Liu et al., 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/afeeb8f5018eebb1a1d334b94dbbfc48d167efef). The drop-in attention-replacement framing is supported by mixer and frequency-domain attention replacements in [(Li et al., 2023-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b4d4f7cc5b270073061a3cf219c62881f55c6351), [(Zhou et al., 2022-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/563bac1c5cdd5096e9dbf8d4f3d5b3c4f7284e06), and [(Ilbert et al., 2024-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c8f5f6d981d96ebc84ba0a2665f00be3d1cfc27e). The H=96, F=96/192/336/720 MSE/MAE protocol and baseline ecosystem are strongly supported by [(Wang et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/425182f5c96c1d239da5cbe3a24371b2bab6319b), [(Zhang et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/180860f60e6655bebc9757b695b4fbc18a0c39ee), [(Hong et al., 2024-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ca175146dfa9293e72c5d6fbb902be8565960d9e), and [(Wang et al., 2024-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/db5cb5354d8c1ff9cccf2a1c9ef5c218c9323cc1).

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions are that non-causal models can already match or beat causal claims: correlation spanning-tree ContextRNN [(Sriramulu et al., 2024-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cd3a5091d16b3c568041c3d40f0a8e254a91f553), learned latent correlation StemGNN [(Cao et al., 2020-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/645054d31fa26b29bbfb0cf73b75f8906c359415), non-attention MTS-Mixers [(Li et al., 2023-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b4d4f7cc5b270073061a3cf219c62881f55c6351), patch-attention UniTST [(Liu et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9a1f121e4fc3b2ade9237ded9a4d91822e09714d), and kNN graph TimeFilter [(Hu et al., 2025-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4a441f7bb94e09559b90a7999a8ee9ba299f3f78) all challenge the necessity of explicit TE causality. TE/pTE reliability is also challenged: unconditioned TE can infer spurious indirect edges and false positives increase with spectral radius in [(Sun et al., 2014-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c71578f0b21109a12d4d0aba5c351218b21fcac4) and [(Sun, and Bollt, 2014-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6fcaba22c15e0df6b14acb557f8f7c6117a63a95), and standard TE can be unreliable in heterogeneous linear systems and EEG in [(Tanizawa, and Nakamura, 2022-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/93ed71aa2a409526e0c4ecd611ebf17b2a7b54ca). The fast-pTE complexity claim is not directly supported by Gaussian TE complexity evidence in [(Lizier, 2014-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/70b45b61d38bb3d44794b57fdd1dbf9d1212b18b) or TE graph complexity in [(Febrinanto et al., 2023-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/374376e2184f669322f842166a729f3bf54a22c7), and whole-series TE may smooth short-segment fluctuations in [(Wang et al., 2020-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/87e77ac0c054abddfef9cd8e579f10c8559217c9). The normal-distribution pTE assumption is risky for non-Gaussian or volatile series, as suggested by [(Chen et al., 2017-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4e2bd6be9c1e0486515a123336dc2fc857d48e7e) and [(Feng et al., 2022-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8f77ce56d4fc5500d43eaf215d17e816253a7cc6). Evaluation weaknesses include MSE/MAE without distributional diagnostics in [(Zhou et al., 2022-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/563bac1c5cdd5096e9dbf8d4f3d5b3c4f7284e06), fixed H=96 and financial-horizon ambiguity in [(Arabi et al., 2024-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8bf19ea01b4590278851956ab4349c08709fb373) and [(Hong et al., 2024-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ca175146dfa9293e72c5d6fbb902be8565960d9e), and a baseline set that may omit stronger recent models such as TimeXer, GridTST, and ForecastGrapher in [(Wang et al., 2024-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/db5cb5354d8c1ff9cccf2a1c9ef5c218c9323cc1), [(Cheng et al., 2024-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ef564011ceac0666afdc0001cb58aae8437000f0), and [(Cai et al., 2024-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/60560da7e2483d4f788c228ebd5e226ae1f40002).

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Harden the Cross-TE module before claiming causal superiority: condition pTE on intermediate variables or use parent-set/causation-entropy selection, add permutation/bootstrap significance and FDR, compare fast-pTE with exact TE, KSG TE, Granger causality, and conventional attention on synthetic Gaussian linear networks and real datasets, and prove/benchmark the claimed O(d^2T) to O(dT) reduction with runtime, memory, and numerical equivalence on the RTX 4090, addressing concerns in [(Sun et al., 2014-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c71578f0b21109a12d4d0aba5c351218b21fcac4), [(Sun, and Bollt, 2014-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6fcaba22c15e0df6b14acb557f8f7c6117a63a95), [(Lizier, 2014-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/70b45b61d38bb3d44794b57fdd1dbf9d1212b18b), [(Chen et al., 2017-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4e2bd6be9c1e0486515a123336dc2fc857d48e7e), and [(Duan et al., 2020-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4fb598f7669a30ae783344de6bde02f14ba91117).

Add direct causal-graph and strong non-causal baselines and test the drop-in claim under the identical protocol: include CauGNN/TEGNN, ADLGNN, MTGNN, StemGNN, ContextRNN, TimeXer, UniTST, TimeFilter, and ForecastGrapher where feasible, alongside DLinear, TiDE, RLinear, iTransformer, PatchTST, Crossformer, Stationary, and TimesNet; validate SPACE as a replacement for self-attention, cross-attention, or channel-wise attention in at least three backbones; report multiple seeds, mean/std or CIs, paired significance tests, RSE/RAE/CORR, ADF/KS diagnostics, and full splits/normalization/hyperparameters, following [(Duan et al., 2020-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4fb598f7669a30ae783344de6bde02f14ba91117), [(Wang et al., 2020-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/87e77ac0c054abddfef9cd8e579f10c8559217c9), [(Sriramulu et al., 2023-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/35c970fc96b86f719a303b1e02d63c52ec67b3ca), [(Cao et al., 2020-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/645054d31fa26b29bbfb0cf73b75f8906c359415), [(Sriramulu et al., 2024-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cd3a5091d16b3c568041c3d40f0a8e254a91f553), [(Wang et al., 2024-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/db5cb5354d8c1ff9cccf2a1c9ef5c218c9323cc1), [(Liu et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9a1f121e4fc3b2ade9237ded9a4d91822e09714d), [(Hu et al., 2025-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4a441f7bb94e09559b90a7999a8ee9ba299f3f78), [(Li et al., 2023-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b4d4f7cc5b270073061a3cf219c62881f55c6351), [(Zhang et al., 2023-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/017bc4b451726ea90d132a20452b99e708d9c510), [(Liu et al., 2022-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8064d3873c646dc9ff949d72c54c634a906fc092), and [(Zhou et al., 2022-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/563bac1c5cdd5096e9dbf8d4f3d5b3c4f7284e06).

Make ablations and interpretability quantitative: run parameter-matched variants that remove the Sequence Enhancer, patchified embedding, CGNN/2DMixer, or replace pTE with attention, random, correlation, or learned dynamic adjacency; test H=96 versus H=336 and financial F=48/96/192/336; for Weather define an expert-validated edge set and report edge precision, recall, F1, AUROC, permutation p-values, FDR, and attention-weight comparison rather than only visual inspection, motivated by [(Liu et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9a1f121e4fc3b2ade9237ded9a4d91822e09714d), [(Cheng et al., 2024-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ac7b15e0b7fc9c51624bfd1a2f5926059a7e0ed8), [(Hong et al., 2024-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ca175146dfa9293e72c5d6fbb902be8565960d9e), [(Wang et al., 2020-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/87e77ac0c054abddfef9cd8e579f10c8559217c9), [(Wu et al., 2020-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/75e924bd79d27a23f3f93d9b1ab62a779505c8d2), [(Cai et al., 2024-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/60560da7e2483d4f788c228ebd5e226ae1f40002), [(Febrinanto et al., 2023-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/374376e2184f669322f842166a729f3bf54a22c7), and [(Runge, 2018-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0e6d57e635ebf616ae8f039aa446dfe297f6f1a5).
