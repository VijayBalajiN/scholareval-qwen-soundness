## Strengths 
The strongest support is for using an E. coli alkB deletion background with alkylating-agent challenge to test ALKB-family repair interactions [(Zhao et al., 2021-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/901accb975508ad5b4d1f5e2638fd6e158541ab8), for colony-based DNA-damage survival assays with MMS and defined genetic controls [(Chang et al., 2019-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/636ea3aae485ff7ed512c4cc0c042a9fa07751a0), for LC-MS quantification of 5mC in extracted DNA [(Catania et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8a2d762c80b1ffec121b73ab3b97935eebd7523d), for methylation-sensitive restriction digestion as site-specific validation [(Yamada et al., 2004-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4c3ab39f8450fb083b49b223dd8c8a763f944e00), for genome-wide 5mC mapping by sequencing [(Schotanus et al., 2021-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3e5f7ef8a0964b6bf3dc40d1aa344fa9a1c00869) and [(Schotanus et al., 2020-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/852826090a7b566dd02937d3a1dd314ed1c91f21), for eukaryotic TET expression in a bacterial or phage context with mass-spectrometry readouts [(Pozhydaieva et al., 2024-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a81beb6556cc55566a2ba6653019d6f63dfa5b59), and for E. coli oxidative-stress survival and gene-expression assays involving oxyR and ycgZ-ymgABC [(Park et al., 2017-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/51f7ff5b9d2a2375937a5292b3d41df028295b11).

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions are that methylation-sensitive restriction digestion is not established as genome-wide 5mC quantification because it is locus-specific and qualitative or semi-quantitative [(Yamada et al., 2004-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4c3ab39f8450fb083b49b223dd8c8a763f944e00), while genome-wide 5mC assessment in related work uses Nanopore or bisulfite sequencing rather than restriction digest plus mass spectrometry [(Schotanus et al., 2021-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3e5f7ef8a0964b6bf3dc40d1aa344fa9a1c00869) and [(Schotanus et al., 2020-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/852826090a7b566dd02937d3a1dd314ed1c91f21). The TET co-expression support does not test DNMT-induced 5mC oxidation-product toxicity in E. coli, lacks quantitative survival or mass-spectrometry benchmarks, and is phage-centric rather than host-genome-centric [(Pozhydaieva et al., 2024-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a81beb6556cc55566a2ba6653019d6f63dfa5b59). The transcriptomic support is targeted qRT-PCR and Western blot rather than RNA-seq, and an oxyR-only focus may miss ycgZ-ymgABC and oxyR-independent oxidative-stress pathways [(Park et al., 2017-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/51f7ff5b9d2a2375937a5292b3d41df028295b11). The survival-assay support lacks exact quantitative protocols, replicates, statistical models, and lesion-tolerance or mutation-rate readouts needed to distinguish true survival defects from altered mutagenesis [(Chang et al., 2019-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/636ea3aae485ff7ed512c4cc0c042a9fa07751a0).

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Operationalize the alkB experiment as a controlled factorial survival assay in C2523 with wild-type, vector, DNMT, alkB deletion, and DNMT plus alkB deletion strains, MMS dose-response, CFU percentage survival, independent biological clones, technical replicates, predefined two-way or three-way ANOVA, and ALKBH2 or ALKBH6 complementation controls as supported by [(Zhao et al., 2021-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/901accb975508ad5b4d1f5e2638fd6e158541ab8); add lesion-tolerance and mutation-rate readouts such as rifampicin-resistance colony counts, SOS monitoring, and DNA-content measurements to distinguish survival from mutagenesis as supported by [(Chang et al., 2019-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/636ea3aae485ff7ed512c4cc0c042a9fa07751a0).

Decouple methylation quantification by using LC-MS/MS nucleoside quantification for total 5mC, 5hmC, and 5fC with isotope-labeled internal standards, MRM transitions, linearity, recovery, limit of detection, biological and technical replicates, and statistical comparisons among empty vector, M.SssI, M.MpeI, arabinose dose, and time points, as supported by [(Catania et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8a2d762c80b1ffec121b73ab3b97935eebd7523d); use methylation-sensitive restriction digestion only as site-specific confirmation of DNMT activity, not as primary genome-wide quantification, consistent with [(Yamada et al., 2004-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4c3ab39f8450fb083b49b223dd8c8a763f944e00); add orthogonal Nanopore direct 5mC calling or whole-genome bisulfite sequencing to map genome-wide methylation, as supported by [(Schotanus et al., 2021-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3e5f7ef8a0964b6bf3dc40d1aa344fa9a1c00869) and [(Schotanus et al., 2020-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/852826090a7b566dd02937d3a1dd314ed1c91f21).

Strengthen TET co-expression and transcriptomic validation by first quantifying 5mC, 5hmC, and 5fC with LC-MS/MS and, where feasible, Nanopore sequencing, then comparing no DNMT/no TET, DNMT only, DNMT plus NgTET, DNMT plus TET43, and DNMT plus both TETs after MMS, H2O2, and cisplatin with inactive TET controls, arabinose titration, ROS sensors, and predefined effect-size thresholds, as supported by [(Pozhydaieva et al., 2024-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a81beb6556cc55566a2ba6653019d6f63dfa5b59); broaden RNA-seq beyond oxyR-regulated genes to include ycgZ-ymgABC and oxyR-independent oxidative-stress pathways, include an oxyR deletion or controlled condition, validate hits by qRT-PCR and Western blot, and integrate transcriptomic results with CFU survival, ROS, and mass-spectrometry measurements, as supported by [(Park et al., 2017-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/51f7ff5b9d2a2375937a5292b3d41df028295b11).
