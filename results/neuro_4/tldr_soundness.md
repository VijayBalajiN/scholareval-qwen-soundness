## Strengths 
The strongest literature support is for a quantitative mouse novel object recognition paradigm with discrimination indices and dentate gyrus engram reactivation readouts, because [(Nomoto et al., 2016-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1d5530db8f36fd580af3f270df37d058aab2bab0) and [(Karaca et al., 2020-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9f836d570f65f2c10bab20475a9346728eaafc9d) provide NOR/engram templates, and [(Murthy et al., 2023-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/da036951b18fbe5c0a7c18f2957d107e2f0413d4) validates cFos/tTA/TRE activity-dependent labeling. Optogenetic necessity and sufficiency tests are also well supported by [(Roy et al., 2022-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cc963736961f46f3aaea6a92db2c582c1ad24448) and [(Guskjolen, and Cembrowski, 2023-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1520337fe58e3073719959a7335ccc3cfeaae5cc), while enrichment, DG object memory, Rac1, spine morphometry, and computational engram selectivity receive relevant support from [(Leger et al., 2012-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cfcd24585a098ff44e325ffaddf2f35ae418aa23), [(Jiang et al., 2023-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c2d52fb80d6c9b8e1d55b189868d97a9a4e6f8d0), [(Wang et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7dd2feca6a9015add0169a875eb667b6b075d8ca), [(Medina et al., 2023-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ed971fba573ae431d649aacd7dcec442ef2a0bb8), and [(Tomé et al., 2022-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c6bf46daf6f943ef16e0678f4c0fceacb1b9c11d).

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions concern the reliability and specificity of the proposed c-fos-tTA/AAV9-TRE and eYFP/c-Fos colocalization pipeline, because [(Murthy et al., 2023-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/da036951b18fbe5c0a7c18f2957d107e2f0413d4) reports weaker cFos/TRE labeling than ArcCreER T2, and [(Karaca et al., 2020-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9f836d570f65f2c10bab20475a9346728eaafc9d) and [(Lee et al., 2022-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2f25b51973ad794190f6db027c45a79f3cddad16) do not validate eYFP/c-Fos colocalization in dentate gyrus object memory across the proposed forgetting timeline. Post-forgetting ChR2 sufficiency is not directly established by [(Roy et al., 2022-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cc963736961f46f3aaea6a92db2c582c1ad24448), context-only engram inhibition is conceptually risky because [(Poll et al., 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7146d4db450005e9be70c21b1410ae52f349c080) did not test that manipulation and suggests engram-like activity may be sparse or memory-independent, and Rac1 pharmacology is not validated in this paradigm by [(Wang et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7dd2feca6a9015add0169a875eb667b6b075d8ca), [(Lv et al., 2019-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/98d1379b79445285e251c1b57012e3a111e86728), and [(Wu et al., 2019-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3ba9174d81d903f1f69fc067d70808dd8150af29). The computational model also lacks direct support for an error-driven engram-relevancy mechanism from [(Tomé et al., 2022-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c6bf46daf6f943ef16e0678f4c0fceacb1b9c11d).

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Run a dentate gyrus-specific pilot to validate c-fos-tTA/AAV9-TRE labeling and eYFP/c-Fos colocalization before the main forgetting experiments, using doxycycline timing, viral titer, no-dox/no-virus/non-engram controls, confocal z-stack imaging, and explicit metrics such as reactivation rate, Jaccard overlap, and specificity, as recommended by [(Murthy et al., 2023-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/da036951b18fbe5c0a7c18f2957d107e2f0413d4), [(Karaca et al., 2020-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9f836d570f65f2c10bab20475a9346728eaafc9d), and [(Lee et al., 2022-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2f25b51973ad794190f6db027c45a79f3cddad16).

Make the object-recognition timeline and optogenetic tests pre-registered, powered, and control-rich: report discrimination index, total exploration, habituation, object preference, randomization, blinding, and mixed-effects analyses at 24 h, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, and 3 weeks; test ArchT necessity at 24 h and ChR2 sufficiency after forgetting with non-engram, light-only, and pre-forgetting controls, following [(Nomoto et al., 2016-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1d5530db8f36fd580af3f270df37d058aab2bab0), [(Karaca et al., 2020-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9f836d570f65f2c10bab20475a9346728eaafc9d), and [(Roy et al., 2022-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cc963736961f46f3aaea6a92db2c582c1ad24448).

Reframe the computational model as an explicit error-driven engram-relevancy model with a prediction-error term, inhibitory plasticity, and competing engrams, then derive testable predictions for context-only exposure, reminder recovery, enrichment, and Rac1 modulation, and fit maximum-likelihood parameters to discrimination indices and c-Fos/eYFP colocalization while comparing null models, as suggested by [(Tomé et al., 2022-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c6bf46daf6f943ef16e0678f4c0fceacb1b9c11d) and [(Poll et al., 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7146d4db450005e9be70c21b1410ae52f349c080).
