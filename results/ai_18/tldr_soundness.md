## Strengths 
The methods with the strongest support are a reusable 3D embodied benchmark with a simulator, agent interface, task sampling, and objective metrics. R2R validates instruction-based navigation, seen/unseen splits, human/random baselines, and SR/SPL/NE [(Anderson et al., 2017-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c37c23b12e00168833eccff8025a830ce27c5abc) [(Gupta et al., 2017-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5cbdcf09754b9b96694f88c8fb3fbebc2d338fc2); EQA validates grounded QA and localization thresholds [(Ma et al., 2022-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c1a4fb211cf995b1af1247a152fcc145594ec13b); Habitat validates Gym-style APIs and sim-real checks [(Xia et al., 2018-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e89a4fe6e8286eccedd702216153f0f248adb151); and standardized baselines are supported by [(Bellemare et al., 2012-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f82e4ff4f003581330338aaae71f60316e58dd26). Urban LLM/VLM benchmarking is supported by CityBench and CityGPT [(Feng et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1d0cc75418eb129922f46c5201b76749d3b30231) [(Xu et al., 2023-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/34ad751c1e797975452c655e3912313044d1dd17) and M3DBench [(Li et al., 2023-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c2f1a101eb1c54cab321c47ce4f3b4b416f4786a), while AirSim-based sensors and control are supported by [(Shah et al., 2017-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a20f3dfc9142b48b924e68ee22ba259a0d621bb2) and [(Guerra et al., 2019-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1d7bdedb161a6bafb7eee3432db76565e5d1eb78).

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions are that current MLLMs are weak on grounded spatial QA and planning, and n-gram metrics can reward fluent but spatially wrong answers, so BLEU, ROUGE, METEOR, CIDEr, and Sentence-BERT alone are insufficient [(Ma et al., 2022-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c1a4fb211cf995b1af1247a152fcc145594ec13b) [(Zhang et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/33539cd2a03a1ebcc9625ba10ecdc6e7e3b4c42f) [(Fu et al., 2024-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/09ff7cdc583b28ec63dfc85c7142f2cb0e5e4eef) [(Ying et al., 2024-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/10d3c1cfb98f4e547f579a30b31f2361297983f1) [(Anderson et al., 2016-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f90d9c5615f4a0e3f9a1ce2a0075269b9bab6b5f). A single manually built Beijing district and manually annotated optimal trajectories may not generalize or remain valid under dynamic traffic, sensor noise, and sim-real gaps, requiring unseen splits, traffic safety metrics, and sim2real validation [(Dosovitskiy et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ebf0615fc4d98cf1dbe527c79146ce1e50dce9af) [(Kadian et al., 2019-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8f1f19bfddf103e4e3ac74610f84d944496406b3) [(Xia et al., 2018-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e89a4fe6e8286eccedd702216153f0f248adb151) [(Wang et al., 2023-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/55d184e7bcf467f2824cd9603194e523b206f8e7) [(Serrano et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/91409f2bc5102535318efce84143f6f1f6b73943) [(Vasudevan et al., 2019-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/490a7473715e9136de8a62fee16704eb4631b69b) [(Jain et al., 2019-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/68ccecb380ecfc0a4b294b84e3d0b6ff6884c4df). Manual Blender modeling of 200 buildings and 6,000 elements lacks quantitative realism validation and may be less scalable than OSM, street-view, CAD, or generative pipelines [(Shang et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/65145427d8dad66577b61225e169f8003819257f) [(Deng et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4b538c3e66f1456f5c7b4f7cb34523905d297a43) [(Buyukdemircioglu, and Elberink, 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cf68a6229c21760e2ecd4abb9d278ae2b40637bb) [(Lin et al., 2023-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/feef7e0835d93b17faa04461917888e304efe224) [(Xie et al., 2023-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ddc64a5ce90b7b97e9bcad122682f289ac854ae). AirSim and an HTTP proxy are not quantitatively validated for the full city-scale multi-sensor suite [(Shah et al., 2017-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a20f3dfc9142b48b924e68ee22ba259a0d621bb2) [(Song et al., 2020-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/34b144320e73a4e0c7789df2f6f5b90d726c76a9) [(Guerra et al., 2019-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1d7bdedb161a6bafb7eee3432db76565e5d1eb78).

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Formalize a reproducible evaluation protocol before model testing: define geographic seen/unseen splits by street, building, or district; task-complexity tiers; maximum steps; success radii; repeated trials; and baselines including random, shortest-path, map-only, oracle, human, and MLLM with/without tools. Report task-specific metrics with confidence intervals and significance tests, and augment BLEU, ROUGE, METEOR, CIDEr, and Sentence-BERT with grounded correctness metrics such as exact match, multiple-choice accuracy, Acc@0.5m, Acc@1.0m, Acc@15 degrees, Acc@30 degrees, counting MAE, plan success, OSR, collision rate, and time-to-collision [(Bellemare et al., 2012-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f82e4ff4f003581330338aaae71f60316e58dd26) [(Anderson et al., 2017-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c37c23b12e00168833eccff8025a830ce27c5abc) [(Ma et al., 2022-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c1a4fb211cf995b1af1247a152fcc145594ec13b) [(Zhang et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/33539cd2a03a1ebcc9625ba10ecdc6e7e3b4c42f) [(Lee et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/103a79f7413e4b13eb54ca82c817d8aec491d72e) [(Gao, and Huang, 2022-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6cb7fd11450540ca82efcb8cda3029cbe9669f95).

Add quantitative validation of the UE5.3, AirSim, and Mirage platform: benchmark FPS, latency, memory, and concurrent-agent throughput; validate GPS, IMU, depth, segmentation, and LiDAR against ground truth or real sensors with error CDFs, latency, update-rate jitter, and statistical tests; validate Mirage traffic with density, lane/crossing compliance, collision rate, and time-to-collision; and compare rendered views to Baidu or Amap street view using FID, KID, depth error, camera error, and a small real-world or SRCC check [(Shah et al., 2017-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a20f3dfc9142b48b924e68ee22ba259a0d621bb2) [(Guerra et al., 2019-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1d7bdedb161a6bafb7eee3432db76565e5d1eb78) [(Serrano et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/91409f2bc5102535318efce84143f6f1f6b73943) [(Kadian et al., 2019-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8f1f19bfddf103e4e3ac74610f84d944496406b3) [(Xia et al., 2018-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e89a4fe6e8286eccedd702216153f0f248adb151) [(Wang et al., 2023-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/55d184e7bcf467f2824cd9603194e523b206f8e7) [(Shang et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/65145427d8dad66577b61225e169f8003819257f).

Convert manual asset and trajectory construction into a hybrid, metadata-grounded pipeline: use OSM, CityJSON, CAD, and street-view data to auto-layout and texture buildings and the 6,000 urban elements, with manual refinement reserved for task-critical assets; build a machine-readable scene graph or PDDL metadata layer with object states, reachability, and traffic constraints; generate QA, dialogue, navigation, and planning candidates programmatically and filter them before human QC with inter-annotator agreement; and for navigation, generate candidate routes with A* or OSRM, then human-validate safety and naturalness under dynamic traffic while allowing multiple valid trajectories [(Xie et al., 2023-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ddc64a5ce90b7b97e9bcad122682f289ac854ae) [(Lin et al., 2023-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/feef7e0835d93b17faa04461917888e304efe224) [(Shang et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/65145427d8dad66577b61225e169f8003819257f) [(Li et al., 2024-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4bf1d4946ec440a00f2521f861d14a29e81712a5) [(Vasudevan et al., 2019-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/490a7473715e9136de8a62fee16704eb4631b69b) [(Chen et al., 2018-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f75f0750a00f6f85107985c70eca9c275b5e0962) [(Rawal et al., 2024-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/20b65e39d681d901e956a8116353e224ebd4c0fb).
