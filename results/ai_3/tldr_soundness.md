## Strengths 
The strongest support is for layer-wise, function-preserving norm rescaling and initial-scale anchoring as mechanisms for preserving learned behavior while controlling weight growth. [(Stock et al., 2019-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8dd29bd8bd4197a97d28614f3c13b18fc6c9e2b5) directly supports the core SWR premise by showing that p=2 Equi-normalization can preserve the implemented function and be applied after SGD, validating layer-wise proportional rescaling. [(Li, and Arora, 2019-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/00521e876d109fdcf344e027f218a768499060c8) supports the need to treat BatchNorm affine parameters and the final classifier separately, because scale invariance can be maintained by freezing those components. [(Kumar et al., 2023-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/39be2eab576bbe918da77e56f8e8771a0b1afb8c) supports anchoring to initial weight scale for plasticity preservation, while [(Lee et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6e69e24316ca5a7db9725302da477c07214229e7) and [(Lee, and Storkey, 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4f15766d05d090bd1987cb8e92db5bd92c37e4c9) support the proposed warm-start and 10-chunk continual-learning evaluation. [(Li et al., 2021-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1ff80b0786c4286c7477560339f97387f69bcfe6) supports the 200-epoch single-task protocol, and [(Huang et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d80d5862f5406a46225e91a71bb046eda451c430) supports weight-norm control as a practical image-classification regularizer.

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions concern exact functional preservation, normalization-layer interactions, optimizer-state effects, and the proposed scaling formula. [(Ash, and Adams, 2019-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/538dbb608312b0a107cf98ccbaca2e0f563e2a61) shows that shrink-and-perturb and last-layer reset do not fully recover random-initialized performance in warm-start settings, challenging the assumption that soft rescaling alone recovers plasticity. [(Kumar et al., 2023-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/39be2eab576bbe918da77e56f8e8771a0b1afb8c) shows that L2 Init can outperform shrink-based methods, suggesting norm-only scaling may be weaker than full initial-weight anchoring. [(Li, and Arora, 2019-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/00521e876d109fdcf344e027f218a768499060c8) and [(Cai et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/253eba43d7e734ee6c5b00dca29be3c6c589f4a5) show that BatchNorm affine parameters and epsilon make scaling approximate and can cause instability or overflow, so focusing only on the final normalization layer is insufficient. [(Stock et al., 2019-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8dd29bd8bd4197a97d28614f3c13b18fc6c9e2b5) indicates that rescaling creates parameter-space jumps and requires optimizer-state correction. The proposed c_l formula can exceed 1 when the current norm is below the initial norm, contradicting a pure downscaling or bounded-growth claim, as highlighted by the need for bounded rescaling in [(Stock et al., 2019-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8dd29bd8bd4197a97d28614f3c13b18fc6c9e2b5) and EMA-based scale control in [(Chiley et al., 2019-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b9aecad9e5dd0ed2023e9f82b3d7fee935ef3536). [(Zhou et al., 2019-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/bfb9ea4f70aef592c59817d70f69b832e605c773) also warns that aggressive norm constraints can underuse capacity or destabilize training.

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Replace the static lambda formula with a bounded EMA scaling rule and correct optimizer state. Maintain m_l = beta m_{l-1} + (1-beta) ||W_init_l|| / ||W_l|| and use c_l = min(1, m_l) for downscaling, or prove a two-sided invariant; rescale SGD momentum or Adam moments with the same c_l; compare per-step, periodic, and ENorm-style rescaling. This addresses the formula's ability to upscale and the parameter-space jump identified in [(Stock et al., 2019-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8dd29bd8bd4197a97d28614f3c13b18fc6c9e2b5), [(Chiley et al., 2019-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b9aecad9e5dd0ed2023e9f82b3d7fee935ef3536), and [(Gupta, and Asthana, 2023-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8c2fbee1ad62cc42f9aa50a488824c78602e87f).

Derive and empirically validate normalization-aware proportionality rules for all BatchNorm affine parameters, not only the final layer. Either freeze or co-rescale all BatchNorm gamma and beta parameters and the final classifier as in [(Li, and Arora, 2019-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/00521e876d109fdcf344e027f218a768499060c8), or derive closed-form updates consistent with [(Ba et al., 2016-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/97fb4e3d45bb098e27e0071448b6152217bd35a5) and [(Cai et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/253eba43d7e734ee6c5b00dca29be3c6c589f4a5); run a fixed-input invariance test on MLP, CNN-BN, and VGG-16, sweep BatchNorm epsilon and separate BatchNorm learning rates, and monitor gamma/beta, pre-activation mean and variance, and first-epoch loss as in [(Sun et al., 2020-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4ab1b2261579c4d3fdef95b70bf8a4d7483e674c).

Make the evaluation a controlled plasticity-recovery benchmark with explicit upper and lower bounds. Add fresh random initialization and pure warm-start baselines, report fixed-budget and convergence or wall-clock results, and include continual-learning metrics A_T and F_T, per-chunk accuracy, forgetting, backward transfer, and weight-norm trajectories as in [(Kim et al., 2023-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/163b61ea53685ab9ec60c6818bd4f289e0060dac) and [(Lee, and Storkey, 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4f15766d05d090bd1987cb8e92db5bd92c37e4c9); compare against L2, L2 Init, S&P, head reset, replay or exemplar baselines, and scheduler variants, with seeds, confidence intervals, and overhead reporting as motivated by [(Ash, and Adams, 2019-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/538dbb608312b0a107cf98ccbaca2e0f563e2a61), [(Lee et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6e69e24316ca5a7db9725302da477c07214229e7), and [(Li et al., 2021-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1ff80b0786c4286c7477560339f97387f69bcfe6).
