## Strengths 
The proposal is best supported for its acute-stage tachyzoite screen and general phenotypic/metabolomic logic. Human myogenic cells can be differentiated into multinucleated myotubes, supporting the proposed KD3 myotube host system [(Shiomi et al., 2011-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1ac41625d5270c6cda76074bb1ed591e0a872ac2). The MMV Pathogen Box, DMSO control, and fluorescence-based tachyzoite viability readout are feasible because the same 400-compound box was screened in T. gondii with DMSO controls and mitochondrial/ETC-relevant hits were identified [(Hayward et al., 2022-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/91528e3922fc8dcbae597add91ec748fcfb9f60f). tdTomato-expressing tachyzoites in fibroblasts are supported for acute infection and fluorescence monitoring [(Sheiner et al., 2011-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/509405c603028d4e987f340a9f9891ef3e1e6803). Dose-response validation and host cytotoxicity controls are conceptually supported by prior anti-Toxoplasma screening workflows [(Murata et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/837e410f668638b1ac773178019675813e8082c9). Untargeted LC-MS metabolomics and stable isotope-resolved labeling are feasible, though require careful data processing [(Scalbert et al., 2009-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/729cb2d75bd5526f87313c16b909f5baf06fc69f) [(Parker et al., 2018-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/32129556d4eb50b84223917610a757bfb6ca5107). Mitochondrial membrane potential, TEM, and ATP-related readouts have precedent in T. gondii, and atovaquone/bc1 target validation provides a useful positive-control framework [(Liu et al., 2024-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e38e281e76211e80b7675e9c9eed8dd5bcbd0f9e) [(Hargrave et al., 2019-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c76a6e0f555cac881976a4e2947527c5ebdf72b1) [(Lavine, and Arrizabalaga, 2012-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2b3f6768b907150c1cb212bca19d193f75319ece) [(Mühleip et al., 2021-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f6de8cf608383d37be2edf1ca26f5396abf9a469) [(Souza et al., 2024-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f1939ffaa5cabdc13e6e11e10b46118f2d8e3eca) [(Maclean et al., 2021-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3a60e8a80ac027f3facc33dc97725c12718e6943).

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradiction is that the central bradyzoite model is not directly validated: no provided source confirms that KD3 myotubes support T. gondii infection, bicarbonate-depleted bradyzoite conversion, four-week cyst maturation, or the proposed 28-day inhibitor-washout regrowth endpoint [(Shiomi et al., 2011-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1ac41625d5270c6cda76074bb1ed591e0a872ac2) [(Hayward et al., 2022-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/91528e3922fc8dcbae597add91ec748fcfb9f60f) [(Murata et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/837e410f668638b1ac773178019675813e8082c9). The tachyzoite screen is supported, but the closest Pathogen Box study used OCR and proliferation readouts rather than the proposed 10 uM, 7-day tdTomato endpoint, so hit ranking may differ [(Hayward et al., 2022-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/91528e3922fc8dcbae597add91ec748fcfb9f60f). The metabolomics plan lacks a specified extraction, normalization, QC, and statistical-validation pipeline, and prior metabolomics work warns that automated feature extraction and PLS-DA can generate artifacts or overfitting [(Scalbert et al., 2009-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/729cb2d75bd5526f87313c16b909f5baf06fc69f). Mitochondrial assays are not validated in mature bradyzoites, and MitoTracker signal in infected cultures may reflect host mitochondria rather than parasite mitochondrial membrane potential [(Hargrave et al., 2019-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c76a6e0f555cac881976a4e2947527c5ebdf72b1) [(Liu et al., 2024-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e38e281e76211e80b7675e9c9eed8dd5bcbd0f9e). Finally, atovaquone metabolite-abundance data do not clearly establish a simple abundance signature under the proposed acute 3-hour, 3-5x IC50/LC50 conditions [(Souza et al., 2024-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f1939ffaa5cabdc13e6e11e10b46118f2d8e3eca).

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Validate the KD3 myotube bradyzoite model before screening: differentiate KD3 myoblasts, infect with Pru-deltahxgprt tdTomato under bicarbonate-depleted conditions, allow four weeks for cyst maturation, and quantify cyst number, tdTomato fluorescence, host-cell viability by resazurin, and 28-day tachyzoite regrowth after DMSO or active-compound removal; include myogenic marker, karyotype, and immortalization controls to ensure the host system does not confound bradyzoite maturation [(Shiomi et al., 2011-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1ac41625d5270c6cda76074bb1ed591e0a872ac2) [(Hayward et al., 2022-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/91528e3922fc8dcbae597add91ec748fcfb9f60f).

Add a quantitative pilot and validation layer for the Pathogen Box screen: define parasite strain, host passage, MOI, DMSO concentration, fluorescence settings, and resazurin CC50 cutoff; test known active and host-toxic controls; then confirm hits by serial dilution IC50 for tachyzoites and washout-based LC50 for bradyzoites with 95% confidence intervals, selectivity indices, replicates, and statistical tests; optionally add Seahorse OCR as a parallel mitochondrial readout to capture ETC-active compounds [(Hayward et al., 2022-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/91528e3922fc8dcbae597add91ec748fcfb9f60f) [(Murata et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/837e410f668638b1ac773178019675813e8082c9) [(Sheiner et al., 2011-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/509405c603028d4e987f340a9f9891ef3e1e6803).

Build matched positive-control datasets for atovaquone and buparvaquone under the exact proposed metabolomic and mitochondrial conditions: use 3-5x IC50/LC50 for 3 hours, DMSO controls, identical parasite isolation, acetonitrile extraction, pHILIC/BEH-amide LC-MS, 13C6-glucose and 15N-amide-glutamine, targeted HPLC/UV metabolite panel, MitoTracker validation in isolated parasites, glucose-starvation ATP assay, and quantitative TEM; require concordant metabolite, isotope, and mitochondrial phenotypes before assigning bc1/ETC target engagement [(Souza et al., 2024-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f1939ffaa5cabdc13e6e11e10b46118f2d8e3eca) [(Maclean et al., 2021-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3a60e8a80ac027f3facc33dc97725c12718e6943) [(Scalbert et al., 2009-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/729cb2d75bd5526f87313c16b909f5baf06fc69f).
