## Strengths 
The proposal is best supported by prior mitoribosome-profiling studies showing that protected-fragment mapping can resolve codon-specific stalling, queuing, and initiation events [(Wakigawa et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9f1a70d1dd46868ac8229668227f0e49c6d52fa6), and that phasing, footprint length, and coverage are informative QC metrics for mitochondrial translation [(Li et al., 2022-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/940dd61674973323a0b004866a30c27061b164c0). Sucrose-gradient separation of 55S mitoribosomes from 80S cytoplasmic ribosomes is directly supported by mammalian mitoribosome-profiling workflows [(Krüger et al., 2023-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c06e68969775fca74af6f315c7b943bd81d38931), while codon-resolution occupancy analysis is supported by ribosome residence-time and motif/boundary methods [(Sun et al., 2016-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/16d6195ee1a1362145673295cba88363cdf3b608). The 5'-end footprint strategy is supported by prior MRPF size/phasing and 5' terminus analyses [(Rooijers et al., 2013-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1aa6b70f5b0b48f475cc7f253858b4e37aaf4e2f), [(Gao et al., 2017-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d035d1fca0463a49a51648ae88e633ab70f0e701), and N-terminal peptide validation is feasible using mitochondrial dimethylation-TAILS proteomics [(Lualdi et al., 2019-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7077854de1c7ed7bdf34252f68d87dffe775b967).

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions are that the cited mitoribosome-profiling evidence does not test the proposed antibiotic panel, HEK293 concentrations, 30-minute treatment, or DMSO controls, so the expected initiation versus elongation arrest patterns remain unvalidated [(Wakigawa et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9f1a70d1dd46868ac8229668227f0e49c6d52fa6). Prior QC studies do not provide quantitative thresholds for RNase I versus MNase, phasing, footprint length, or coverage, making the proposed optimization under-specified [(Li et al., 2022-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/940dd61674973323a0b004866a30c27061b164c0). Sucrose-gradient separation is not quantitatively validated under antibiotic stress, leaving possible 80S contamination or altered sedimentation unresolved [(Krüger et al., 2023-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c06e68969775fca74af6f315c7b943bd81d38931). The -1/P/A context metric lacks direct support from antibiotic-induced mitoribosome stalling and was previously applied to steady-state yeast data rather than acute HEK293 mitoribosome profiling [(Sun et al., 2016-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/16d6195ee1a1362145673295cba88363cdf3b608). Finally, mitochondrial RPFs can be poorly captured and bimodal, and cryptic initiation or protease-generated N-termini can confound start-codon and N-terminal peptide interpretation [(Rooijers et al., 2013-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1aa6b70f5b0b48f475cc7f253858b4e37aaf4e2f), [(Wakigawa et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9f1a70d1dd46868ac8229668227f0e49c6d52fa6), [(Lualdi et al., 2019-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7077854de1c7ed7bdf34252f68d87dffe775b967).

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Add a quantitative QC and statistical design before interpreting antibiotic effects: pilot the exact proposed exposures (100 µg/mL tiamulin or josamycin and 10 µg/mL retapamulin for 30 min) with at least three biological and two technical replicates for DMSO and each antibiotic, titrate RNase I and MNase, and predefine pass/fail gates for 3-nt phasing score, MRPF length mean/median/width, per-codon coverage, 5' to 3' coverage ratio, and mitochondrial versus cytoplasmic rRNA contamination, with FDR-controlled differential MRPF abundance testing and orthogonal RNA-Seq or cytoplasmic Ribo-Seq controls [(Li et al., 2022-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/940dd61674973323a0b004866a30c27061b164c0), [(Krüger et al., 2023-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c06e68969775fca74af6f315c7b943bd81d38931), [(Wakigawa et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9f1a70d1dd46868ac8229668227f0e49c6d52fa6).

Make the context-dependent stalling analysis explicit and collision-aware: normalize MRPF counts per codon or amino acid, calculate log2 fold change versus DMSO, test -1/P/A triads against P/A or -1/P windows using permutation or FDR correction, include telithromycin as a positive context control and non-context-dependent antibiotics as negative controls, and add MitoIP-Thor-Disome-Seq or an equivalent disome assay to distinguish true stalling from queuing or collision artifacts [(Wakigawa et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9f1a70d1dd46868ac8229668227f0e49c6d52fa6), [(Sun et al., 2016-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/16d6195ee1a1362145673295cba88363cdf3b608).

Require multi-evidence validation for initiation and novel translation events: accept candidate start sites only when 5' MRPF size, in-frame codon phasing, amino acid composition, and antibiotic-versus-DMSO coverage are concordant, then validate with N-terminal dimethylation-TAILS MS using protease inhibitors, exact peptide matching to predicted start-codon-derived N-termini, FDR-controlled peptide identification, and targeted PRM/MRM for top candidates [(Wakigawa et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9f1a70d1dd46868ac8229668227f0e49c6d52fa6), [(Lualdi et al., 2019-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7077854de1c7ed7bdf34252f68d87dffe775b967), [(Gao et al., 2017-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d035d1fca0463a49a51648ae88e633ab70f0e701), [(Rooijers et al., 2013-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1aa6b70f5b0b48f475cc7f253858b4e37aaf4e2f).
