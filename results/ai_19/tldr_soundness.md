## Strengths 
The methods with the strongest support are: (1) contrastive/reference activation attribution, where contrasting target activations or states with alternative references improves token-level relevance and class-specificity [(Yin, and Neubig, 2022-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1ec16018e9152c50bbaed1d49c3077b9fb6d2838), [(Eberle et al., 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b940088cc1fb62459ef1a848aa6658a67502b757), [(Wang et al., 2019-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2e257b5f0a0148076e9161202880b022e521ac59), and [(Li et al., 2023-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a33311f58b24ff14dbbb92bfcd57b3bd452b745e); (2) gradient-weighted attention and multi-layer propagation in transformers, which supports combining gradients, attention, and layer-specific signals for token attribution [(Chefer et al., 2021-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7ec5f207263100ea2d45db595712f611a74bafd9), [(Ferrando et al., 2022-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/bb1c9cb431e771660cffdda1d80a7f15ff40c764), and [(Barkan et al., 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/dac10b39e4d7b75332e0b437a5426cfa81686f29); and (3) deletion-based Most Relevant First and Least Relevant First faithfulness evaluation on BERT sentiment tasks, which directly supports the planned AOPC, LOdds, and token-deletion protocol [(Arras et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f22ac2e3e45debb13d635fddaccfff7e997e7db9), [(Samek et al., 2015-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6df11b0bb0244d4d36e8955436067cc5d19734fa), [(Vukadin et al., 2024-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/17093c3088bc678b90fc208c5641c1c7d98339c8), and [(Song et al., 2024-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/81c80ec8ad4bde6553e54a8bd6d67bd80ab5212e). Kendall-tau rank correlation is also feasible for token-ranking evaluation in transformer text classification [(Neely et al., 2021-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/884dffa4c248663a32cacc1b37cb4a5ecd4d2b65).

## Weaknesses 
The methods with the strongest contradictions are: (1) low-confidence activation contrast on binary sentiment datasets, because contrastive and non-contrastive LRP rationales were nearly identical on binary SST2 and DynaSent, suggesting limited gains from added reference activations unless the reference changes the decision boundary [(Eberle et al., 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b940088cc1fb62459ef1a848aa6658a67502b757); (2) using averaged attention as a primary relevance signal, because attention weights are often weakly aligned with blank-out importance, input gradients, and causal token effects, and can over-weight attractors or special tokens [(Serrano, and Smith, 2019-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/135112c7ba1762d65f39b1a61777f26ae4dfd8ad), [(Abnar, and Zuidema, 2020-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/76a9f336481b39515d6cea2920696f11fb686451); (3) treating reference selection and deletion-based refinement as automatically stable, because reference-based attribution can be sensitive to reference choice and input transformations, and deletion tests can be slow, noisy, or direction-dependent [(Kindermans et al., 2017-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/62e39c6dbcead1fe4d29017914591d929aa6ac4c), [(Ghorbani et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/96cf326c91adc96aa59382bf71fbead29ea34c52), [(Ancona et al., 2017-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/de2447a25012c71ad316487b4b9e7378a4fcccc0), and [(Arras et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f22ac2e3e45debb13d635fddaccfff7e997e7db9); and (4) assuming multi-layer aggregation and Kendall-tau confidence will automatically improve faithfulness or class distinctness, because single-layer or width-matched models can be competitive, layer signals can be unstable, and rank correlation can be weak or misleading without perturbation and polarity controls [(Brown et al., 2022-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/86fb2ee92569d35a8b471d814fa4c7653728536f), [(Neely et al., 2021-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/884dffa4c248663a32cacc1b37cb4a5ecd4d2b65), and [(Liu et al., 2022-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5fdb05e17fd503b8dbdbadc338e0a00829929dcc).

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Run a controlled reference-activation ablation on BERT-base for Amazon Polarity, Yelp Polarity, SST2, and IMDB: compare low-confidence target-class references against random, same-class, zero, foil-class, and no-reference baselines; sweep gamma values such as 0.1, 0.3, 0.5, and 0.7 and reference counts such as 1, 3, 5, and 10; report AOPC, LOdds under MoRF and LeRF, Kendall-tau, confidence intervals, and Wilcoxon or bootstrap significance tests; and add paraphrase or token-order invariance checks to test whether low-confidence references are stable rather than merely threshold-selected [(Eberle et al., 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b940088cc1fb62459ef1a848aa6658a67502b757), [(Kindermans et al., 2017-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/62e39c6dbcead1fe4d29017914591d929aa6ac4c), [(Li et al., 2023-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a33311f58b24ff14dbbb92bfcd57b3bd452b745e), [(Vukadin et al., 2024-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/17093c3088bc678b90fc208c5641c1c7d98339c8), and [(Song et al., 2024-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/81c80ec8ad4bde6553e54a8bd6d67bd80ab5212e).

Make the faithfulness protocol reproducible and stronger by specifying the BERT-base checkpoint, tokenizer, 512-token limit, [CLS] pooling, dataset splits, deletion or masking strategy, top-k percentages, random seeds, and hardware; compare against strong baselines including ALTI, DecompX, LRP(AH+LN), GAE, Grad-SAM, CAT, AttCAT, TIS, and random saliency; and add polarity-violation tests, comprehensiveness/sufficiency, token-F1, and rank correlations with blank-out or gradient importance so that AOPC and LOdds are not the only evidence [(Song et al., 2024-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/81c80ec8ad4bde6553e54a8bd6d67bd80ab5212e), [(Ferrando et al., 2022-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/bb1c9cb431e771660cffdda1d80a7f15ff40c764), [(Modarressi et al., 2023-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3327bdc202fb4b3673c2fa1ac18ab436adc72fef), [(Liu et al., 2022-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5fdb05e17fd503b8dbdbadc338e0a00829929dcc), and [(Vukadin et al., 2024-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/17093c3088bc678b90fc208c5641c1c7d98339c8).

Treat attention and layer aggregation as ablated components rather than assumptions: test no-attention, raw averaged attention, attention rollout/flow, and gradient-weighted attention; test single layers, bottom-half, top-half, all-layer, and layer-weighted aggregation with normalized layer scores; report per-layer diagnostics, runtime, and statistical significance; and if multi-layer aggregation yields only small gains, use selective layer weighting or a single tuned layer instead of uniform all-layer averaging [(Chefer et al., 2021-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7ec5f207263100ea2d45db595712f611a74bafd9), [(Abnar, and Zuidema, 2020-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/76a9f336481b39515d6cea2920696f11fb686451), [(Ali et al., 2022-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/577a350ade92578913245e2d474aeabcb576e6d6), [(Brown et al., 2022-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/86fb2ee92569d35a8b471d814fa4c7653728536f), and [(Wang et al., 2023-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cab8726de462497bbbdcac5e10736b9f5a5c2d8b).
