## Strengths 
The strongest support is for the tablet-based rapid right-index-finger reaching task, repeated pre/in/post phase design, and model-based kinematic analysis. [(Macaluso et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b3cfc02cac9b750070c5469e65b890259c2934ac) used the same core paradigm in microgravity and neutral buoyancy with foot straps, left-hand stabilization, and feedforward kinematic indices, directly supporting the proposed task structure and movement measures. [(Bieg et al., 2023-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f3ff0bedae6143e46d2dcaca72405a0669643591) supports repeated touchscreen target acquisition across microgravity-analog phases and shows that phase effects can be detected in movement time, throughput, error count, and touch duration. The two-joint model-based approach is supported by [(Wochner et al., 2020-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5b80bce80ea49b57dd45a526f3666bd8f8940fe5), and the use of submovement decomposition to identify corrective movements is supported by [(Elliott et al., unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6a4899d4f0ae330dcc88b69ad3d76e7658c2aa5a).

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions concern in-flight stabilization, statistical robustness, and direct validation of the proposed directional mass manipulation. [(Thonnard et al., 2020-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/576fedf707528768a0d83b8bec20672d07f495ea) indicates that pelvis restraint and shoulder straps are important for stability, whereas the proposal relies only on foot straps and left-hand tabletop grasp, risking contamination of finger kinematics by trunk or shoulder motion. [(Bieg et al., 2023-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f3ff0bedae6143e46d2dcaca72405a0669643591) also shows that repeated touchscreen performance in a microgravity analog did not produce uniform slowing, included practice-related throughput increases, elevated errors and touch duration, missing recovery data, and used mixed models rather than simple repeated-measures ANOVA. The proposed 45, 90, and 135 degree 12 cm targets, 650 ms deadline, 100 Hz tracking, and interpretation of submovements as evidence of mass underestimation are not directly validated by the cited work, and [(Elliott et al., unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6a4899d4f0ae330dcc88b69ad3d76e7658c2aa5a) shows that corrective submovements can arise from many sources, not only insufficient feedforward force.

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Add an in-flight stabilization and geometry validation protocol: use pelvis or torso restraint and shoulder straps where feasible, record trunk, shoulder, hip, and tablet motion, and use fiducial markers or camera calibration to verify tablet position, tilt, and screen-to-body geometry with predefined acceptance thresholds; exclude or covary trials with excessive body motion or placement error, following [(Macaluso et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b3cfc02cac9b750070c5469e65b890259c2934ac), [(Thonnard et al., 2020-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/576fedf707528768a0d83b8bec20672d07f495ea), and [(Bieg et al., 2023-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f3ff0bedae6143e46d2dcaca72405a0669643591).

Replace or supplement repeated-measures ANOVA with a pre-registered mixed-model analysis: use linear mixed-effects models for continuous kinematics with random intercepts for participant, session, block, and target or direction, a zero-inflated Poisson or negative-binomial model for error counts, interquartile-range outlier removal, multiple-comparison correction, and a power analysis for 12 taikonauts, 3 directions, and 3 phases; add throughput, error rate, touch duration, and success rate as secondary metrics, following [(Bieg et al., 2023-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f3ff0bedae6143e46d2dcaca72405a0669643591) and [(Koppelmans et al., 2017-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0b8f60cb14f6bd8139a2d02dcf8c7110f53941d3).

Run a ground pilot to validate the directional task and model pipeline before flight: confirm that the 45, 90, and 135 degree 12 cm targets produce meaningfully different effective masses, calibrate the 100 Hz tracking, smoothing, peak acceleration and speed timing, and submovement thresholds, and test the two-joint forward optimal controller and movement utility predictions under actual versus misperceived mass, using [(Wochner et al., 2020-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5b80bce80ea49b57dd45a526f3666bd8f8940fe5), [(Teka et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5d2b60ab92485344ecbde5e05f62f7d3be9ce5c8), [(Liao, and Kirsch, 2014-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2a706f0680cc85cb7c47cabcd9331282eb727d3a), and [(Elliott et al., unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6a4899d4f0ae330dcc88b69ad3d76e7658c2aa5a).
