## Strengths 
The proposal's strongest supported method is a controlled, multi-metric benchmark for protein generative models. Protein-specific evidence directly supports the planned quality, diversity, and distributional-similarity suite: [(Meshchaninov et al., 2024-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9e81434435fe1cbb032006334b089c70e863732b) evaluates pLDDT, perplexity, scPerplexity, CD at 50 percent identity, FD-seq, MMD, and 1-Wasserstein distance on 2,048 generated sequences and shows that ESM-2 encoder scale changes pLDDT, FD-seq, and CD. Recent protein benchmarks also support comparing pLDDT, model-specific perplexity, pairwise TM-score, and Max Clust. across ProGen2, EvoDiff, DPLM, and ESM3, while showing large finite-sample variability at n=50 [(Ye et al., 2024-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7c6729bd4b2b3bdf013482b499c7401ab6de44ef). The synthetic perturbation and sample-size design is strongly supported by image-generation metric studies showing that FID, GAN-test, GAN-train, and sliced Wasserstein behave differently under subsampling and noise [(Shmelkov et al., 2018-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8b1a1949789d12997b0a5ade953309308a81e115), that tiny perturbations can destabilize sample-based likelihood metrics [(Theis et al., 2015-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/39e0c341351f8f4a39ac890b96217c7f4bde5369), and that FID can fluctuate across training snapshots and obscure precision-recall tradeoffs [(Kynkaanniemi et al., 2019-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d383dd8ced85d7898d8b1546c514a34fb626ea16). pLDDT is supported as a foldability signal by AlphaFold validation [(Jumper et al., 2021-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/dc32a984b651256a8ec282be52310e6bd33d9815) and large-scale ESMFold calibration [(Lin et al., 2022-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c49a0912595a1cc70aab63524f64ed08c92194a8), while MMD bandwidth analysis is supported by [(Gretton et al., 2006-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9bca4d7b932e0854c3325f1578cfd17341dd8ea8) and [(Muandet et al., 2016-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f0b9b5d6f79d62305f47ef560512bf53d17e38e2). Real-world validation is supported by DiMA/RFDiffusion and protein-LLM evaluations [(Meshchaninov et al., 2024-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9e81434435fe1cbb032006334b089c70e863732b), [(Zeinalipour et al., 2024-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/54487bb7cb4945a4445c91add90bc8f45f28c53f).

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions concern metric stability, model dependence, and the sufficiency of the planned sample sizes. Sample-based distributional metrics can be unreliable in high-dimensional spaces: 10,000-sample Parzen likelihood failed and tiny shifts changed nearest-neighbor retrieval [(Theis et al., 2015-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/39e0c341351f8f4a39ac890b96217c7f4bde5369); FID conflated quality, diversity, and subset size [(Shmelkov et al., 2018-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8b1a1949789d12997b0a5ade953309308a81e115); and FID varied by up to plus or minus 14 percent between StyleGAN snapshots [(Kynkaanniemi et al., 2019-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d383dd8ced85d7898d8b1546c514a34fb626ea16). pLDDT is not a universal quality score: ESMFold pLDDT correlates only r=0.79 with AlphaFold LDDT [(Lin et al., 2022-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c49a0912595a1cc70aab63524f64ed08c92194a8), predictor choice changes accuracy and success [(Lai, and Chen, 2023-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/88c9b1885765a9bcd4539642de1295491f84fdb4), and high-confidence designs can require physical or energetic checks [(Moffat et al., 2022-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1dc9e3d2128ca9c11cd401a884d72183beb6a846). Perplexity is model-specific and not directly comparable across architectures [(Ye et al., 2024-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7c6729bd4b2b3bdf013482b499c7401ab6de44ef), [(Hsu et al., 2022-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f9c95ea9bcd529309c1d743f0926b5137f8ed60f). CD may be coarse or saturate: CD0.5 changed only from 0.981 to 0.969 across ESM-2 scale [(Meshchaninov et al., 2024-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9e81434435fe1cbb032006334b089c70e863732b), cluster metrics saturated at n=50 [(Ye et al., 2024-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7c6729bd4b2b3bdf013482b499c7401ab6de44ef), and structural diversity can diverge from sequence diversity [(Wang et al., 2024-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9ec09a9534fe41d3a5231693050150e0bbec8515). MMD sigma has no universal robust setting [(Gretton et al., 2006-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9bca4d7b932e0854c3325f1578cfd17341dd8ea8). The 0-30 percent SwissProt noise protocol is also not directly validated; the closest protein precedent used 25-100 percent substitution without quantitative outcomes [(Madani et al., 2020-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c5f7074a264356c9a022a8dff24df79d1db8c3d3), and synthetic noise may not reproduce real undertraining [(Hu et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c757ffd5b8cc24ba12c9b0d80fecb7c035097b7d). Real-world protein validations often lack confidence intervals or significance tests [(Meshchaninov et al., 2024-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9e81434435fe1cbb032006334b089c70e863732b), [(Zeinalipour et al., 2024-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/54487bb7cb4945a4445c91add90bc8f45f28c53f).

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Convert the SwissProt 0-30 percent and five-family cluster experiments into a formal metric-reliability benchmark: pre-register ground-truth labels, compute bootstrap or block-bootstrap confidence intervals, rank correlations to perturbation level, AUC or ROC for cluster elimination, imbalance, and duplication, paired tests, and degenerate baselines such as random, duplicate, shuffled, and k-means-like collapse; use nested subsamples from a large generated pool and extend to 2^15 to 2^18 where feasible, and, where feasible, validate the synthetic signal against real undertrained checkpoints, following [(Theis et al., 2015-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/39e0c341351f8f4a39ac890b96217c7f4bde5369), [(Shmelkov et al., 2018-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8b1a1949789d12997b0a5ade953309308a81e115), [(Papineni et al., 2002-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d7da009f457917aa381619facfa5ffae9329a6e9), [(Ye et al., 2024-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7c6729bd4b2b3bdf013482b499c7401ab6de44ef), [(Meshchaninov et al., 2024-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9e81434435fe1cbb032006334b089c70e863732b), and [(Hu et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c757ffd5b8cc24ba12c9b0d80fecb7c035097b7d).

Make quality metrics comparable and calibrated: compute ppl, pppl, and scPerplexity against fixed reference protein language models such as ESM-2 650M or 3B and ProtT5 in addition to generator-specific values, with explicit definitions; run identical sequences through AlphaFold2, ESMFold, and OmegaFold under fixed settings, report pLDDT and pTM, length, MSA depth, recycling, runtime, and failure modes, and calibrate to LDDT or TM-score where available; add a small physical-validation subset with clashes, CN-score, and Rosetta relax to guard against high-confidence false positives, following [(Ye et al., 2024-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7c6729bd4b2b3bdf013482b499c7401ab6de44ef), [(Hsu et al., 2022-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f9c95ea9bcd529309c1d743f0926b5137f8ed60f), [(Lin et al., 2022-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c49a0912595a1cc70aab63524f64ed08c92194a8), [(Jumper et al., 2021-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/dc32a984b651256a8ec282be52310e6bd33d9815), [(Lai, and Chen, 2023-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/88c9b1885765a9bcd4539642de1295491f84fdb4), and [(Moffat et al., 2022-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1dc9e3d2128ca9c11cd401a884d72183beb6a846).

Expand diversity and distributional-similarity reporting beyond single scalars: report CD50 and CD95 with full MMseqs2 parameters and confidence intervals, add structural diversity such as pairwise TM-score, inner-TM, or Max Clust., report IPR and D&C as precision-recall curves, and sweep MMD RBF sigma using the median pairwise distance baseline plus a quantile grid, reporting conditional recommendations rather than one universal sigma, following [(Meshchaninov et al., 2024-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9e81434435fe1cbb032006334b089c70e863732b), [(Ye et al., 2024-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7c6729bd4b2b3bdf013482b499c7401ab6de44ef), [(Wang et al., 2024-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9ec09a9534fe41d3a5231693050150e0bbec8515), [(Kynkaanniemi et al., 2019-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d383dd8ced85d7898d8b1546c514a34fb626ea16), [(Gretton et al., 2006-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9bca4d7b932e0854c3325f1578cfd17341dd8ea8), and [(Muandet et al., 2016-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f0b9b5d6f79d62305f47ef560512bf53d17e38e2).
