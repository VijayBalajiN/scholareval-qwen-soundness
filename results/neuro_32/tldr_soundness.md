## Strengths 
The strongest support is for the PDMS mock-finger instability-mapping method, because [(Caron, 2023-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6613171e0a6a2bbbd3e3635321380c7bf47437fc) shows Sylgard 184 PDMS friction is governed by adhesion, elastic junction elongation, stick-slip, and velocity-dependent relaxation, and [(Viswanathan, and Sundaram, 2017-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cb1e347ec77f475a86c8b034b73f577ea814ccaf) shows soft elastomer force traces can be assigned to distinct slip and wave events. The minimal silane-coated silicon surface method is supported by [(Chaudhury et al., 2014-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/690276dcd975b048dbab78fc363f584466da7b2a), which demonstrates vapor-deposited silanes can create chemically distinct regions on the same silicon wafer. The human comparison and higher-rate trace analysis are supported by [(Zhang et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c2abc15c408b0b8ef82c77555f23af75aa64115e), [(Skedung et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/475c09ef715548a6b38bdb18d1fd4d6811c6dbe1), and [(Zhou et al., 2023-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0fc0ee667ac306ac827c67058c1d3a58daa65558), which show human friction is variable, 100 Hz may be too low for oscillation extraction, and constrained finger-down motion can produce stable signals.

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions concern the scale-up and sampling assumptions: [(Caron, 2023-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6613171e0a6a2bbbd3e3635321380c7bf47437fc) is nanoscale and does not validate gram-scale PDMS mock-finger phases, while [(Viswanathan, and Sundaram, 2017-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cb1e347ec77f475a86c8b034b73f577ea814ccaf) and [(Gao, and Wang, 2022-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2c49d57dd06c0fbe068552e9a37e9602566e9107) imply high-frequency or time-dependent events may challenge 550 Hz and nine fixed traces. The six-coating minimal-surface method is weakly supported because [(Chaudhury et al., 2014-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/690276dcd975b048dbab78fc363f584466da7b2a) only demonstrates wetting contrasts for one silane under condensation conditions, not friction, roughness, hysteresis, or aging. The three-phase taxonomy and load range are not validated by [(Skedung et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/475c09ef715548a6b38bdb18d1fd4d6811c6dbe1) or [(Li et al., 2024-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ad53062ed60bef45cadbaea85bd65a643d40cbab), which show instability may not map cleanly to surface parameters and the planned low loads may remain adhesion-dominated. The human vertical-downward protocol is challenged by [(Zhou et al., 2023-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0fc0ee667ac306ac827c67058c1d3a58daa65558), which did not test voluntary vertical strokes and observed velocity variability and a 508 Hz component above the 275 Hz Nyquist limit of 550 Hz sampling. Conventional baselines may already be informative, as [(Zhang et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c2abc15c408b0b8ef82c77555f23af75aa64115e) and [(Tomlinson et al., 2011-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c8bfb3872e88c45c1acc1528be4c1d2df59315d9) show roughness and geometry-based friction metrics can explain friction variation.

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Add a scale-bridging and sampling pilot before final data collection: use the same PDMS and at least two silane coatings in an AFM or micro-tribometer to measure adhesion, shear strength, friction versus load and velocity, and stick-slip frequency, then compare those boundaries with the macroscopic mock-finger traces; simultaneously pilot 550 Hz against 2 to 10 kHz force acquisition and optional high-speed imaging at 45 mm/s to check for aliasing, following concerns raised by [(Caron, 2023-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6613171e0a6a2bbbd3e3635321380c7bf47437fc), [(Viswanathan, and Sundaram, 2017-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cb1e347ec77f475a86c8b034b73f577ea814ccaf), and [(Zhou et al., 2023-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0fc0ee667ac306ac827c67058c1d3a58daa65558).

Predefine an objective instability-classification and pair-selection pipeline: specify bandpass filters, amplitude thresholds, event-detection rules, and inter-rater validation for steady sliding, slow frictional waves, and stiction spikes; compute event rates, durations, amplitudes, and phase-boundary locations across all 16 mass-velocity conditions; select the six human-test pairs using non-overlapping confidence intervals or effect sizes in instability differences, while checking surface and mock-finger topography before and after testing, as recommended by [(Skedung et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/475c09ef715548a6b38bdb18d1fd4d6811c6dbe1), [(Juvekar, and Singh, 2024-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d7d7c26b9b7c7929fb09e2ca7c96e59fe4dd8ddd), and [(Bochereau et al., 2017-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0710910fe3b849426f987ec89e16bc83b030ba73).

Strengthen the human protocol and baseline comparisons: pilot voluntary vertical downward strokes with a low-friction guide or real-time force or velocity feedback, record normal force, tangential force, velocity, and skin condition, and use at least 2 kHz sampling for human force data; measure average friction coefficient, 3D roughness, and contact angle hysteresis under the same controlled conditions and include them, along with velocity, load, stroke duration, and humidity, as covariates in the GLMM, addressing limitations in [(Zhou et al., 2023-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0fc0ee667ac306ac827c67058c1d3a58daa65558), [(Zhang et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c2abc15c408b0b8ef82c77555f23af75aa64115e), and [(Sergachev et al., 2021-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/322f92233f72b50913c369245230611ac0f61cfd).
