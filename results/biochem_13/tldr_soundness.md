## Strengths 
The strongest support is for the core cell-based PISA method: [(Gaetani et al., 2019-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/157a25bd7a9cb5bff789a977bac58a0d9154f7ff) demonstrated 1D PISA in K562 cells using heat treatment, soluble-fraction isolation, TMT-based LC-MS/MS, and recovery of known MTX and 5-FU targets, while [(Tan et al., 2015-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0de8ce01c8cc0c9446d52e9c8f457891718f7b9f) provided a CETSA precedent for measuring compound-dependent soluble protein abundance in cells. Lysate-based thermal proteomics and TMT-based MS quantification are also supported conceptually by [(Mateus et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3c0a85323332e10263ba6c99e5cc027978215fcb), [(Viéitez et al., 2021-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cde5f77f0d227dfa5a5c7afbc12883de08156fc9), and [(Saei et al., 2021-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a7ceeef78b31a8f57930cf9928f3c31262d2a5f1).

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions concern unvalidated operational details: [(Massey, 2018-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d968a6dca045ce64533cc039429052e79fe514c6) showed that heating method can change or invert apparent thermal-shift signals, challenging the assumption that a fixed 48-58 C cell-based window will unambiguously report engagement. [(Viéitez et al., 2021-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cde5f77f0d227dfa5a5c7afbc12883de08156fc9) used full 2D-TPP thermal series, implying that single-point PISA may miss subtle or protein-specific stability changes unless benchmarked. The cited PISA proof-of-concept in [(Gaetani et al., 2019-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/157a25bd7a9cb5bff789a977bac58a0d9154f7ff) used only a small 9-compound panel and does not validate TMTPro 16-plex scale, 384-hour instrument time, the proposed statistical cutoffs, or a native lysate arm. Additionally, [(Robers et al., 2015-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/70323254fe1d8f1c389bd3be59fb3d5e171dc45e) suggests that a single 30-minute endpoint may be insufficient for slow-binding or long-residence compounds.

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Run a pilot K562 cell and dounce-lysate PISA panel before the 96-compound screen, using the annotated 9-compound set, known binders and non-binders, vehicle, heat-only, compound-only, and lysate-only controls, and multiple heating conditions; compare cell versus lysate hits, benchmark single-temperature PISA against 2D-TPP where feasible, and derive the proposed log2 fold-change and variance cutoffs from pilot data [(Gaetani et al., 2019-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/157a25bd7a9cb5bff789a977bac58a0d9154f7ff), [(Massey, 2018-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d968a6dca045ce64533cc039429052e79fe514c6), [(Mateus et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3c0a85323332e10263ba6c99e5cc027978215fcb), [(Viéitez et al., 2021-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cde5f77f0d227dfa5a5c7afbc12883de08156fc9).

Reconcile the PISA design with the cited 1D PISA method by selecting one optimized temperature within 48-58 C or explicitly justifying a thermal gradient, then run a TMTPro 16-plex pilot to measure instrument time, missing values, normalization stability, plex effects, label carryover, and reproducibility before committing to 16 multiplexes and 384 hours [(Gaetani et al., 2019-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/157a25bd7a9cb5bff789a977bac58a0d9154f7ff), [(Viéitez et al., 2021-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cde5f77f0d227dfa5a5c7afbc12883de08156fc9).

Add quantitative orthogonal validation for selected hits, including time-course and washout experiments to test whether 10 uM for 30 minutes is sufficient, dose-response PISA across at least three to five concentrations, biochemical binding or target-occupancy assays, western blotting for predicted targets and downstream signaling nodes, and targeted MS where appropriate, with predefined acceptance criteria and side-by-side controls [(Robers et al., 2015-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/70323254fe1d8f1c389bd3be59fb3d5e171dc45e), [(Saei et al., 2021-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a7ceeef78b31a8f57930cf9928f3c31262d2a5f1), [(Grailer et al., unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/89c611f97f1e25b4f45d07ce186a795e35e019e3).
