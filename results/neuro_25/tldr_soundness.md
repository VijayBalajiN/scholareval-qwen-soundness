## Strengths 
The strongest support is for using task-based fMRI to detect duration-related temporal signals in parietal cortex, as shown by [(Hayashi et al., 2018-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c3d9101426abdb68a07d4f49760f77c5b1d795cf), which used GLM and searchlight MVPA to show frontal and parietal ROIs carried duration information, and by [(Baker et al., 2015-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/beae3f2b36080c965b214dd5fa1fcaec650dc548), which linked parietal connectivity to continuous duration variables. The learning and external-perspective components are also supported by [(Bellmund et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/05d25cae2f8b92be391f3c1b0cee9e60348b7194), which used a day-learning paradigm and medial temporal RSA to show that event-time and sequence knowledge can be acquired and represented in hippocampal and entorhinal regions. Finally, the planned permutation-based cluster FWE inference is methodologically supported by [(unknown_author, unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9a56f02d4ebfef73d76b0dec0f614f9ec9dd9f17), which provides a nonparametric framework for voxel and cluster tests.

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradiction is that the cited literature does not directly validate the central complementary hippocampal-parietal hypothesis: [(Hayashi et al., 2018-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c3d9101426abdb68a07d4f49760f77c5b1d795cf) did not test egocentric versus allocentric perspectives, sequential distance, or hippocampal sequence storage, and [(Baker et al., 2015-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/beae3f2b36080c965b214dd5fa1fcaec650dc548) used resting-state connectivity rather than task-based parametric modulation. A second major weakness is that parietal duration effects may reflect low-level or task-irrelevant duration processing, as suggested by [(Hayashi et al., 2015-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cfea4efde550cf5ae458eae2efe0fc303f713623), which found right IPL duration adaptation even when duration was not task-relevant. The external-perspective task also lacks direct validation because [(Bellmund et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/05d25cae2f8b92be391f3c1b0cee9e60348b7194) used event-time reconstruction rather than the proposed same/different part-of-day judgment and did not test the predicted reverse symbolic distance effect.

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Add a high-resolution representational analysis alongside parametric modulation: define a priori hippocampal, entorhinal, and posterior parietal ROIs or use searchlight MVPA/RSA to test whether medial temporal activity encodes the 15-event ritual sequence in a perspective-independent way while parietal activity shows perspective-by-sequential-distance or perspective-by-duration interactions, extending the duration-decoding approach in [(Hayashi et al., 2018-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c3d9101426abdb68a07d4f49760f77c5b1d795cf) and the medial temporal event-time RSA in [(Bellmund et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/05d25cae2f8b92be391f3c1b0cee9e60348b7194).

Tighten the GLM and confound-control pipeline by orthogonalizing sequential distance and duration, including trial-level regressors for reaction time, accuracy, syllable length, part-of-day, and head motion, and adding non-temporal or duration-matched controls to separate high-level temporal perspective processing from low-level duration adaptation, as cautioned by [(Hayashi et al., 2015-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cfea4efde550cf5ae458eae2efe0fc303f713623) and supported by the regression-based nuisance-control precedent in [(Gomez et al., 2013-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/69775409ccb5bc4106a250986e2e68a2216c6b85).

Make the learning and perspective manipulation behaviorally validated and preregistered: specify the 15-event ritual, durations, parts-of-day, reading and imagination instructions, and pre-scan learning criteria; add part-of-day categorization and event-time ordering or reconstruction checks; and preregister the expected symbolic distance effect in the internal task and reverse effect in the external task, building on the day-learning paradigm in [(Bellmund et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/05d25cae2f8b92be391f3c1b0cee9e60348b7194) and the permutation inference framework in [(unknown_author, unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9a56f02d4ebfef73d76b0dec0f614f9ec9dd9f17).
