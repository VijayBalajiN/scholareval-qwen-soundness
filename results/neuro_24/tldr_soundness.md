## Strengths 
The proposal is best supported by prior proof that genetically encoded neural reporters can be used in zebrafish [(Naumann et al., 2010-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/84d2a258aab602b91cd34b9048473d695528fd71), that larval zebrafish can be imaged live with low-phototoxic optical methods [(Truong et al., 2011-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ed2ef02903943051c5e90eccafbda291be872863), and that GCaMP-based wide-field fluorescence can capture PTZ-induced brain activity [(Turrini et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/663b2db2f2aec02e45542328a7593ed80cbbd5ba). The PTZ seizure model is also supported by larval behavioral PTZ assays [(Zheng et al., 2018-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6ea4b1b3cf860bd9e5b0027a4ccc1530e0f875ef), while CRISPR F0 crispant generation is supported by zebrafish CRISPR-Cas9 workflows [(Hwang et al., 2013-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d1efae3880aeb54be0b98c1fbb93a2282e756f94). Baseline whole-brain activity analysis is supported by template-based larval zebrafish calcium imaging pipelines [(Zarei et al., 2021-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cbbb8b0e79344a142d14ffa7e62962ae82fbaf52) and transgenic GCaMP time-series feasibility [(Kim et al., 2014-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c47e947d8dffb186a8c338274e16c5cb6406fa5e). Seizure quantification is supported by event-counting and duration metrics in PTZ and genetic models [(Hunyadi et al., 2017-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0b3e3428a2e9a28098d9a9aa2597ff0905fe61b7), and the galr1a receptor question is conceptually supported by GalR1 loss-of-function seizure work [(Schauwecker, 2010-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/fca024df28efb8d1425c55972c41976a8debf8d4). qPCR validation is supported by zebrafish brain expression assays [(Jeong et al., 2019-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1ad3940ba2b748c00d0e9dd47d0c4cad77123847) and reference-gene validation workflows [(Ahi et al., 2019-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/324fc9d52c65096c5f0450932db7a578dc05db91).

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions concern the lack of direct validation for the proposed imaging and seizure thresholds. The cited live-imaging work used two-photon scanned light-sheet imaging of a heart, not 1-hour wide-field epifluorescence of whole-brain GCaMP signals [(Truong et al., 2011-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ed2ef02903943051c5e90eccafbda291be872863), and the transgenic reporter precedent used aequorin bioluminescence rather than elavl3:GCaMP5G/6f [(Naumann et al., 2010-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/84d2a258aab602b91cd34b9048473d695528fd71). The PTZ evidence is behavioral or qualitative and does not justify a fixed 20 mM dose, rebound protocol, or 100% whole-brain ΔF/F0 seizure definition [(Zheng et al., 2018-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6ea4b1b3cf860bd9e5b0027a4ccc1530e0f875ef), [(Turrini et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/663b2db2f2aec02e45542328a7593ed80cbbd5ba). Seizure detection is also weakened because a single amplitude threshold can include artifacts and dubious events, so duration, spatial extent, manual review, and classifier validation are needed [(Hunyadi et al., 2017-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0b3e3428a2e9a28098d9a9aa2597ff0905fe61b7). The eaat2a-/- model is complicated by SLC1 paralog redundancy and region-specific expression [(Gesemann et al., 2010-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/55a57b4aeecfd77f426fccb54a14a45683d73dc5), and the manual wide-field ROI approach is less standardized than template-registered imaging [(Zarei et al., 2021-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cbbb8b0e79344a142d14ffa7e62962ae82fbaf52). The galr1a crispant plan lacks quantitative mutation, mosaicism, off-target, and receptor-loss validation [(Hwang et al., 2013-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d1efae3880aeb54be0b98c1fbb93a2282e756f94), [(Facchinello et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/40cda564f1fd4927212be96931fbe4dfd9cfed33). qPCR is weakened by the need for condition-specific reference-gene validation and by the possibility that receptor perturbations may not change galanin transcript levels [(Ahi et al., 2019-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/324fc9d52c65096c5f0450932db7a578dc05db91), [(Botz et al., 2016-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1001bdf2d5155a63142dbdb5ef89e2a6f31a38b0). Finally, the statistical plan is too generic and lacks power analysis, randomization, blinding, and metric-specific models [(Hübner et al., 2018-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/81f1e9fd9fbc4b6c54248686f769aa567ea9dafa).

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Run a pilot 1-hour wide-field imaging validation in 5 dpf elavl3:GCaMP5G and elavl3:GCaMP6f larvae to measure photobleaching, phototoxicity, motion artifacts, signal-to-noise, and baseline stability, using low illumination, no-illumination or low-illumination controls, and template-assisted ROI segmentation; then calibrate the 5%, 10%, and 100% ΔF/F0 thresholds against noise, indicator kinetics, and alternative z-scored or threshold-sensitivity analyses [(Truong et al., 2011-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ed2ef02903943051c5e90eccafbda291be872863), [(Zarei et al., 2021-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cbbb8b0e79344a142d14ffa7e62962ae82fbaf52), [(Kim et al., 2014-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c47e947d8dffb186a8c338274e16c5cb6406fa5e).

Add a PTZ and seizure-detection validation experiment before main galanin experiments: test a small dose-response around 20 mM in acute and rebound conditions with vehicle controls, record simultaneous muscle movement or behavior, manually annotate a representative subset of recordings to classify seizure, artifact, dubious, and interictal events, and report sensitivity, specificity, positive predictive value, false positives, and false negatives; refine the seizure definition with minimum duration, spatial extent, refractory period, and threshold sensitivity analyses [(Turrini et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/663b2db2f2aec02e45542328a7593ed80cbbd5ba), [(Zheng et al., 2018-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6ea4b1b3cf860bd9e5b0027a4ccc1530e0f875ef), [(Hunyadi et al., 2017-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0b3e3428a2e9a28098d9a9aa2597ff0905fe61b7).

Strengthen genetic, molecular, and statistical validation: sequence and genotype galr1a crispants, quantify mutation frequency, mosaicism, off-target risk, and galr1a transcript/protein loss, and include rescue or receptor antagonist controls; validate eaat2a-/- loss and SLC1 paralog expression; validate qPCR reference genes under all genotypes and PTZ conditions using multiple independent pools; and pre-register a metric-specific analysis plan with power analysis, randomization, blinding, mixed or nonparametric tests for continuous variables, and negative binomial regression for count data [(Hwang et al., 2013-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d1efae3880aeb54be0b98c1fbb93a2282e756f94), [(Facchinello et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/40cda564f1fd4927212be96931fbe4dfd9cfed33), [(Gesemann et al., 2010-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/55a57b4aeecfd77f426fccb54a14a45683d73dc5), [(Ahi et al., 2019-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/324fc9d52c65096c5f0450932db7a578dc05db91), [(Botz et al., 2016-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1001bdf2d5155a63142dbdb5ef89e2a6f31a38b0), [(Hübner et al., 2018-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/81f1e9fd9fbc4b6c54248686f769aa567ea9dafa).
