## Strengths 
The strongest support is that macaque extracellular recording with fixation, receptive-field mapping, direction tuning, firing-rate summaries, and eye-data quality control is feasible, as shown by [(Wild et al., 2022-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/07aa74fcc4bb9da49fdb117f36166988d74769eb), and that candidate encoding models can be compared using likelihood-based cross-validation on held-out data, as motivated by [(Pillow, unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/371b1a357007fb7c42763c8bd95d8c9ac9f8c512). This supports the proposed single-neuron MT recording and model-comparison framework, although the cited evidence is indirect for MT speed tuning and bi-speed stimuli.

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradiction is that the cited macaque recording work does not validate the MT-specific, speed-tuning, or bi-speed steps of the proposal, because it lacks area MT, speed ratios, bi-speed stimuli, fixation performance, neuron count, and statistical outcomes, as noted in [(Wild et al., 2022-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/07aa74fcc4bb9da49fdb117f36166988d74769eb). The second strongest contradiction is the overfitting risk in model comparison: a more parameterized model can fit training data well but fail on new data, as illustrated by [(Pillow, unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/371b1a357007fb7c42763c8bd95d8c9ac9f8c512), so the proposal must not rely only on training-set fit for averaging, soft-max, speed-bias, or normalization models.

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Add an explicit likelihood-based cross-validation protocol for every encoding model: fit averaging, soft-max, slow-speed bias, fast-speed bias, and modified divisive normalization models on a training subset of single-speed and bi-speed trials, then compare held-out log-likelihoods, AIC/BIC, likelihood-ratio or permutation tests, parameter counts, and confidence intervals, following the validation standard in [(Pillow, unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/371b1a357007fb7c42763c8bd95d8c9ac9f8c512).

Operationalize the macaque MT recording pipeline with concrete feasibility details: specify electrode type, sampling rate, spike-sorting criteria, fixation thresholds, eye-data quality control, receptive-field mapping metrics, direction-tuning indices, speed-tuning curves, and inclusion criteria for the target 100 neurons, extending the general macaque recording workflow supported by [(Wild et al., 2022-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/07aa74fcc4bb9da49fdb117f36166988d74769eb).

Align the bi-speed stimulus design with the psychophysical speed pairs and add controls and population analysis: use the same 4:1 and 2:1 speed ratios and speed ranges, randomly interleave single-speed and bi-speed trials with sufficient trial counts, include 90-degree direction-separation and opposite-field attention controls, and evaluate pseudo-population responses with cross-validated linear classifiers while reporting full reproducibility details.
