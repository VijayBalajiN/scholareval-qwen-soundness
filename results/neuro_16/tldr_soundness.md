## Strengths 
The most strongly supported methods are the core all-optical pipeline and its sequential validation structure. In vivo two-photon calcium imaging combined with two-photon holographic optogenetic stimulation of small ensembles while recording a wider field of view is directly supported by prior work in mouse visual cortex using a bicistronic GCaMP8s-family indicator and red-shifted opsin [(LaFosse et al., 2022-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1010d6127adc58e0580faef6a74013b57edb08a7) and [(LaFosse et al., 2023-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/26ba0364095470a0f3fb647cfdb2aa7a50e25d8a). This supports the proposed simultaneous GCaMP8s imaging and rsChRmine stimulation, the four-session baseline/stimulation/post-stimulation design, tuning analysis, and measurement of optogenetic response kinetics. Additional support comes from two-photon auditory-cortex imaging for tone-evoked ΔF/F baselines [(Kline et al., 2021-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4ce09852f40083849115774740cef38e1da56746), auditory optogenetic modulation with no-light or baseline comparisons [(Aizenberg et al., 2015-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/39a2ec89389ede9d0403c56ca9527571816afdb8) and [(Nocon et al., 2023-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2e1794b37373003ba18544c465d2e12ce8c68ae7), passive no-stimulation controls [(James et al., 2018-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e2a18feaf7a7bfd92f6159c891cca2567bcc4836), A1 frequency-preference maps and local spatial tuning heterogeneity [(Bowen et al., 2019-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f2e23eb3d78060040112339530fabb33b24815d2), holographic spatial-offset and depth/power calibration [(Triplett et al., 2023-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9d1fdb72be2328293d106365d9f20374230ad1a3) and [(Ronzitti et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/20b6db4822512484b3fda84d998d801e21196604), and baseline-relative activation criteria with distance decay [(Schmieder et al., 2022-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ac0ac520022ceeeb270a260aa9e68fc209d54716).

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions are validation gaps rather than direct experimental failures. Prior all-optical work was performed in visual cortex with a different construct and visual stimuli, so it does not prove that AAV9-hSyn-GCaMP8s-T2A-rsChRmine will support reliable five-cell co-tuned targeting in auditory cortex at the proposed 5 mW per cell, 15 μm spiral, 30 revolutions, and 100 ms parameters [(LaFosse et al., 2022-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1010d6127adc58e0580faef6a74013b57edb08a7) and [(LaFosse et al., 2023-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/26ba0364095470a0f3fb647cfdb2aa7a50e25d8a). The auditory baseline evidence is not directly A1 pure-tone and lacks quantitative validation of the proposed 16 kHz and 54 kHz, 100 ms, 30-trial random-order protocol [(Kline et al., 2021-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4ce09852f40083849115774740cef38e1da56746). The 0 mW control is not directly proven sufficient because optogenetic effects can alter temporal coding, reliability, and discrimination, not only evoked amplitude [(Nocon et al., 2023-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2e1794b37373003ba18544c465d2e12ce8c68ae7), [(Aizenberg et al., 2015-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/39a2ec89389ede9d0403c56ca9527571816afdb8), and [(Zhu et al., 2015-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/aee9f349f9dbeb75033cb153e2ad9960c8e0d73e). Session 4 persistence is not supported by prior delayed no-light tone series [(James et al., 2018-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e2a18feaf7a7bfd92f6159c891cca2567bcc4836). Spatial and holographic validation is also limited by synthetic or slice-derived data, speckle, NA loss, SLM tiling artifacts, and lack of in vivo auditory multi-cell benchmarks [(Triplett et al., 2023-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9d1fdb72be2328293d106365d9f20374230ad1a3), [(Ronzitti et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/20b6db4822512484b3fda84d998d801e21196604), and [(Schmieder et al., 2022-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ac0ac520022ceeeb270a260aa9e68fc209d54716).

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Add a dedicated pilot validation of AAV9-hSyn-GCaMP8s-T2A-rsChRmine and holographic stimulation before the main auditory experiments: quantify co-expression, spectral separation, phototoxicity, GCaMP8s signal-to-noise, and rsChRmine activation at 5 mW per cell, 15 μm spiral, 30 revolutions, and 100 ms; pre-register a ΔF/F activation criterion for each target cell and report per-cell activation probability, ensemble success rate, write-in error, and distance or depth decay, benchmarking against validated red-shifted holographic systems where needed [(LaFosse et al., 2023-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/26ba0364095470a0f3fb647cfdb2aa7a50e25d8a), [(Triplett et al., 2023-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9d1fdb72be2328293d106365d9f20374230ad1a3), [(Ronzitti et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/20b6db4822512484b3fda84d998d801e21196604), and [(Schmieder et al., 2022-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ac0ac520022ceeeb270a260aa9e68fc209d54716).

Strengthen the control design to separate optogenetic ensemble effects from adaptation, laser artifacts, and spatial spillover: implement a true 0 mW no-light condition with identical optical path, hologram timing, tone sequence, ISI, trial order, and session length; counterbalance or interleave 0 mW and 5 mW sessions; add position-shifted, non-targeted, non-co-tuned, and subthreshold low-power controls; model adaptation as a function of trial and session; and analyze ΔF/F amplitude plus tuning selectivity, trial-to-trial reliability, latency, and temporal coding metrics [(Aizenberg et al., 2015-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/39a2ec89389ede9d0403c56ca9527571816afdb8), [(Nocon et al., 2023-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2e1794b37373003ba18544c465d2e12ce8c68ae7), [(James et al., 2018-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e2a18feaf7a7bfd92f6159c891cca2567bcc4836), and [(Zhu et al., 2015-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/aee9f349f9dbeb75033cb153e2ad9960c8e0d73e).

Make the spatial and persistence analyses explicit and statistically powered: build frequency-preference maps and local tuning-heterogeneity metrics in A1, compare affected neurons to co-tuned and non-co-tuned baselines, and use spatially explicit offset mapping with x/y and, if feasible, z shifts plus PSF and speckle calibration; make Session 4 an identical no-light persistence test compared with Session 2 and a no-stimulation control using pre-registered mixed-effects or permutation tests, multiple-comparison correction, effect sizes, confidence intervals, and sample-size justification [(Bowen et al., 2019-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f2e23eb3d78060040112339530fabb33b24815d2), [(Triplett et al., 2023-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9d1fdb72be2328293d106365d9f20374230ad1a3), [(Ronzitti et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/20b6db4822512484b3fda84d998d801e21196604), and [(James et al., 2018-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e2a18feaf7a7bfd92f6159c891cca2567bcc4836).
