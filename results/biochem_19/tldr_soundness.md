## Strengths 
The strongest literature support is for the BFB probe validation and mitochondrial affinity-MS workflow: a metformin-derived clickable surrogate can be used in cell-based mitochondrial assays including imaging, western blotting, ROS, membrane potential, and metal quantification [(Müller et al., 2018-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e65a71ba3750cac105e8504db4d05c2d17fc543e), and metformin has pancreatic cancer relevance in KC/KPC models [(Chen et al., 2017-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/201f5417bd7a3b2670c664d2295cd6fed39ba5c0). Streptavidin bead capture of affinity-labeled mitochondrial proteomes with LC-MS label-free quantification and concentration-dependent analysis is directly analogous to the planned BFB pull-down [(Tzeng, and Maier, 2016-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8caca5242057f3d6f9c3b7791cbfe1591759d40b). Mitochondrial morphology readouts are supported methodologically by IF/confocal studies of mitochondrial perturbations [(Lovy et al., 2020-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/12ae70424db85f91b1dc3b73e1d76caa5dfe86af) and [(Thejer et al., 2019-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1b95707106286e73e733088ab656050a19b47394). Pooled CRISPR screens are supported by MAGeCK analysis [(Li et al., 2014-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3fcd6a0ae673c4e196dc13a994d7f12514fc53d) and drug-conditioned screen validation workflows [(Haldrup et al., 2023-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3e2f525ff4f082d565654d86a8e72306d2e1fa1a), [(Deng et al., 2024-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3c1094cbe0489538a05dbfae684364d473197081).

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions concern the central ATP5I hypothesis and the specificity of the BFB pull-down. The literature does not directly establish ATP5I as a biguanide target; metformin may act through mitochondrial copper homeostasis, so BFB-ATP5I binding could be indirect [(Müller et al., 2018-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e65a71ba3750cac105e8504db4d05c2d17fc543e), and the pancreatic cancer evidence is in vivo and lacks quantitative cellular molecular detail [(Chen et al., 2017-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/201f5417bd7a3b2670c664d2295cd6fed39ba5c0). The analogous streptavidin-MS workflow used covalent HNE adducts rather than reversible biguanide binding and lacked the proposed biotin-amine control and metformin competition, so it does not prove that competition elution will specifically release ATP5I [(Tzeng, and Maier, 2016-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8caca5242057f3d6f9c3b7791cbfe1591759d40b). Mitochondrial morphology readouts lack quantitative scoring and TOMM20 may not resolve cristae [(Lovy et al., 2020-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/12ae70424db85f91b1dc3b73e1d76caa5dfe86af), [(Thejer et al., 2019-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1b95707106286e73e733088ab656050a19b47394). CRISPR screen support does not establish that NALM-6 metformin, rotenone, or oligomycin A screens will generate sufficient selection or identify ATP5I-related mechanisms [(Li et al., 2014-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3fcd6a0ae673c4e196dc13a994d7f12514fc53d), [(Haldrup et al., 2023-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3e2f525ff4f082d565654d86a8e72306d2e1fa1a), [(Deng et al., 2024-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3c1094cbe0489538a05dbfae684364d473197081).

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Add a quantitative BFB validation package before pull-downs: test BFB, metformin, and the biotin-amine control in KP-4 cells with dose-response curves for AMPK phosphorylation, viability, MitoTracker/TOMM20 colocalization, ROS, membrane potential, and copper/iron, using at least three biological replicates, endogenous biotin blocking, non-biotinylated biguanide and biotinylated non-biguanide controls, and copper chelation or supplementation to distinguish direct ATP5I engagement from copper-mediated effects [(Müller et al., 2018-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e65a71ba3750cac105e8504db4d05c2d17fc543e), [(Chen et al., 2017-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/201f5417bd7a3b2670c664d2295cd6fed39ba5c0).

Make the BFB pull-down specificity quantitative and competition-based: run BFB, biotin-amine, no-probe, bead-only, and streptavidin-only captures; elute with titrated metformin; compare eluted and retained proteins by MS and immunoblotting; use a mitochondrial reference proteome, biological and technical replicates, predefined fold-change and FDR thresholds, and report bead amount, incubation time, wash stringency, and MS settings; validate ATP5I by independent immunoblot and SPR [(Tzeng, and Maier, 2016-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8caca5242057f3d6f9c3b7791cbfe1591759d40b).

Pre-specify the CRISPR screen and validation pipeline: use MAGeCK with sgRNA read counts, normalization, mean-variance modeling, FDR control, and gene-level RRA scores; choose growth-inhibitory metformin, rotenone, and oligomycin A concentrations from dose-response curves, include vehicle controls, biological replicates, and multiple time points; if selection is weak, extend duration, increase drug pressure, or use CRISPRi/a; validate top hits and ATP5I with KO/rescue, dose-response viability, AMPK, Seahorse OCR/ECAR, NAD+/NADH, quantitative TOMM20 morphology with blinded scoring and EM or super-resolution if needed, and transcriptomics, comparing genetic interaction profiles by rank correlation and pathway enrichment [(Li et al., 2014-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3fcd6a0ae673c4e196dc13a994d7f12514fc53d), [(Haldrup et al., 2023-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3e2f525ff4f082d565654d86a8e72306d2e1fa1a), [(Deng et al., 2024-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3c1094cbe0489538a05dbfae684364d473197081).
