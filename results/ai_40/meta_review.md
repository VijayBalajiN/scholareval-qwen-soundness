## Context: To create multiple graph views for contrastive learning while preserving essential structure, the plan uses adaptive augmentations. Method: Graph Augmentation Strategy: We will implement an adaptive augmentation approach that generates multiple views of the original graph while preserving essential structural properties. This includes node-wise augmentations (feature masking and node dropping), edge-wise augmentations (edge perturbation), subgraph sampling (k-hop neighborhoods), and global augmentations (attribute masking and virtual node addition).

### Support

The strongest support for the proposed adaptive augmentation strategy comes from graph contrastive work that directly perturbs node attributes and topology and evaluates on the plan's citation/social benchmarks. [(Zhu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0d67d3ddca1c4e370eaf1e99ec674f612c39c66c) GCA samples two stochastic augmentation functions per step, applies topology-level and node-attribute-level perturbations, uses a two-layer GCN and two-layer projection head, and reports node-level InfoNCE with inter- and intra-view negatives; it is competitive with supervised GCN/GAT on Wiki-CS, Amazon-Computers, Amazon-Photo, Coauthor-CS, and Coauthor-Physics and shows a plateau when probabilities are moderate, which supports the plan's use of adaptive node/edge/attribute views and the need to tune probabilities. This is reinforced by [(You et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/76c124786ccf4263e6403a15a8e350ac28be4e65), which explicitly tests node dropping, edge perturbation, attribute masking, and random-walk subgraph sampling on graph classification, finds that no augmentation is often worse than training from scratch, that a single well-chosen augmentation improves NCI1/PROTEINS/COLLAB/RDT-B by 1.62/3.15/6.27/1.66 percent, and that composing augmentations helps, directly validating the plan's multi-primitive augmentation suite except virtual node addition. The same core primitives are supported on the plan's exact datasets by [(Zhu et al., 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4bf76588122827157c43a59e656dccc6b6a22e90), whose GRACE uses random edge removal and node-feature masking with a two-layer GCN for Cora/Citeseer/PubMed/DBLP, a three-layer GraphSAGE-GCN with neighbor sampling for Reddit, and a two-layer MLP projection head, reports consistent gains over unsupervised baselines and a notable PPI gain attributed to edge removal when features are sparse, and finds stability to corruption probabilities. Earlier, [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098) already showed that feature corruption and k-hop patch sampling can produce useful node embeddings on Cora, Citeseer, PubMed, and Reddit, with 82.3/71.8/76.8 percent transductive accuracy and 0.940 micro-F1 on Reddit, although it treats corrupted graphs as negatives rather than positive augmented views. Newer adaptive/structure-preserving evidence includes [(Li et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0974d431b08d94436a99e939aada96a8535b338b), which uses rationale probabilities to preserve semantically important nodes during augmentation and improves average gain over training from scratch from 3.77 to 6.16 on MoleculeNet transfer, and [(Köse, and Shen, 2021-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9dba5a90206ea7c29cbe27923018f070bf8a0016), which combines feature masking with degree- or triangle-aware edge deletion on Pokec social networks and improves fairness with only 0.07-0.26 percent accuracy loss, supporting adaptive edge perturbation that preserves structure. The taxonomy in [(Wu et al., 2021-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2d00798b8a7d979c925901e9faa5fe4360030ca2) further supports the plan by categorizing adaptive augmentation and local/context/global contrasts, while image-domain ablations in [(Chen et al., 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7af72a461ed7cda180e7eab878efd5f35d79bbf4) show that augmentation composition, not merely two views, determines representation quality, with crop plus color distortion reaching 56.3/55.8 linear top-1 versus 33.1 for crop alone. Weaker but conceptually relevant support comes from [(He et al., 2019-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/add2f205338d70e10ce5e686df4a690e2851bdfc) and [(Grill et al., 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/38f93092ece8eee9771e61c1edaf11b1293cae1b) for projection heads, temperature, and view-based pretraining, from [(Qiu et al., 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/91fb815361fdbf80ff15ce4d783a41846bd99232) for subgraph instances and contrastive pretraining, from [(Sun et al., unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ce91e8cfa68fc4e70b82685e7bd30399e21543f4) for molecular overlap of node dropping, edge perturbation, subgraph extraction, and attribute masking, from [(Sun et al., 2022-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/da925c21ca185846855e9f1d4af10c35ff59141f) for contrastive view quality and dimensional collapse mitigation, and from [(Huo et al., 2022-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e30b0536a8b12450ba4b34280308d5e30c70e816) for threshold-controlled multi-view contrast, although these do not directly validate the plan's adaptive hierarchical graph augmentation.

### Contradictions

Strongest contradiction is that structure-changing adaptive augmentation may not be necessary: [(Wang et al., 2022-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/34f409fa13fd45db84e56ade5e5d26a82baedf0b) FastGCL avoids node/edge/subgraph perturbations and contrasts weighted versus unweighted neighborhood aggregation, yet remains competitive with recent graph contrastive methods, outperforms supervised GNNs on WikiCS and Computers, and converges in fewer than 100 epochs on WikiCS while BGRL needs more than 9,000 epochs for best result, implying the plan must justify adaptive augmentation over simpler non-augmenting contrast. A second strong contradiction is that complex adaptive augmentation may not beat simple fixed perturbations and can be memory expensive: [(Thakoor et al., 2021-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b38fcf2aba8b173ba11ada830b7038f496643893) BGRL with simple augmentations matches or exceeds GRACE and GCA on WikiCS, Amazon Computers, Amazon Photos, Coauthor CS, and Coauthor Physics (e.g., 79.98 versus 80.14/78.35 on WikiCS and 90.34 versus 89.53/88.94 on Amazon Computers) while using 0.47-5.50 GB versus 1.81-11.78 GB or OOM for GRACE on a 16GB V100, challenging the assumption that richer adaptive views are required. Third, augmentation effects are dataset-specific and can be harmful: [(You et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/76c124786ccf4263e6403a15a8e350ac28be4e65) reports edge perturbation beneficial for social networks but harmful for some biochemical molecule datasets, and no augmentation can be worse than training from scratch, so the plan's fixed inclusion of edge perturbation and subgraph sampling on Cora/Citeseer/PubMed/Reddit needs dataset-specific ablation. [(Zhu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0d67d3ddca1c4e370eaf1e99ec674f612c39c66c) also shows GCA accuracy degrades when augmentation probabilities are too large, e.g., edge removal 0.9 creates isolated nodes, contradicting a one-size-fits-all adaptive schedule. In low-label settings, self-supervised edge-removal contrast can underperform: [(Wu et al., 2023-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c45ca17fd3f360128c2f1f2ee288efa2a33bd253) finds DGI/GRACE/GBT/SupCon with edge-removal augmentation perform poorly on TwiBot-20 where only a small fraction of nodes are labeled, while supervised contrastive BotSCL is superior, warning the plan's low-label 1-10 per class experiments may not benefit from self-supervised augmentation alone. Older evidence also limits the claim: [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098) uses feature shuffling and k-hop sampling but as negatives, not positive augmented views, and supervised GCN outperforms it on PubMed, while [(Sun et al., unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ce91e8cfa68fc4e70b82685e7bd30399e21543f4) and [(Qiu et al., 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/91fb815361fdbf80ff15ce4d783a41846bd99232) provide no numerical evidence in the supplied excerpts, so their support for the exact adaptive hierarchical method is weak. Finally, [(Lee, and Shin, 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f1648a82c10421c2ccb11d251e31bf1fc2720436) shows that multi-scale contrast can collapse if a single scale is used alone, e.g., membership-level contrast alone collapses on Citeseer, DBLP, and Mushroom, indicating the plan's node/subgraph/graph losses cannot be assumed to combine safely without ablation.

### Suggested Action

Most importantly, the plan should replace the generic phrase adaptive augmentation with an explicit adaptive mechanism and a controlled ablation against simpler alternatives. Because [(Zhu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0d67d3ddca1c4e370eaf1e99ec674f612c39c66c) already uses stochastic topology/attribute augmentation and [(Li et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0974d431b08d94436a99e939aada96a8535b338b) uses rationale probabilities to preserve semantic nodes, the method should define how node dropping, feature masking, edge perturbation, k-hop sampling, global attribute masking, and virtual node addition are selected or weighted, e.g., attention/gradient-based scores, degree/triangle-aware edge deletion as in [(Köse, and Shen, 2021-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9dba5a90206ea7c29cbe27923018f070bf8a0016), or rationale probabilities, and should report per-dataset probability schedules. The first experiments should compare the full adaptive suite against fixed GRACE-style edge removal plus feature masking [(Zhu et al., 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4bf76588122827157c43a59e656dccc6b6a22e90), GCA-style stochastic augmentation [(Zhu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0d67d3ddca1c4e370eaf1e99ec674f612c39c66c), BGRL-style simple augmentation [(Thakoor et al., 2021-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b38fcf2aba8b173ba11ada830b7038f496643893), and FastGCL-style no structure-changing contrast [(Wang et al., 2022-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/34f409fa13fd45db84e56ade5e5d26a82baedf0b), with ablations for each primitive because [(You et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/76c124786ccf4263e6403a15a8e350ac28be4e65) shows composition helps but edge perturbation can be domain-specific and [(Chen et al., 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7af72a461ed7cda180e7eab878efd5f35d79bbf4) shows augmentation composition determines quality. Second, the implementation should be scaled to the target datasets: use a 2-layer GCN or GIN but consider hidden dimension 512 and projection dimension 256 as in [(Zhu et al., 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4bf76588122827157c43a59e656dccc6b6a22e90), [(Zhu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0d67d3ddca1c4e370eaf1e99ec674f612c39c66c), and [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098), rather than only hidden 128/projection 64, and for Reddit use neighbor sampling such as 30/25/20 or 10/10/25 hops to avoid memory issues, reporting GPU memory and training time as in [(Thakoor et al., 2021-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b38fcf2aba8b173ba11ada830b7038f496643893). Third, the hierarchical loss should be validated by removing node, subgraph, and graph terms separately, with learnable weights constrained or regularized, because [(Lee, and Shin, 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f1648a82c10421c2ccb11d251e31bf1fc2720436) shows multi-scale contrast helps only when scales are combined and single-scale contrast can collapse, and [(Wu et al., 2021-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2d00798b8a7d979c925901e9faa5fe4360030ca2) supports local/context/global contrast but provides no numerical guarantee. Fourth, global augmentations need special care: virtual node addition has no direct support in the supplied graph works, so it should be treated as an ablation and compared with graph-level readout or fingerprint/global consistency losses such as those in [(Sun et al., unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ce91e8cfa68fc4e70b82685e7bd30399e21543f4) and [(Qiu et al., 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/91fb815361fdbf80ff15ce4d783a41846bd99232); global attribute masking should follow [(You et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/76c124786ccf4263e6403a15a8e350ac28be4e65) and [(Zhu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0d67d3ddca1c4e370eaf1e99ec674f612c39c66c) with moderate probabilities. Fifth, temperature and augmentation ratio should be tuned using validation, with initial ranges informed by [(He et al., 2019-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/add2f205338d70e10ce5e686df4a690e2851bdfc) temperature 0.07, [(Li et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0974d431b08d94436a99e939aada96a8535b338b) temperature around 0.1, [(You et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/76c124786ccf4263e6403a15a8e350ac28be4e65) augmentation ratio 0.2, and [(Zhu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0d67d3ddca1c4e370eaf1e99ec674f612c39c66c) plateau at moderate probabilities. Finally, evaluation should use 20 runs with standard deviations and significance tests, include low-label semi-supervised fine-tuning rather than only linear probing because [(Wu et al., 2023-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c45ca17fd3f360128c2f1f2ee288efa2a33bd253) shows self-supervised edge-removal contrast can be weak when labels are sparse, and add quantitative embedding metrics such as silhouette or NMI alongside t-SNE as in [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098) and [(Lee, and Shin, 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f1648a82c10421c2ccb11d251e31bf1fc2720436).

## Context: To encode augmented graph views and produce latent representations for contrastive learning, the plan specifies a shared GNN encoder and projection head. Method: Model Architecture: Our framework will employ a shared GNN encoder (such as GCN or GIN) to learn node embeddings from each augmented view, followed by a projection head implemented as a multi-layer perceptron to map embeddings into a latent space for contrastive learning.

### Support

The strongest support for the proposed shared GNN encoder plus MLP projection head is [(Shao et al., 2021-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8d16a43dfdb9d280a8de327635a3a6c6fe1bfd86) because it directly uses a shared GCN-based encoder and shared MLP projection heads for node and graph contrastive learning, with PyTorch, Adam, Xavier initialization, one GCN layer, 2,000 epochs, 512-dimensional node and graph latent representations, early stopping patience 20, and logistic regression on Cora, Citeseer, Pubmed, WikiCS, CoauthorCS, Cornell, and Actor; it reports 4% and 1.4% gains over GCA and GMI on Cora, 4.7% and 13.4% over MVGRL and GCA on Cornell, outperforms supervised GCN, GAT, and GraphSAGE across seven datasets, and ablations show feature-space kNN augmentation beats edge perturbation, attribute masking, and graph diffusion by 15.3% absolute over the edge-perturbation variant and node-plus-graph contrast beats node-only or graph-only, supporting the plan's shared encoder, projection head, and multi-level contrastive losses, though it omits subgraph-level loss, low-label regimes, Reddit, and inductive evaluation. Taken iteratively, this 2021 result builds on the 2020 projection-head and augmentation-composition evidence of [(Chen et al., 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7af72a461ed7cda180e7eab878efd5f35d79bbf4), which used a ResNet base encoder, a one-hidden-layer ReLU MLP projection head, and NT-Xent on ImageNet linear probing, where crop alone achieved 33.1, color distortion alone 18.8, and crop plus color distortion achieved 56.3 or 55.8 depending on order, supporting the plan's separate MLP head and composed node, edge, subgraph, and global augmentations, although the evidence is image-based and lacks p-values or confidence intervals. A further support is [(You et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/76c124786ccf4263e6403a15a8e350ac28be4e65), which validates graph augmentations that overlap the plan, including node dropping, edge perturbation, attribute masking, and random-walk subgraph sampling with a default augmentation ratio of 0.2, and shows that without augmentation graph contrastive learning is not helpful and is often worse than training from scratch, while a single best augmentation improves NCI1 by 1.62%, PROTEINS by 3.15%, COLLAB by 6.27%, and RDT-B by 1.66% over scratch, aligning with the plan's adaptive augmentation strategy and GraphCL baseline, but it is graph-level and does not specify a projection head. An older but still important support is [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098), which uses a shared GNN encoder and contrastive patch-summary views with 50 training runs, Adam learning rate 0.001 except 1e-5 on Reddit, hidden dimension 512 except 256 on Pubmed, Reddit minibatches of 256 nodes and neighborhood subsampling of 10, 10, and 25 neighbors producing patches of 2611 nodes, and achieves 82.3 +/- 0.6% on Cora, 71.8 +/- 0.7% on Citeseer, 76.8 +/- 0.6% on Pubmed, 0.940 +/- 0.001 micro-F1 on Reddit, and 0.638 +/- 0.002 on PPI, outperforming DeepWalk with features and random initialization, establishing a strong self-supervised node-classification baseline, although it uses an averaging readout and bilinear discriminator rather than an MLP projection head. The weakest support is [(Jiang et al., 2024-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8bfae1bbe1643461ad65c3c85680f2c9e4b20b3b) because it describes an encoder, projection head, decoder, normalized temperature-scaled cross-entropy loss, and augmentations including node dropout, edge perturbation, attribute masking, subgraph sampling, and feature masking, but the provided excerpt contains no quantitative outcomes, baseline comparisons, dataset names, hardware, optimizer settings, or statistical significance, so it only weakly supports the architectural and augmentation choices.

### Contradictions

The strongest contradiction is [(Hassani, and Ahmadi, 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1f3c381eedfe8914b81e93070bfdb00cf86ac943) because it directly ablates encoder sharing in a GNN contrastive framework with a shared MLP projection head and local-global node-graph contrast, finding that dedicated encoders outperform a shared encoder on all eight reported benchmarks; shared accuracies were 86.2 +/- 0.6, 72.8 +/- 0.6, 79.5 +/- 0.1, 82.8 +/- 1.9, 55.9 +/- 2.2, 73.0 +/- 0.7, 50.0 +/- 0.3, and 81.3 +/- 2.0, while dedicated accuracies were 86.8 +/- 0.5, 73.3 +/- 0.5, 80.1 +/- 0.7, 89.7 +/- 1.1, 62.5 +/- 1.7, 74.2 +/- 0.7, 51.1 +/- 0.5, and 84.5 +/- 0.6, with the largest gains on graph classification from 82.8 to 89.7 and from 55.9 to 62.5; the setup used PyTorch on a single GPU, Adam with learning rate 0.001, hidden dimension 512, one GCN layer for node classification, 2000 epochs, early stopping patience 20, and precomputed structural views including adjacency and personalized PageRank, suggesting that the plan's single shared GNN for all augmented node, subgraph, and graph views may underperform when views are structurally heterogeneous, especially because the plan adds a subgraph-level view that this paper does not test. As an iterative line, this 2020 result challenges the older shared-encoder contrastive design of [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098) and is later reinforced by the 2022 view-specific GOOD-D design, although the 2021 shared-encoder success of [(Shao et al., 2021-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8d16a43dfdb9d280a8de327635a3a6c6fe1bfd86) complicates the conclusion by showing that shared encoders can work for node-graph contrast on citation datasets. The next contradiction is [(Liu et al., 2022-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/305941292b59d808af1f6646993747ba0f76f4ac), which uses view-specific GNN encoders for feature and structure views, L-layer projection networks, and node, graph, and group contrastive objectives, and reports best average rank among 12 baselines on 8 of 10 OOD dataset pairs with runner-up status on the remaining groups, a GOOD-Dsimp average rank of 2.2, five repeated runs with AUC, and strong anomaly detection on 15 TU datasets, reinforcing the idea that view-specific encoders can be useful for multi-level contrastive learning, but the task is graph-level OOD and anomaly detection rather than node classification and the excerpt lacks the numeric AUC table, making it weaker for the plan. A further contradiction is [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098) because it achieves strong self-supervised node representations without an MLP projection head, using an averaging readout and bilinear discriminator, and still outperforms DeepWalk with features and random initialization while exceeding supervised GCN on Cora and Citeseer, although it remains below supervised GCN on Pubmed and below supervised average pooling on Reddit and PPI, indicating that a projection head is not necessary for all gains and that supervised baselines can remain competitive. This is older and less direct than the dedicated-encoder evidence. Another contradiction is [(You et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/76c124786ccf4263e6403a15a8e350ac28be4e65) because it shows that graph contrastive learning without augmentation is not helpful and is often worse than training from scratch, and that gains from a single best augmentation are modest at 1.62% on NCI1, 3.15% on PROTEINS, 6.27% on COLLAB, and 1.66% on RDT-B, implying that the plan's hierarchical losses may fail if node, edge, subgraph, or global augmentations are too weak, too destructive, or poorly tuned, although this is graph-level evidence and does not test the proposed node, subgraph, and graph hierarchy. The weakest contradiction is [(Jiang et al., 2024-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8bfae1bbe1643461ad65c3c85680f2c9e4b20b3b) because its decoder-based masking objective, per-iteration sampled augmentations, and temperature-scaled cross-entropy loss are not part of the proposed plan, but the provided excerpt contains no quantitative outcomes, so it cannot strongly contradict the proposed architecture.

### Suggested Action

Most important, the plan should add a direct shared-encoder versus view-specific-encoder ablation because the strongest contradictions from [(Hassani, and Ahmadi, 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1f3c381eedfe8914b81e93070bfdb00cf86ac943) and [(Liu et al., 2022-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/305941292b59d808af1f6646993747ba0f76f4ac) show that view-specific encoders can improve multi-level contrastive learning; for each node, subgraph, and graph view, compare a shared GCN or GIN encoder, dedicated encoders, and a shared encoder with lightweight view adapters, reporting node classification accuracy and micro-F1 on Cora, Citeseer, PubMed, and Reddit in transductive, inductive, and low-label 1-10 settings, with at least 10 to 50 runs, standard deviations, and significance tests, and if dedicated encoders win, adopt view-specific encoders or adapters while retaining the shared MLP projection head. Second, the plan should ablate the MLP projection head by comparing the proposed 2-layer hidden dimension 64 head, a 1-layer head, and no projection head with readout or bilinear scoring as in [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098), while also using the projection-head and linear-probing evidence from [(Chen et al., 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7af72a461ed7cda180e7eab878efd5f35d79bbf4) to justify the head if it improves downstream accuracy. Third, the plan should make augmentation design explicit and tunable, following [(You et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/76c124786ccf4263e6403a15a8e350ac28be4e65) and [(Chen et al., 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7af72a461ed7cda180e7eab878efd5f35d79bbf4), by including no-augmentation, single-augmentation, and composed-augmentation baselines, tuning node dropping, edge perturbation, attribute masking, subgraph sampling, and virtual node addition around ratios such as 0.2, and reporting structure-preservation checks and per-augmentation ablations to avoid the failure mode where contrastive learning without useful augmentation is worse than scratch. Fourth, the plan should test the hierarchical loss decomposition by comparing node-only, subgraph-only, graph-only, node-plus-graph, and all three losses, with fixed weight sweeps and learnable alpha, beta, and gamma, because [(Shao et al., 2021-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8d16a43dfdb9d280a8de327635a3a6c6fe1bfd86) supports node-plus-graph contrast but does not validate subgraph-level contrast, and the plan's subgraph contribution is currently unproven. Fifth, the plan should broaden implementation and reproducibility details, because the proposed 2-layer hidden dimension 128 GNN and 2-layer hidden dimension 64 MLP are smaller than the 512-dimensional encoders used in [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098), [(Hassani, and Ahmadi, 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1f3c381eedfe8914b81e93070bfdb00cf86ac943), and [(Shao et al., 2021-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8d16a43dfdb9d280a8de327635a3a6c6fe1bfd86); sweep hidden dimensions 64, 128, 256, and 512, compare GCN and GIN, test 1 and 2 layers, and report Adam learning rates, batch sizes, early stopping patience, temperature tau, seeds, hardware, and for Reddit use minibatch and neighborhood sampling similar to DGI, such as 256-node minibatches and 10, 10, 25 neighbor sampling, possibly with a lower learning rate. Sixth, the plan should strengthen evaluation by adding micro-F1 for Reddit, t-SNE silhouette or other cluster metrics similar to the 0.234 silhouette reported by [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098), and include the planned baselines DGI, GRACE, MVGRL, GraphCL, GCN, GAT, and GraphSAGE with standard deviations and significance tests, while considering PPI or other zero-feature graphs if resources allow to test robustness beyond the planned datasets.

## Context: To demonstrate that the proposed framework outperforms prior approaches, the plan compares against supervised and self-supervised baselines. Method: Baseline Comparisons: We will compare against both supervised methods (GCN, GAT, GraphSAGE) and self-supervised methods (DGI, GRACE, MVGRL, GraphCL) to demonstrate the effectiveness of our hierarchical approach.

### Support

The strongest support comes from [(Wang et al., 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/347843529c352d484155aca55011734d6d946612), which benchmarks GCMAE on exactly the plan's four node-level datasets (Cora, Citeseer, PubMed, Reddit) against supervised GCN and GAT and self-supervised DGI, MVGRL, GRACE, CCA-SSG, and masked-autoencoder baselines, reporting accuracy gains of 1.7, 2.2, 2.0, and 2.1 points over the best baseline and ablations showing contrastive, adjacency reconstruction, and discrimination losses all contribute; this validates the plan's dataset choice, primary accuracy metric, and the need to compare supervised and self-supervised baselines under a common node-classification protocol. A close second is [(Zhang et al., 2021-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e7b666c5ff82321cd35cfe5af3deb026ea0d3059), which compares CCA-SSG against GCN, GAT, DGI, MVGRL, GRACE, and GCA on Cora, Citeseer, PubMed, and larger co-author/co-purchase graphs, reporting 84.2±0.4, 73.1±0.3, and 81.6±0.4 on citation networks and outperforming supervised GCN/GAT and DGI/MVGRL/GRACE on Cora and PubMed, while also showing that self-supervised methods do not uniformly dominate (e.g., below GCA and GCN on Physics), which supports a careful baseline comparison rather than a blanket superiority claim. [(Thakoor et al., unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/58d3278e45284e2910d5d972b2ec2c7ce6e34c4f) provides a strong protocol template on medium-scale graphs with 7,650 to 34,493 nodes: a 2-layer GCN encoder, frozen embeddings, L2-regularized logistic regression probe, 20 random splits, and memory measurements on a 16GB V100, comparing supervised GCN with DGI, DeepWalk, MVGRL, GRACE, GCA, and Random-Init; its BGRL results (79.98±0.10 WikiCS, 90.34±0.19 Amazon-Computers, 93.17±0.30 Amazon-Photos, 93.31±0.13 Coauthor-CS, 95.73±0.05 Coauthor-Physics) and GRACE OOM on Coauthor-Physics support the plan's linear-probe evaluation and warn that memory must be reported for GRACE. Older foundational works support the supervised baseline set: [(Kipf, and Welling, 2016-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/36eff562f65125511b5dfab68ce7f7a943c27478) establishes the 2-layer GCN semi-supervised protocol on Cora/Citeseer/PubMed with 20 labels/class, 500 validation, 1000 test, Adam, early stopping, dropout, and L2; [(Velickovic et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/33998aff64ce51df8dee45989cdca4b6b1329ec4) gives GAT reference accuracies of 83.0±0.7, 72.5±0.7, and 79.0±0.3 over 100 runs on Cora (2708 nodes), Citeseer (3327 nodes), and PubMed (19717 nodes), plus inductive PPI settings; [(Hamilton et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6b7d6e6416343b2a122f8416e69059ce919026ef) supports GraphSAGE as an inductive baseline with K=2, S1=25/S2=10, micro-F1 results on citation and Reddit (232,965 posts), and shows supervised GraphSAGE-pool outperforms DeepWalk+features by 19.7% and 37.2%. Self-supervised baseline support includes [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098) for DGI (82.3±0.6 Cora, 71.8±0.7 Citeseer, 76.8±0.6 PubMed, 0.940±0.001 Reddit, 0.638±0.002 PPI) and linear probing; [(Zhu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0d67d3ddca1c4e370eaf1e99ec674f612c39c66c) for GCA's adaptive topology/attribute augmentation, node-level InfoNCE, two-layer GCN/projection, and overlap with GCN/GAT/DGI/MVGRL; [(You et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/76c124786ccf4263e6403a15a8e350ac28be4e65) for GraphCL's augmentation toolkit and evidence that no augmentation is not helpful while single/composed augmentations yield 1.62-6.27 gains on graph datasets; [(Jin et al., 2021-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/914b71998c8fd65d65bf7cea417a13a546e28acb) for MERIT's GCN/GAT/DGI/GRACE/MVGRL comparison with 10-run linear probing; [(Xu et al., 2021-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/229cf7592e45193ff6d3fed5ef13dae511f7a0d5) for including GraphSAGE and GraphCL in broader baselines; [(Mao et al., 2021-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/44c9137f30e265ca43f2212eace8449388702c0a) for GCN/GraphSAGE/GAT/DGI/GRACE comparisons with 5-seed Macro-F1/AUC; [(Chen et al., 2023-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/994d4d5803be2835cbb345c928c0c6e95bd5ac7e) for node classification/clustering/link prediction baselines and Wilcoxon p=9.77e-4; and [(Lee et al., 2021-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2812cc5b514a5c87e29de2e051b3ebadc021897d) for scalable augmentation-free comparison against DGI/MVGRL/GRACE/GCA/BGRL. Additional protocol support comes from [(Salehi, and Davulcu, 2019-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cd33eba5b51c871d0c7610d01b6a357e7f492ad5), which uses Cora/Citeseer/PubMed, GCN/GAT/GraphSAGE/DGI baselines, ablation, and t-SNE; [(Peng et al., 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/896f2aac3d9f32cd676442de2f88e8a802d8c349), which keeps a fixed GCN encoder and compares DGI/EP-B under a single Tesla P40, Adam, early stopping, and Reddit/PPI subsampling; [(Wu et al., 2020-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6827a46b59672e2d9fa4163e62216915fc8e8b8b), which compares GCN/GraphSAGE against embedding and adaptation baselines; and [(Hou et al., 2022-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b161c4aaddd2983a9d4d5a240bd5ffa84b36c4e7), which positions masked autoencoders against contrastive methods. Iteratively, random-walk methods [(Perozzi et al., 2014-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/fff114cbba4f3ba900f33da574283e3de7f26c83) and [(Grover, and Leskovec, 2016-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/36ee2c8bd605afd48035d15fdc6b8c8842363376) established unlabeled graph representation baselines, with the latter reporting p<0.01 over 10 seeds; GNN supervised baselines [(Kipf, and Welling, 2016-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/36eff562f65125511b5dfab68ce7f7a943c27478), [(Hamilton et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6b7d6e6416343b2a122f8416e69059ce919026ef), and [(Velickovic et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/33998aff64ce51df8dee45989cdca4b6b1329ec4) raised the supervised bar, DGI [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098) introduced local-global self-supervision, and newer contrastive/adaptive methods [(Zhu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0d67d3ddca1c4e370eaf1e99ec674f612c39c66c), [(You et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/76c124786ccf4263e6403a15a8e350ac28be4e65), [(Hassani, and Ahmadi, 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1f3c381eedfe8914b81e93070bfdb00cf86ac943), [(Thakoor et al., unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/58d3278e45284e2910d5d972b2ec2c7ce6e34c4f), [(Zhang et al., 2021-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e7b666c5ff82321cd35cfe5af3deb026ea0d3059), [(You et al., 2021-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0e595fe9e18e422d0569a3faad7a32ba3c576e5a), and [(Wang et al., 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/347843529c352d484155aca55011734d6d946612) show that the planned baseline set is a reasonable but not exhaustive snapshot of the field.

### Contradictions

The strongest contradiction to the baseline-comparison method is that GraphCL, listed as a self-supervised baseline, is primarily a graph-level classification pretraining method, not a node-level node-classification baseline; [(You et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/76c124786ccf4263e6403a15a8e350ac28be4e65) reports graph classification gains (1.62% NCI1, 3.15% PROTEINS, 6.27% COLLAB, 1.66% RDT-B) and augmentation sensitivity, but does not provide the plan's Cora/Citeseer/PubMed/Reddit node-classification protocol, low-label regime, or supervised GCN/GAT/GraphSAGE comparison, so using it directly may conflate graph-level and node-level objectives. A second strong contradiction is memory and scalability: [(Thakoor et al., unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/58d3278e45284e2910d5d972b2ec2c7ce6e34c4f) shows GRACE uses 11.78GB on Coauthor-CS and OOM on Coauthor-Physics on a 16GB V100, while [(Zhang et al., 2021-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e7b666c5ff82321cd35cfe5af3deb026ea0d3059) notes GRACE and GCA OOM on Coauthor-Physics with 16GB and require 32GB, and [(Lee et al., 2021-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2812cc5b514a5c87e29de2e051b3ebadc021897d) reports instance-discrimination methods GRACE/GCA are memory-consuming and fail on large datasets; this contradicts an assumption that GRACE can be run unchanged on Reddit or larger graphs. Third, the low-label 1-10 regime is not supported by most baseline comparisons: [(Kipf, and Welling, 2016-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/36eff562f65125511b5dfab68ce7f7a943c27478), [(Velickovic et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/33998aff64ce51df8dee45989cdca4b6b1329ec4), [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098), [(Zhu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0d67d3ddca1c4e370eaf1e99ec674f612c39c66c), [(Zhang et al., 2021-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e7b666c5ff82321cd35cfe5af3deb026ea0d3059), and [(Wang et al., 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/347843529c352d484155aca55011734d6d946612) use standard 20-label or full unsupervised protocols, while only [(You et al., 2021-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0e595fe9e18e422d0569a3faad7a32ba3c576e5a) directly tests 1% and 10% labels but on graph-level TUDataset/ogbg-ppa, not node classification on the plan's datasets. Fourth, the plan's shared GNN encoder is contradicted by [(Hassani, and Ahmadi, 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1f3c381eedfe8914b81e93070bfdb00cf86ac943), which found dedicated per-view encoders outperformed a shared encoder (86.8±0.5/73.3±0.5/80.1±0.7 vs 86.2±0.6/72.8±0.6/79.5±0.1) and that adding more views or multi-scale/hybrid/ensemble objectives did not consistently improve performance; this suggests the hierarchical shared-encoder design may need ablation. Fifth, several works show DGI/GRACE are not uniformly strong: [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098) reports DGI below supervised GCN on PubMed (76.8±0.6 vs 79.0) and below supervised average pooling on Reddit/PPI, and [(Mao et al., 2021-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/44c9137f30e265ca43f2212eace8449388702c0a) finds DGI/GRACE perform poorly in cross-domain adaptation because they optimize representation quality rather than target-domain discriminability; therefore the plan should not expect these baselines to be competitive in every setting. Sixth, capacity and protocol inconsistencies weaken direct comparability: many cited self-supervised methods use hidden dimension 512 (e.g., [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098), [(Peng et al., 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/896f2aac3d9f32cd676442de2f88e8a802d8c349), [(Jin et al., 2021-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/914b71998c8fd65d65bf7cea417a13a546e28acb), and [(Hassani, and Ahmadi, 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1f3c381eedfe8914b81e93070bfdb00cf86ac943)), while the plan specifies a 2-layer GNN hidden dimension 128 and MLP hidden dimension 64; additionally, [(Hamilton et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6b7d6e6416343b2a122f8416e69059ce919026ef) uses micro-F1 for inductive GraphSAGE, [(Wang et al., 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/347843529c352d484155aca55011734d6d946612) uses LIBSVM 5-fold cross-validation and an RTX 4090, [(Mao et al., 2021-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/44c9137f30e265ca43f2212eace8449388702c0a) uses Macro-F1/Macro-AUC over 5 seeds, and [(Thakoor et al., unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/58d3278e45284e2910d5d972b2ec2c7ce6e34c4f) uses 20 random splits and linear logistic regression, so the plan must standardize splits, probes, metrics, and hardware. Seventh, augmentation assumptions are not guaranteed: [(Zhu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0d67d3ddca1c4e370eaf1e99ec674f612c39c66c) shows GCA degrades when augmentation probabilities are too large, especially above 0.5, and edge removal at 0.9 isolates nodes, while [(You et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/76c124786ccf4263e6403a15a8e350ac28be4e65) shows augmentation type/composition is dataset-specific; thus a fixed hierarchical augmentation schedule may underperform. Eighth, [(Wang et al., 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/347843529c352d484155aca55011734d6d946612) shows that removing adjacency reconstruction caused the largest ablation drops (3.2 points on Cora, 4.3 on Citeseer, 2.4 on PubMed), suggesting that a purely contrastive hierarchical objective may be incomplete without structural reconstruction. Finally, older random-walk baselines [(Perozzi et al., 2014-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/fff114cbba4f3ba900f33da574283e3de7f26c83) and [(Grover, and Leskovec, 2016-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/36ee2c8bd605afd48035d15fdc6b8c8842363376) are not GNN or contrastive and provide only weak support for the planned baseline set.

### Suggested Action

Most importantly, standardize the baseline comparison protocol before claiming superiority: use the same Cora/Citeseer/PubMed/Reddit splits, transductive and inductive settings, 1-10 labels per class, primary accuracy plus macro/micro F1, at least 10 random seeds, mean±std, paired statistical tests, and explicit hardware/memory/runtime, following the linear-probe and split templates in [(Thakoor et al., unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/58d3278e45284e2910d5d972b2ec2c7ce6e34c4f), [(Wang et al., 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/347843529c352d484155aca55011734d6d946612), and [(Zhang et al., 2021-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e7b666c5ff82321cd35cfe5af3deb026ea0d3059). Next, resolve the GraphCL mismatch by either implementing a node-level GraphCL adaptation with a clear pretraining-to-node-probe protocol or replacing/augmenting it with node-level self-supervised baselines such as BGRL, GCA, CCA-SSG, AFGRL, and GCMAE, because [(You et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/76c124786ccf4263e6403a15a8e350ac28be4e65) is graph-level and its augmentation evidence is dataset-specific. Then, add explicit low-label experiments with 1, 2, 5, and 10 labels per class, training supervised GCN/GAT/GraphSAGE from scratch and evaluating self-supervised methods via linear probing and fine-tuning, because most cited baseline comparisons use 20-label or full unsupervised protocols while only [(You et al., 2021-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0e595fe9e18e422d0569a3faad7a32ba3c576e5a) directly tests 1% and 10% labels in a graph-level setting. Also, address GRACE scalability by reporting memory, using 24/32GB GPUs, negative subsampling, minibatch/subsampling as in [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098) and [(Peng et al., 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/896f2aac3d9f32cd676442de2f88e8a802d8c349), and including scalable alternatives such as BGRL or AFGRL based on [(Thakoor et al., unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/58d3278e45284e2910d5d972b2ec2c7ce6e34c4f) and [(Lee et al., 2021-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2812cc5b514a5c87e29de2e051b3ebadc021897d). Then, ablate the shared encoder assumption and encoder capacity by testing shared versus dedicated per-view encoders, separate projection heads, and hidden dimensions 128 versus 512, because [(Hassani, and Ahmadi, 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1f3c381eedfe8914b81e93070bfdb00cf86ac943) found dedicated encoders outperformed a shared encoder and many baselines use 512. Expand the baseline set beyond the four named self-supervised methods to include BGRL, GCA, CCA-SSG, AFGRL, GraphMAE, and GCMAE, since [(Zhang et al., 2021-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e7b666c5ff82321cd35cfe5af3deb026ea0d3059), [(Lee et al., 2021-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2812cc5b514a5c87e29de2e051b3ebadc021897d), [(Hou et al., 2022-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b161c4aaddd2983a9d4d5a240bd5ffa84b36c4e7), and [(Wang et al., 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/347843529c352d484155aca55011734d6d946612) show that recent self-supervised and masked-autoencoder baselines are now standard. Tune the adaptive augmentation strategy on validation data, avoiding high edge-removal probabilities that isolate nodes as warned by [(Zhu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0d67d3ddca1c4e370eaf1e99ec674f612c39c66c), and ablate node dropping, edge perturbation, attribute masking, subgraph sampling, and virtual-node addition separately. Finally, report t-SNE and quantitative embedding diagnostics such as silhouette score, NMI, ARI, and link-prediction AUC, following [(Hassani, and Ahmadi, 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1f3c381eedfe8914b81e93070bfdb00cf86ac943), [(Chen et al., 2023-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/994d4d5803be2835cbb345c928c0c6e95bd5ac7e), and [(Wang et al., 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/347843529c352d484155aca55011734d6d946612), while documenting the 2-layer GNN hidden dimension 128, MLP projection hidden dimension 64, Adam hyperparameters, temperature, loss weights, epochs, and GPU model to ensure reproducibility.

## Context: To assess generalization, the plan evaluates in both transductive and inductive settings. Method: Experimental Settings: We will assess performance in both transductive and inductive settings. For transductive evaluation, the model will have access to the entire graph structure during training. For inductive evaluation, we will train on subgraphs and test on unseen nodes to assess generalization capability.

### Support

The strongest support comes from [(Li et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0974d431b08d94436a99e939aada96a8535b338b), which explicitly separates transductive Cora, Citeseer, and PubMed from inductive Reddit and PPI, trains a node-centered subgraph contrastive model, and reports CV accuracy 84.7 ± 0.3 on Cora, 73.8 ± 0.5 on Citeseer, 81.5 ± 0.4 on PubMed, and micro-averaged F1 95.8 ± 0.1 on Reddit and 67.1 ± 0.2 on PPI, outperforming DGI, GMI, GRACE, MVGRL, GCN, and FastGCN under a one-layer GCN hidden dimension 512, Adam lr 0.001 except 1e-4 for Reddit, early stopping patience 20 or fixed 150/20 epochs, and four RTX 3090 GPUs; this directly validates the plan's inductive subgraph training and unseen-node evaluation, and shows that newer subgraph contrastive work can close or reverse the older inductive gap. Next, [(Xie et al., 2022-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/812d3ed6d4fef56f090d6528c0a171919aef1221) supports the same transductive/inductive split by training LaGraph only on training graphs for inductive PPI, Reddit, and Flickr and probing with regularized logistic regression, although the excerpt lacks numeric results. [(Pina, and Vilaplana, 2023-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cdfd03f223ad30296fd8983af9ed160e383932c4) further supports inductive batched subgraph training on ogbn-products and PPI, noting that full-subgraph methods such as GraphMAE can fail out-of-memory, which justifies the plan's subgraph-based inductive arm. The foundational protocol is in [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098), which defines transductive citation evaluation and inductive Reddit/PPI unseen-node evaluation, uses 512-dimensional encoders, neighbor subsampling 10/10/25 for Reddit, 50 runs, and t-SNE silhouette 0.234 versus 0.158, supporting both the plan's split design and t-SNE analysis. [(Peng et al., 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/896f2aac3d9f32cd676442de2f88e8a802d8c349) supports fair objective comparison with fixed encoder and splits, using a single Tesla P40, Adam lr 0.001, 256-node minibatches, 8/5 neighbor sampling, and 50-run accuracy/F1. [(Zhu et al., 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4bf76588122827157c43a59e656dccc6b6a22e90) supports the plan's baseline set and inductive Reddit neighbor sampling 30/25/20, PPI same-graph negatives, and frozen l2 logistic regression. [(Zhu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0d67d3ddca1c4e370eaf1e99ec674f612c39c66c) supports transductive linear probing on five medium graphs with 20 random splits and augmentation sensitivity. [(Thakoor et al., unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/58d3278e45284e2910d5d972b2ec2c7ce6e34c4f) and [(Thakoor et al., 2021-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b38fcf2aba8b173ba11ada830b7038f496643893) support the frozen linear-probe benchmark on WikiCS, Amazon-Computers, Amazon-Photos, Coauthor-CS, and Coauthor-Physics, with 20 splits, 16GB V100 memory, and 2-10x lower memory than GRACE, though their inductive condition is subsampled neighborhoods rather than explicit unseen nodes. [(Zhang et al., 2021-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e7b666c5ff82321cd35cfe5af3deb026ea0d3059) supports transductive node classification with CCA and reports 84.2 ± 0.4 Cora, 73.1 ± 0.3 Citeseer, 81.6 ± 0.4 PubMed, 88.74 ± 0.28 Computer, 93.14 ± 0.14 Photo, 93.31 ± 0.22 CS, and 95.38 ± 0.06 Physics. Indirect support for the plan's 2-layer MLP projection head and contrastive pretraining followed by linear probing comes from [(Chen et al., 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7af72a461ed7cda180e7eab878efd5f35d79bbf4), which shows augmentation composition matters with crop plus color distortion scoring 56.3 in linear evaluation, [(Grill et al., 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/38f93092ece8eee9771e61c1edaf11b1293cae1b), which uses an online predictor and slowly updated target with a two-layer MLP projection head, and [(Chen, and He, 2020-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0e23d2f14e7e56e81538f4a63e11689d8ac1eb9d), which shows a predictor and stop-gradient can improve negative-free self-supervised learning; however, these are ImageNet results and do not validate graph transductive/inductive splits. [(Liu et al., 2021-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e259ee075998eedc0b0c91c17769bf9dffeba46f) conceptually supports same-scale and cross-scale node/subgraph/graph contrast, but provides no quantitative benchmark. Finally, [(Huo et al., 2022-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e30b0536a8b12450ba4b34280308d5e30c70e816) and [(Wang et al., 2024-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3a2caaf04f08d838a0e111343490bdc06a3229f6) indirectly support low-label and inductive robustness by varying training ratios and evaluating an inductive set under scarce labels, although they do not provide the plan's 1-10 labels per class or hierarchical losses.

### Contradictions

The strongest contradiction is quantitative evidence that inductive self-supervised graph learning can remain far below supervised baselines, especially on sparse multi-graph PPI: [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098) reports DGI micro-averaged F1 0.638 ± 0.002 on PPI versus supervised average-pooling 0.969 ± 0.002, and 0.940 ± 0.001 on Reddit versus supervised 0.958 ± 0.001, using 50 runs and subsampling because Reddit did not fit in GPU memory; this cautions the plan's claim that hierarchical contrastive learning will consistently outperform supervised GCN, GAT, and GraphSAGE in inductive settings. [(Zhu et al., 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4bf76588122827157c43a59e656dccc6b6a22e90) similarly reports that GRACE remains below supervised models and attributes PPI difficulty to over 40 percent all-zero node features, which makes feature-shuffling corruption less informative; this contradicts the assumption that adaptive feature masking alone will solve inductive generalization. The plan's fixed 2-layer GNN hidden dimension 128 is also contradicted by the implementation evidence for large inductive Reddit: [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098), [(Zhu et al., 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4bf76588122827157c43a59e656dccc6b6a22e90), and [(Li et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0974d431b08d94436a99e939aada96a8535b338b) use three-layer GraphSAGE or GCN encoders with 512 hidden features and neighbor sampling for Reddit, suggesting that a 2-layer 128-dimensional encoder may be underpowered for the plan's largest dataset. The low-label 1-10 regime is under-evidenced: most cited works use standard splits or full labels for linear probes, while only [(Wang et al., 2024-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3a2caaf04f08d838a0e111343490bdc06a3229f6) and [(Huo et al., 2022-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e30b0536a8b12450ba4b34280308d5e30c70e816) mention scarce labels or varying training ratios without reporting the plan's exact 1-10 labels per class, baselines, or significance. Several works also do not directly validate the inductive arm: [(Zhu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0d67d3ddca1c4e370eaf1e99ec674f612c39c66c) is transductive only and shows degradation when augmentation probabilities exceed 0.5, [(Thakoor et al., unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/58d3278e45284e2910d5d972b2ec2c7ce6e34c4f) and [(Thakoor et al., 2021-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b38fcf2aba8b173ba11ada830b7038f496643893) use subsampled-neighborhood inductive-style training rather than explicit unseen nodes, and [(Zhang et al., 2021-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e7b666c5ff82321cd35cfe5af3deb026ea0d3059) is transductive only. [(Park et al., 2019-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ebf27510ccbe7a5e5edab92ddc135d2552c8df00) warns that attention over relation types can become bimodal and over-weight a single view, which is analogous to learnable loss weights collapsing to one hierarchical scale. Finally, most related work reports mean and standard deviation over 20 or 50 runs but not formal statistical significance, so the plan's t-SNE-only qualitative analysis and lack of significance testing would be weaker than the evidence standard.

### Suggested Action

The most important action is to make the inductive protocol explicit and benchmark-compatible: train only on subgraphs or training nodes, hold out unseen nodes or graphs, use standard splits, and report micro-averaged F1 for multi-label inductive datasets such as Reddit and, if feasible, PPI or Flickr, following [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098), [(Zhu et al., 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4bf76588122827157c43a59e656dccc6b6a22e90), [(Li et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0974d431b08d94436a99e939aada96a8535b338b), and [(Xie et al., 2022-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/812d3ed6d4fef56f090d6528c0a171919aef1221); for Reddit, use neighbor sampling and document batch size, hops, and memory. Second, scale the encoder for Reddit or justify the 2-layer hidden 128 choice with an architecture ablation, because [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098), [(Zhu et al., 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4bf76588122827157c43a59e656dccc6b6a22e90), and [(Li et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0974d431b08d94436a99e939aada96a8535b338b) use 512-dimensional three-layer encoders for large inductive graphs. Third, implement the low-label 1-10 regime with fixed random splits, at least 20 runs, and compare frozen linear probes plus end-to-end fine-tuning against supervised GCN, GAT, GraphSAGE, and self-supervised DGI, GRACE, GCA, MVGRL, and GraphCL, because [(Wang et al., 2024-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3a2caaf04f08d838a0e111343490bdc06a3229f6) and [(Huo et al., 2022-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e30b0536a8b12450ba4b34280308d5e30c70e816) only indirectly support scarce-label evaluation. Fourth, treat learnable loss weights as a hypothesis to test rather than a default: include fixed-weight baselines, ablate node, subgraph, and graph losses, and run sensitivity analyses for tau and alpha beta gamma, informed by [(Zhu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0d67d3ddca1c4e370eaf1e99ec674f612c39c66c) augmentation probability degradation above 0.5, [(Li et al., 2023-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/112964aa0caee373cb8c87ab73d1e7d39124cd8b) temperature around 0.1 and independence around 0.1, [(Zhu et al., 2023-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7b56cac048a947d45aefc1da3c5c31479afda463) CMI coefficient 0.1 with 100 prototypes, and [(Park et al., 2019-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ebf27510ccbe7a5e5edab92ddc135d2552c8df00) evidence that over-weighting one view can be detrimental. Fifth, design adaptive augmentation to preserve structure: combine node dropping, edge perturbation, feature masking, and k-hop subgraph sampling, but avoid probabilities that isolate nodes, following [(Zhu et al., 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4bf76588122827157c43a59e656dccc6b6a22e90) evidence that corrupting both topology and features is necessary and [(You et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/76c124786ccf4263e6403a15a8e350ac28be4e65) evidence that augmentation without contrastive learning can be unhelpful. Sixth, strengthen evaluation by reporting mean and standard deviation over at least 20 or 50 runs, paired statistical tests or bootstrap confidence intervals, and quantitative embedding metrics such as silhouette, ARI, or NMI alongside t-SNE, as in [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098) and [(Hassani, and Ahmadi, 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1f3c381eedfe8914b81e93070bfdb00cf86ac943). Finally, re-run all baselines under identical splits, encoder, projection head, and linear-probe settings rather than relying on previously reported numbers, and adapt GraphCL from graph-level to node-level or report it separately, because [(Thakoor et al., 2021-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b38fcf2aba8b173ba11ada830b7038f496643893) and [(Peng et al., 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/896f2aac3d9f32cd676442de2f88e8a802d8c349) show that fair comparison requires fixed encoder, splits, and training details.

## Context: To measure performance consistently and fairly, the plan uses standard benchmark splits and node classification accuracy. Method: Evaluation Protocols:
- Standard benchmark splits for fair comparison with existing methods
- Node classification accuracy as the primary evaluation metric

### Support

Strongest support for the proposed evaluation protocol comes from the canonical transductive citation-network setup in [(Kipf, and Welling, 2016-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/36eff562f65125511b5dfab68ce7f7a943c27478), which uses 20 labeled nodes per class, 500 validation nodes, and 1000 test nodes on Cora, Citeseer, and PubMed, evaluates node classification accuracy, and trains with Adam at learning rate 0.01, at most 200 epochs, early stopping patience 10, Glorot initialization, row-normalized features, and tuned dropout, L2, and hidden units. This protocol is directly confirmed by [(Velickovic et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/33998aff64ce51df8dee45989cdca4b6b1329ec4), which uses the same 20/500/1000 splits and reports test-node accuracy over 100 runs, e.g., GAT 83.0 ± 0.7% on Cora, 72.5 ± 0.7% on Citeseer, and 79.0 ± 0.3% on Pubmed, with a two-layer 8-head attention model, ELU, L2 0.0005, dropout 0.6, Adam learning rate 0.01 or 0.005, and patience 100. Strong self-supervised support follows from [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098), which trains DGI without labels, uses a one-layer GCN with PReLU and 512 or 256 features, corrupts node features, evaluates with a linear logistic regression probe, and reports 82.3 ± 0.6% Cora, 71.8 ± 0.7% Citeseer, and 76.8 ± 0.6% PubMed over 50 runs. [(Hassani, and Ahmadi, 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1f3c381eedfe8914b81e93070bfdb00cf86ac943) further supports the plan by freezing self-supervised node and graph representations, training a linear classifier, and reporting 86.8 ± 0.5, 73.3 ± 0.5, and 80.1 ± 0.7 over 50 runs with a 1-layer GCN, 2000 epochs, Adam learning rate 0.001, patience 20, hidden dimension 512, and adjacency/PPR structural views. [(Zhang et al., 2021-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e7b666c5ff82321cd35cfe5af3deb026ea0d3059) provides quantitative public-split node accuracy, e.g., 84.2 ± 0.4 Cora, 73.1 ± 0.3 Citeseer, and 81.6 ± 0.4 Pubmed, and extends the protocol to Amazon Computer, Amazon Photo, Coauthor CS, and Coauthor Physics with 1:1:9 splits. [(Zhu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0d67d3ddca1c4e370eaf1e99ec674f612c39c66c) supports transductive node-classification accuracy with a two-layer GCN, two-layer MLP projection head, symmetric InfoNCE loss, l2-regularized logistic regression, and 20-run averaging on benchmark-style datasets. Weaker but relevant support includes [(Shi et al., 2023-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/74b70618fe4bf5a163702581a655bd8fe3fe5ea1), which uses standard citation and e-commerce node benchmarks, GCN/GAT encoders, logistic regression, and accuracy over five runs; [(Wu et al., 2021-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2d00798b8a7d979c925901e9faa5fe4360030ca2), which explicitly defines node classification accuracy as a standard metric; [(Yang et al., 2016-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3d846cb01f6a975554035d2210b578ca61344b22), which uses labeled, unlabeled, and test benchmark splits; and [(Kingma, and Ba, 2014-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a6cb366736791bcccc5c8639de5a8f9636bf87e8), which indirectly supports the plan's Adam optimizer choice. The weakest support is analogous benchmark-accuracy evidence from [(Sun et al., 2019-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2fb59ebe271d6b007bb0429c1701fd1004782d1b), [(He et al., 2019-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/add2f205338d70e10ce5e686df4a690e2851bdfc), [(Tian et al., 2020-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/feb1d269982f851f0b6c23dd495f77bc0e458baf), and [(You et al., 2021-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0e595fe9e18e422d0569a3faad7a32ba3c576e5a), which use standard splits, repeated runs, and accuracy or rank metrics but are graph-level or image-domain rather than node classification.

### Contradictions

Strongest contradiction is that the plan's primary node classification accuracy may be the wrong or incomplete metric for Reddit and inductive multi-label evaluation. [(Hamilton et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6b7d6e6416343b2a122f8416e69059ce919026ef) evaluates Reddit inductively with a temporal split, first 20 days of September 2014 for training, remaining days for testing, 30% of the test period for validation, 232,965 posts, average degree 492, 300-dimensional GloVe features, and reports micro-averaged F1 rather than accuracy, e.g., GraphSAGE-pool 0.892 unsupervised and 0.948 supervised. [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098) similarly reports micro-averaged F1 for inductive Reddit, 0.940 ± 0.001, and PPI, 0.638 ± 0.002, not ordinary node accuracy, so the plan should not assume accuracy alone is comparable for Reddit. A second major contradiction is that the proposed low-label regime of 1-10 labeled nodes per class is not directly supported by the strongest standard-split evidence: [(Kipf, and Welling, 2016-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/36eff562f65125511b5dfab68ce7f7a943c27478) uses 20 labels per class on citation networks, [(Velickovic et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/33998aff64ce51df8dee45989cdca4b6b1329ec4) also uses 20 labels per class, and [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098), [(Hassani, and Ahmadi, 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1f3c381eedfe8914b81e93070bfdb00cf86ac943), and [(Zhu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0d67d3ddca1c4e370eaf1e99ec674f612c39c66c) do not report 1-10 label experiments, so the plan's scarce-label claims require a separate validation protocol. [(Liu et al., 2023-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d11a500d3f0dec27f0113d0f9a0893542d55ea0c) partially supports low-label accuracy but uses class-disjoint 2-way or 5-way 1-shot and 5-shot splits, 20 runs, and a meta-task construction method, not the plan's transductive or inductive node-level splits, so it cannot be treated as direct evidence that the standard transductive split remains fair under 1-10 labels. Additional indirect contradictions concern evaluation completeness and implementation constraints: [(You et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/76c124786ccf4263e6403a15a8e350ac28be4e65) shows that graph contrastive learning without augmentation was not helpful and reports graph-level classification gains of 1.62% on NCI1, 3.15% on PROTEINS, 6.27% on COLLAB, and 1.66% on RDT-B, suggesting that a single node-accuracy metric may not capture whether the hierarchical contrastive objective is effective; [(Zhu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0d67d3ddca1c4e370eaf1e99ec674f612c39c66c) reports OOM on a 32GB GPU for some baselines and degradation when edge perturbation probability p_e = 0.9 creates isolated nodes, implying that the plan must specify memory and augmentation constraints for Reddit; [(Pina, and Vilaplana, 2023-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cdfd03f223ad30296fd8983af9ed160e383932c4) uses inductive linear evaluation but reports micro-average F-score for PPI and excludes methods not compatible with batched inductive training, reinforcing that inductive evaluation requires task-specific metrics and implementation constraints; and [(Xu et al., 2018-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/62ed9bf1d83c8db1f9cbf92ea2f57ea90ef683d9), although graph-level, shows that GNN aggregation and architecture choices affect accuracy, so the plan should not assume that a fixed 2-layer GCN or GIN encoder with hidden dimension 128 is sufficient without ablations.

### Suggested Action

Most important, define exact standard splits and metrics per dataset: for Cora, Citeseer, and PubMed use the 20/500/1000 transductive split from [(Kipf, and Welling, 2016-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/36eff562f65125511b5dfab68ce7f7a943c27478) and [(Velickovic et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/33998aff64ce51df8dee45989cdca4b6b1329ec4), with node classification accuracy as the primary metric; for Reddit use the inductive temporal split from [(Hamilton et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6b7d6e6416343b2a122f8416e69059ce919026ef) or [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098) and report micro-averaged F1 as the primary or co-primary metric, with accuracy only as a secondary single-label proxy if label semantics allow. Second, design the low-label 1-10 experiment as a separate protocol: keep the standard test and validation sets fixed, sample 1-10 labeled nodes per class repeatedly, e.g., 20-100 random label subsets, report mean and standard deviation of accuracy and F1, and compare against the same baselines; use [(Liu et al., 2023-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d11a500d3f0dec27f0113d0f9a0893542d55ea0c) only as an additional class-disjoint few-shot sanity check, not as the main transductive or inductive evidence. Third, adopt a linear-probe evaluation for self-supervised methods: freeze the hierarchical encoder, train an l2-regularized logistic regression or simple linear classifier on the labeled split, tune regularization on validation, optionally standardize or L2-normalize embeddings, and report test accuracy or F1 over at least 20-50 runs, following [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098), [(Hassani, and Ahmadi, 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1f3c381eedfe8914b81e93070bfdb00cf86ac943), [(Zhu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0d67d3ddca1c4e370eaf1e99ec674f612c39c66c), and [(Shi et al., 2023-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/74b70618fe4bf5a163702581a655bd8fe3fe5ea1). Fourth, report statistical reliability and reproducibility: mean and standard deviation over multiple seeds, random label subsets, and initialization, with confidence intervals or paired significance tests, because [(Velickovic et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/33998aff64ce51df8dee45989cdca4b6b1329ec4) uses 100 runs, [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098) and [(Hassani, and Ahmadi, 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1f3c381eedfe8914b81e93070bfdb00cf86ac943) use 50 runs, and [(Zhu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0d67d3ddca1c4e370eaf1e99ec674f612c39c66c) uses 20 runs. Fifth, specify inductive implementation details for Reddit and unseen nodes: neighborhood sampling, batch size, training/validation/test node sets, memory limits, hardware, Adam learning rate, early stopping patience, hidden dimension, projection head, temperature τ, and loss weights α, β, γ, using [(Hamilton et al., 2017-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6b7d6e6416343b2a122f8416e69059ce919026ef), [(Velickovic et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/967a21a111757d6af7f7a25ca7ea2bdf6d505098), and [(Pina, and Vilaplana, 2023-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cdfd03f223ad30296fd8983af9ed160e383932c4) as templates. Sixth, add ablations and secondary metrics for hierarchical contrastive learning: report node-level, subgraph-level, and graph-level loss removals, augmentation sensitivity, and t-SNE only as qualitative evidence, while using accuracy or F1 as quantitative metrics, and compare against GCN, GAT, GraphSAGE, DGI, GRACE, GCA, MVGRL, and GraphCL under identical splits, noting that [(You et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/76c124786ccf4263e6403a15a8e350ac28be4e65) is graph-level and should be included only if a node-level variant or appendix protocol is available.

## Context: To make the experiments reproducible and tune the hierarchical objective, the plan specifies encoder/projection dimensions and hyperparameters. Method: Implementation Details: We will use a 2-layer GNN encoder with hidden dimension 128, a 2-layer MLP projection head with hidden dimension 64, and optimize using Adam optimizer. We plan to tune hyperparameters including temperature parameter τ and loss weighting coefficients α, β, γ through validation.

### Support

The strongest support for the proposed implementation details and hierarchical objective comes from [(Shao et al., 2021-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8d16a43dfdb9d280a8de327635a3a6c6fe1bfd86), which uses a shared GNN encoder, graph pooling, shared MLP projection heads, node-level and graph-level contrastive losses, Adam, Xavier initialization, 2000 training epochs, early stopping with patience 20, and logistic regression evaluation; it reports Cora outperforming GCA by 4% and GMI by 1.4%, Cornell outperforming MVGRL by 4.7% and GCA by 13.4%, and ablations show combined node-level and graph-level contrastive outperforms node-only and graph-only variants, directly supporting the plan's composite hierarchical loss, projection head, and Adam-based training. Next, [(Sun et al., 2019-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2fb59ebe271d6b007bb0429c1701fd1004782d1b) supports multi-scale contrastive learning by contrasting global graph embeddings with local patch or node embeddings, and its QM9 setup with 130,462 molecules, 5,000 labeled, 10,000 validation, and 10,000 test samples shows that InfoGraph* improves over supervised on all 12 targets and is best on 11 targets, motivating validation-based tuning of loss weights and temperature in the plan. [(You et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/76c124786ccf4263e6403a15a8e350ac28be4e65) supports the plan's augmentation strategy with node dropping, edge perturbation, attribute masking, and random-walk subgraph sampling at a default ratio of 0.2, reporting gains over training from scratch of 1.62% on NCI1, 3.15% on PROTEINS, 6.27% on COLLAB, and 1.66% on RDT-B. [(Zhang et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b16b93061acfd1fd0280cb7f494acdc136325d49) supports contrastive feature augmentation and the need to choose augmentations carefully, finding random projection outperforms random row selection, random noise, and SVD-based sketching and that feature augmentation improves more than topology augmentation. [(Agarwal et al., 2021-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/21b6b07688cf4531b2d7854064e2de5868e2ec4f) supports a projection or transformation step and a composite regularized objective, with lambda tuned through validation, and reports average stability and fairness improvements of 60.87% and 92.01% over unmodified GNNs on German credit, Recidivism, and Credit defaulter graphs. [(Han et al., 2022-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/62f82e0a3271880ca81c7198cad80305bb3719a7) supports node-level contrast and DropEdge in sparse e-commerce bipartite graphs with 70/10/20 edge splits and 100 negatives per positive, showing SSL increases Mean Average Distance and reduces over-smoothing more than DropEdge. Finally, [(Cen et al., 2019-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9ef3c30d0096e1668918f66d9f065e64455bde30) provides weaker but relevant support for a two-layer GNN encoder on citation networks such as Cora with 2,708 nodes and 1,433 features, Citeseer with 3,327 nodes and 3,703 features, Wiki with 2,405 nodes and 4,973 features, and Pubmed with 19,717 nodes and 500 features, though it is reconstruction-based and lacks quantitative outcomes.

### Contradictions

The most direct contradiction is that contrastive graph learning is not uniformly beneficial without carefully chosen augmentations: [(You et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/76c124786ccf4263e6403a15a8e350ac28be4e65) reports that without augmentation graph contrastive learning can be worse than training from scratch, and that augmentation benefits are dataset-specific, with edge perturbation helping social networks but hurting some biochemical molecules, which challenges the plan's fixed use of node dropping, edge perturbation, feature masking, subgraph sampling, and global augmentations without dataset-specific validation. [(Shao et al., 2021-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8d16a43dfdb9d280a8de327635a3a6c6fe1bfd86) also contradicts an overreliance on edge perturbation and attribute masking by showing its feature-space kNN augmentation gives a 15.3% absolute improvement over the edge-perturbation variant in ablations on Cora, Citeseer, and Pubmed, suggesting the plan's topology and attribute augmentations may be suboptimal unless tuned or replaced. [(Sun et al., 2019-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2fb59ebe271d6b007bb0429c1701fd1004782d1b) warns that combining supervised and unsupervised objectives can cause negative transfer, with InfoGraph improving over supervised on only 7 of 12 QM9 targets, showing no improvement on 1 target, and performing worse on 4 targets, so fixed alpha, beta, and gamma weights could degrade node classification if the node, subgraph, and graph objectives emphasize conflicting information. [(Zhang et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b16b93061acfd1fd0280cb7f494acdc136325d49) further contradicts a topology-only augmentation emphasis by reporting that feature augmentation yields larger improvements than topology graph augmentation and that training without augmentation performs badly, implying the plan should include covariance-preserving or feature-space views. [(Agarwal et al., 2021-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/21b6b07688cf4531b2d7854064e2de5868e2ec4f) shows that stronger regularization can increase AUROC and F1 error rates, indicating that aggressive loss weighting or stability constraints may trade off predictive performance. [(Han et al., 2022-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/62f82e0a3271880ca81c7198cad80305bb3719a7) provides a weaker caution that DropEdge can slightly reduce isolation-part MRR and that auxiliary hypergraph noise can hurt head and tail1 parts, so edge perturbation may not help all node regimes. [(Cen et al., 2019-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9ef3c30d0096e1668918f66d9f065e64455bde30) does not directly contradict the plan but provides no quantitative evidence for contrastive projection heads, temperature, or hierarchical loss weights, leaving the proposed 64-dimensional MLP head and tau, alpha, beta, and gamma tuning under-supported by that work.

### Suggested Action

Most importantly, the plan should treat augmentation as a tunable, dataset-specific component rather than a fixed set: run validation-based selection and ablations for node dropping, edge perturbation, feature masking, k-hop subgraph sampling, virtual node addition, and global attribute masking, and add feature-space or covariance-preserving augmentations such as random projection from [(Zhang et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b16b93061acfd1fd0280cb7f494acdc136325d49) and kNN feature-space augmentation from [(Shao et al., 2021-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8d16a43dfdb9d280a8de327635a3a6c6fe1bfd86), because [(You et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/76c124786ccf4263e6403a15a8e350ac28be4e65) shows augmentation type and ratio matter. Second, tune tau and alpha, beta, gamma jointly on validation, with separate ablations for node-only, subgraph-only, graph-only, and combined losses, and monitor for negative transfer as in [(Sun et al., 2019-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2fb59ebe271d6b007bb0429c1701fd1004782d1b); consider adaptive or learnable weights and early stopping if objectives conflict. Third, validate the exact architecture: compare the proposed 2-layer 128-dimensional GNN and 2-layer 64-dimensional MLP head against the 1-layer 512-dimensional GCN and projection setup in [(Shao et al., 2021-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8d16a43dfdb9d280a8de327635a3a6c6fe1bfd86), the default GCN in [(You et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/76c124786ccf4263e6403a15a8e350ac28be4e65), and the two-layer GNN encoder evidence in [(Cen et al., 2019-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9ef3c30d0096e1668918f66d9f065e64455bde30), while reporting Adam learning rate, batch size, Xavier initialization, epochs, early-stopping patience, and hardware for reproducibility. Fourth, strengthen evaluation on the planned Cora, Citeseer, PubMed, and Reddit datasets by including the baselines GCA, GMI, MVGRL, GRACE, GraphCL, and DGI, using standard transductive and inductive splits, low-label regimes, mean and standard deviation over repeated runs, and significance tests where possible. Fifth, explicitly test whether the subgraph-level loss adds value beyond node and graph contrast, since the strongest multi-level evidence is node plus graph in [(Shao et al., 2021-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8d16a43dfdb9d280a8de327635a3a6c6fe1bfd86) and node-level contrast in [(Han et al., 2022-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/62f82e0a3271880ca81c7198cad80305bb3719a7); if subgraph contrast does not improve low-label node classification, simplify the objective. Finally, if stability or fairness is relevant, borrow regularization and validation practices from [(Agarwal et al., 2021-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/21b6b07688cf4531b2d7854064e2de5868e2ec4f), but keep the primary focus on predictive node classification.

## Context: To qualitatively inspect the learned node representations, the plan visualizes embeddings. Method: Qualitative Analysis: We plan to perform embedding visualization using t-SNE to qualitatively assess the discriminative power of learned representations and demonstrate improved cluster separation compared to existing methods.

### Support

The strongest support is from [(Peng et al., 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/896f2aac3d9f32cd676442de2f88e8a802d8c349), because it directly applies t-SNE to node embeddings from an unsupervised GNN on the plan's core datasets Cora, Citeseer, PubMed, and Reddit, using a 512-hidden-unit GCN encoder, bilinear sigmoid discriminator, GMI-mean/GMI-adaptive objectives, PyTorch on a single Tesla P40, Adam with learning rate 0.001, PReLU, five negative samples, and logistic regression for classification; this shows t-SNE is a recognized qualitative tool for inspecting graph node representations in self-supervised settings. The next strongest support is the original t-SNE method [(Maaten, and Hinton, unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1c46943103bd7b7a2c7be86859995a4144d1938b), which demonstrates that t-SNE after PCA to 30 dimensions, with 1000 iterations, adaptive learning rate starting at 100, momentum 0.5 for 250 iterations then 0.8, and early exaggeration 4 for 50 iterations, can separate MNIST, Olivetti, and COIL-20 clusters, and its random-walk variant with k=20 neighbors and 6,000 landmarks from 60,000 MNIST points achieved 5.13 percent 1-NN error versus 5.75 percent for the original 784-dimensional representation, indicating the technique can be scaled and reveal local structure. Support for the broader cluster-separation goal comes from [(Hassani, and Ahmadi, 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1f3c381eedfe8914b81e93070bfdb00cf86ac943), a graph contrastive method using adjacency and personalized PageRank views, dedicated encoders, sum pooling, 512 hidden dimension, and K-Means clustering with NMI/ARI over 50 runs, although it does not report t-SNE; this aligns with the plan's need to show that hierarchical contrastive learning yields class-coherent node embeddings. Further, but weaker, support is provided by [(Pan et al., 2019-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4a751d520534771593d359069f99d7c4621a861a), which overlays original graph linkages on a 2D projection of ARGA node embeddings to inspect compact same-class clusters and local structure, and by [(Liu et al., 2020-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/639206a9a32d91386924f1c94e9760dfb43df72e), which uses qualitative visualization plus a smoothness metric on Cora, CiteSeer, PubMed, Coauthor CS, Coauthor Physics, Amazon Computers, and Amazon Photo to show that decoupling transformation and propagation preserves distinguishability in deep GNNs. The weakest support is from [(Yi et al., 2023-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f08e658d5bc6d291799c70ec8437595e674f56c5), which uses a qualitative clustering visualization but omits algorithm, dataset, and hyperparameter details, and from [(Xie et al., 2015-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f44ff4fc0ed0142cb18472a5ba421bb538aa837e), which inspects DEC clusters by showing 10 top-scoring images per cluster sorted by distance to centroid rather than t-SNE; [(Bo et al., 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/fffbbc9d515ca6b5e9bc59cdded8c96a90c364d3) and [(Liu et al., 2022-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/23391745a65e9c677e712a4e0f594565ee5aadf1) also support the objective of assessing cluster separation, but they do so through quantitative metrics rather than t-SNE.

### Contradictions

The strongest contradiction is that the closest graph clustering and contrastive-learning literature treats cluster separation as a quantitative, reproducible property rather than a visual impression: [(Bo et al., 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/fffbbc9d515ca6b5e9bc59cdded8c96a90c364d3) reports average ACC, NMI, ARI, and macro F1 over 10 runs for SDCN on USPS, HHAR, Reuters, ACM, DBLP, and Citeseer, with K-Means initialized 20 times and average improvements of about 6 percent ACC, 17 percent NMI, and 28 percent ARI over the best baseline, and [(Hassani, and Ahmadi, 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1f3c381eedfe8914b81e93070bfdb00cf86ac943) evaluates local-global graph contrastive learning with K-Means NMI/ARI over 50 runs and linear node-classification accuracies such as 86.8 ± 0.5, 73.3 ± 0.5, and 80.1 ± 0.7; neither uses t-SNE as the evidence for improved separation, so a t-SNE-only analysis would be less rigorous than standard practice. The next strongest contradiction is that newer corrupted-graph evaluation work [(Liu et al., 2022-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/23391745a65e9c677e712a4e0f594565ee5aadf1) uses extrinsic ACC, NMI, ARI, and F1 after Kuhn-Munkres cluster-to-class mapping, and describes intrinsic silhouette, Calinski-Harabasz, and Davies-Bouldin metrics, on Cora, CiteSeer, BAT, EAT, and UAT under 10 percent, 30 percent, 50 percent, 70 percent, and 90 percent missing attributes/edges and Gaussian noise standard deviations 0.01, 0.1, 1, and 10, showing that cluster separation should be measured under controlled perturbations rather than only inspected visually. The original t-SNE paper also contradicts overreliance on visual separation by emphasizing that t-SNE is a qualitative visualization whose outcome depends on PCA to 30 dimensions, neighborhood/perplexity choices, 1000 iterations, early exaggeration, momentum, and random-walk/landmark subsampling for large data, with labels used only to color plots [(Maaten, and Hinton, unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1c46943103bd7b7a2c7be86859995a4144d1938b). Direct graph t-SNE evidence is limited: [(Peng et al., 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/896f2aac3d9f32cd676442de2f88e8a802d8c349) reports t-SNE plots but no quantitative t-SNE outcome, silhouette score, cluster separation metric, or statistical significance, so it does not establish that t-SNE can demonstrate improved separation. Weaker contradictions are that [(Pan et al., 2019-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4a751d520534771593d359069f99d7c4621a861a), [(Liu et al., 2020-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/639206a9a32d91386924f1c94e9760dfb43df72e), and [(Yi et al., 2023-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f08e658d5bc6d291799c70ec8437595e674f56c5) provide qualitative visualizations without specifying the dimensionality-reduction algorithm, t-SNE hyperparameters, hardware, dataset size, or statistical tests, limiting reproducibility and comparability; [(Xie et al., 2015-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f44ff4fc0ed0142cb18472a5ba421bb538aa837e) uses cluster representative images rather than t-SNE and reports no separation metric, so it does not support t-SNE as a rigorous cluster-separation demonstration.

### Suggested Action

The most important improvement is to make the qualitative t-SNE analysis part of a quantitative evaluation protocol, following [(Bo et al., 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/fffbbc9d515ca6b5e9bc59cdded8c96a90c364d3) and [(Hassani, and Ahmadi, 2020-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1f3c381eedfe8914b81e93070bfdb00cf86ac943): report node classification accuracy, NMI, ARI, and macro F1 after K-Means or linear probing, with at least 10 runs and preferably 50 runs, standard deviations, and significance tests, especially in the plan's low-label regimes of 1-10 labels per class and in transductive/inductive settings. Second, specify a reproducible t-SNE protocol inspired by [(Maaten, and Hinton, unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1c46943103bd7b7a2c7be86859995a4144d1938b): fix PCA preprocessing, for example 30 dimensions or a dataset-specific value, perplexity, learning rate, 1000 iterations, early exaggeration, momentum schedule, and random seeds, and use identical t-SNE settings for the proposed hierarchical model and baselines DGI, GRACE, MVGRL, and GraphCL; for Reddit, use subsampling or landmark/random-walk t-SNE to control compute and report the number of nodes visualized. Third, make the visualization graph-aware by overlaying original edges or k-hop neighborhoods, as in [(Pan et al., 2019-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4a751d520534771593d359069f99d7c4621a861a), and optionally include representative nodes per cluster or smoothness/oversmoothing diagnostics, as in [(Liu et al., 2020-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/639206a9a32d91386924f1c94e9760dfb43df72e), to verify that local graph structure is preserved and not only class colors are separated. Fourth, treat t-SNE as supplementary evidence rather than primary proof, and if the plan wants to claim robustness, add quantitative cluster metrics under standard and corrupted/noisy settings, following [(Liu et al., 2022-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/23391745a65e9c677e712a4e0f594565ee5aadf1), while using visual inspection only to illustrate, not replace, metric-based comparisons.
