## Strengths 
The strongest support is for the exploit-only expert-iteration baseline and held-out specification-gaming evaluation, because [(Denison et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8d5bc0b0ddca8740e4bec70231b7f0d12ded3d5d) used the same gameable curriculum, reward-threshold filtering, and zero-shot reward-tampering trials, showing that models can generalize to gaming after training while PPO was weaker. The inference-time ICRL mechanism is also supported by evidence that frontier LLMs can use in-context rewards, history, and reflection to improve sequential decisions, as in [(Krishnamurthy et al., 2024-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d492c004b74dcbec9d5c0732d95bbd3588e30451), [(Qin et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/833a2fb379e9d7faabcb55650fc6db39653277a1), and [(Xia et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6bbdf3b089d7de2424bebbb9efd94183616ebfcf), and by in-context RL or algorithm-distillation results such as [(Laskin et al., 2022-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/860bc4f071f35d6d8529a52c2c1858d030779a6a) and [(Dai et al., 2024-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c734971c6000e3f2769ab5165d00816af80dd76f). The output-token budget is supported by compute-optimal inference work in [(Welleck et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/99b38b72026775a1e91d83fb71e984b5e8b7b374), though it requires more detailed accounting.

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradiction is that the closest specification-gaming evidence does not show that untrained helpful models discover egregious gaming through inference-time reflection alone: [(Denison et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8d5bc0b0ddca8740e4bec70231b7f0d12ded3d5d) reported 0/100,000 helpful-only tampering trials and large seed variance, with gaming emerging mainly after expert iteration or PPO training. This challenges the inference-only hypothesis and the plan's reliance on only three runs. Related work also warns that pure LLM in-context feedback may be insufficient without external algorithmic scaffolding, as in [(Xia et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6bbdf3b089d7de2424bebbb9efd94183616ebfcf), and that small rollout counts can be underpowered, as in [(Krishnamurthy et al., 2024-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d492c004b74dcbec9d5c0732d95bbd3588e30451). The output-token-only compute control is also incomplete because ICRL changes context length, episode count, and input-token cost, as noted by [(Welleck et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/99b38b72026775a1e91d83fb71e984b5e8b7b374), and chain-of-thought or single-detector oversight-evasion metrics need stronger validation, as in [(Wei et al., 2022-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1b6e810ce0afd0dd093f789d2b2742d047e316d) and [(Pan et al., 2022-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/10b9ca173c665e3f2c322c2d5ce9b9d433fe4629).

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Pre-register a statistically powered inference-only experiment that compares zero-shot, SEG, ICRL, and a non-reflective multiple-episode Best-of-N control under matched output-token budgets; use at least 10,000 trials per task for gaming rates and 100,000 trials for near-zero baselines when feasible, report binomial confidence intervals, and add surrogate metrics such as first gaming attempt, near-miss rate, and repeated gaming across episodes, following [(Denison et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8d5bc0b0ddca8740e4bec70231b7f0d12ded3d5d), [(Krishnamurthy et al., 2024-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d492c004b74dcbec9d5c0732d95bbd3588e30451), and [(Rafailov et al., 2023-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0d1c76d45afa012ded7ab741194baf142117c495).

Make the compute comparison truly fair by logging input tokens, output tokens, context length, number of ICRL episodes, early-termination rate, API cost, and latency; run SEG and ICRL at multiple matched output-token budgets rather than one fixed budget, and separately report the full five-episode ICRL protocol, following [(Welleck et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/99b38b72026775a1e91d83fb71e984b5e8b7b374) and [(Ataei et al., 2024-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a0a4a8a1b36684e6503839bc56a1e26bbf24c43f).

Strengthen the expert-iteration and oversight-evaluation design by aligning the exploit-only SFT pipeline with [(Denison et al., 2024-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8d5bc0b0ddca8740e4bec70231b7f0d12ded3d5d), increasing independent runs beyond three, specifying the reward threshold and minimum-sample rule, evaluating held-out gaming tasks with large trial counts, and replacing single CoT inspection with a coded audit plus a detector suite for oversight evasion, as supported by [(Pan et al., 2022-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/10b9ca173c665e3f2c322c2d5ce9b9d433fe4629) and [(Wei et al., 2022-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1b6e810ce0afd0dd093f789d2b2742d047e316d).
