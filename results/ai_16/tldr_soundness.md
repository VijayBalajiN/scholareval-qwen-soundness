## Strengths 
The strongest support is for the proposal's core MARL formulation and transformer/attention components: decentralized intersection agents with local observations, phase actions, and queue/waiting-time rewards are well supported by [(Zhu et al., 2021-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ee3f989a4c39836e92e801fa71266dc258049e00) and by SUMO/PPO waiting-time experiments in [(Wang et al., 2023-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/02262750e20410b55cc4f627cd2d3aa73d5d8ff3). Attention-based spatial coordination is supported by graph-attention TSC in [(Wei et al., 2019-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/40968cf84b03ba9643278cb6c7c878e4accdb8cb) and transformer multi-scenario co-training with zero-shot transfer in [(Jiang et al., 2024-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c94b6bd18cbd86d59947be9e8f168279f2348f83). The PointNet-inspired permutation-invariant lane encoder is supported by set-encoding evidence in [(Charles et al., 2016-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d997beefc0922d97202789d2ac307c55c2c52fba) and traffic-specific shared lane/phase encoders in [(Zang et al., 2020-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d766b67868b3a9a97ad4407cd496760698d2a427), while the automated network/demand pipeline is supported by scenario-generation work in [(Li et al., 2021-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9dae8d598f9dd9d8c96f771699f5e37a27e71df3) and [(Kheterpal et al., 2018-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f7bc1dffcf9f72f1c801199ed5676967baa07cbd).

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions concern the proposed unconstrained transformer communication, PPO assumptions, and minimal-state hypothesis. First, global all-agent attention may be harmful: [(Wei et al., 2019-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/40968cf84b03ba9643278cb6c7c878e4accdb8cb) found performance best with 2 to 5 neighbors and about 5 heads, and communication-minimization or gating evidence in [(Wang et al., 2019-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/66ab093b76aa8842e7cc8c66fa8d84359b8ce88e), [(Singh et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/320f80ad1a95de5824b9477c4e3cec37ee007d10), and [(Kim et al., 2019-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d50a2f71fda43f33328706223e9c6b5bb434da77) shows that always-on attention or uncontrolled peer information can underperform. Second, PPO and phase-change actions are not guaranteed: [(Tang et al., 2023-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/091461cb18c413c77037a6a5e08415e91a01f4fc) reports IPPO instability and stronger COMMA-DDPG results, while [(Ma et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ab954896c78f4c1136e3178ef08a3316d996d316) shows switching costs can materially affect policy quality. Third, minimal state may not be competitive under saturated or nonstationary traffic: [(Kuyer et al., 2008-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c5787226587d6612fe702d7f3a2a869a72cf6573) and [(Alegre et al., 2020-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/37b8c642baebdefaca9282ef573aa290841d1a39) show reduced or coarse state can degrade performance, while strong full-observation baselines such as [(Zheng et al., 2019-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/aa9e02a8ccdd24106d71b2155351f088299abf2d) and [(Zang et al., 2020-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d766b67868b3a9a97ad4407cd496760698d2a427) rely on detailed lane/movement state. Finally, PointNet-style max pooling may discard lane/phase relational structure, as suggested by [(Li et al., 2018-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6400c36efdb8a66b401b6aef26c057227266fddd) and [(Gao et al., 2020-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/296c808abc2b11c6b7cc44237357b6590bac4f35), and much generalization evidence remains small-scale or lacks significance, as in [(Jiang et al., 2024-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c94b6bd18cbd86d59947be9e8f168279f2348f83).

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Make distance-aware local attention and lane/phase relational encoding first-class ablations: implement top-k nearest-neighbor masking with k around 2 to 5, exponential decay lengths, hard radius cutoffs, learned communication gates, and head-count ablations up to about 5; augment the PointNet lane encoder with lane adjacency, phase membership, and stop-line/turn edge features; compare the full model against no-communication local PPO, CoLight, X-Light, MaxPressure, and fixed-time control on the 7-agent ring and 73-agent grid, reporting attention statistics, parameters, FLOPs, convergence curves, and significance tests. This addresses evidence that broader attention can hurt in [(Wei et al., 2019-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/40968cf84b03ba9643278cb6c7c878e4accdb8cb), that communication minimization/gating/dropout matter in [(Wang et al., 2019-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/66ab093b76aa8842e7cc8c66fa8d84359b8ce88e), [(Singh et al., 2018-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/320f80ad1a95de5824b9477c4e3cec37ee007d10), and [(Kim et al., 2019-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d50a2f71fda43f33328706223e9c6b5bb434da77), that transformer TSC benefits from multi-scenario co-training in [(Jiang et al., 2024-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c94b6bd18cbd86d59947be9e8f168279f2348f83), and that relational lane/map structure can improve encoding in [(Li et al., 2018-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6400c36efdb8a66b401b6aef26c057227266fddd) and [(Gao et al., 2020-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/296c808abc2b11c6b7cc44237357b6590bac4f35).

Turn the three observation levels into a controlled minimal-state test under dynamic and saturated traffic: define no-traffic as phase/timing/topology only, limited observation as noisy/delayed high-level speed or congestion metrics, and full observation as lane-level counts or positions; evaluate scheduled flow-rate changes, local versus nonlocal OD patterns, and saturated demand; include rule-based Cools-style, TC-SBC, MaxPressure, fixed-time, and full-observation FRAP/MetaLight baselines; report travel time, delay, queue length, waiting vehicles, fuel consumption, and CO2 emissions with multiple seeds, confidence intervals, and significance tests. This addresses the caution that local counters can be competitive in [(Cools et al., 2006-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2ea05b32801eb6f7d5037e9701e6e96d7b8b755c), that compact congestion state can underperform in [(Kuyer et al., 2008-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c5787226587d6612fe702d7f3a2a869a72cf6573), that reduced/coarse state degrades under context changes in [(Alegre et al., 2020-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/37b8c642baebdefaca9282ef573aa290841d1a39), and that detailed movement-level state can be a strong upper bound in [(Zheng et al., 2019-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/aa9e02a8ccdd24106d71b2155351f088299abf2d).

Stabilize the PPO control loop and make the action/reward design testable: use MAPPO with GAE, advantage normalization, entropy regularization, reward scaling, and an optional centralized critic; compare phase-change actions against continuous green-duration actions; add a switching-cost penalty and report switch proportion, phase stability, and responsible rate; specify SUMO version, 3600-second episodes, 5- or 10-second action intervals, hardware, PPO hyperparameters, training steps, convergence diagnostics, and statistical tests. This addresses PPO instability and continuous-action evidence in [(Tang et al., 2023-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/091461cb18c413c77037a6a5e08415e91a01f4fc), switching-cost sensitivity in [(Ma et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ab954896c78f4c1136e3178ef08a3316d996d316), the SUMO/PPO evaluation protocol in [(Wang et al., 2023-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/02262750e20410b55cc4f627cd2d3aa73d5d8ff3), and the local-observation transfer protocol in [(Zhu et al., 2021-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ee3f989a4c39836e92e801fa71266dc258049e00).
