## Strengths 
The strongest support is for the core TT/PROTES optimizer: [(Batsheva et al., 2023-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c399f4724bde401589e206064e93e9663d93c675) shows PROTES uses probabilistic TT sampling, K=100, k=10, R=5, and 10,000 objective calls, outperforming TTOpt, Optima-TT, and nevergrad on 19 of 20 benchmarks. TT feasibility on ImageNet is supported by [(Entezari, and Papalexakis, 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d17508f5038aad3cdc20fff8378027633fa243ca), which finds 4-mode TT faster and more accurate than Parafac/Tucker. Attribution-guided pixel selection is supported by [(Yang et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a556a1ca23e4b2aaa7dacf928a2803d4faeaa162), [(Kumar et al., 2019-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2e8eb3d8b0c67f593a1a64d28624aeeaa7c736f8), [(Ghorbani et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/96cf326c91adc96aa59382bf71fbead29ea34c52), and [(Sundararajan et al., 2017-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f302e136c41db5de1d624412f68c9174cf7ae8be). Dimensionality reduction is supported by [(Ilyas et al., 2018-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/10ab21b120e305b6d3cbf81c5a906d36521152f1), [(Guo et al., 2019-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/65fd9ded2c411d90bcf6d38132463797754d2d21), and [(Li et al., 2020-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/16d446f22f86596d7bee3a177e5cc846fc8bc02d). The 1000-image, 10,000-query ImageNet protocol and baselines are supported by [(Andriushchenko et al., 2019-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8733fe2371b615609b04e2e910b1ecfa8e77cbc2), [(Weng et al., 2023-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8ed5ffcfc588a879a00476e8ac4599b92232ce87), [(Papernot et al., 2015-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/819167ace2f0caae7745d2f25a803979be5fbfae), and [(Pomponi et al., 2022-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/19216336ba37b64d066cf89d4418f5b5e625f35c). The seven-model target suite is supported by [(Lin et al., 2019-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/fb0568dcb546bbb4d7d1ed71ce395f4b66691003) and [(Dong et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8e37a3b227b68953f8067215828dc8b8714cb21b). HSV/color perturbations are supported by [(Hosseini, and Poovendran, 2018-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/48d88124dd89d988386ae452516dbba6c4a96e85) and [(Wang et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4e5afc4eb4d9ad5281b94542f38ab716dc796854).

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions concern the exact pipeline and hyperparameters. The direct TETRADAT report provides no quantitative validation: [(Chertkov, and Oseledets, 2023-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/bab54ca1f24554caba0e12736770873b9c23a177). PROTES evidence is synthetic and uses lambda=0.05, not 0.01: [(Batsheva et al., 2023-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c399f4724bde401589e206064e93e9663d93c675). TT rank 5 may be too low because ImageNet TT work searched ranks 40 to 150: [(Entezari, and Papalexakis, 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d17508f5038aad3cdc20fff8378027633fa243ca), and low-rank tensor preprocessing can act as a defense: [(Cho et al., 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a27d774cfe6d427e80898f39934979620ee). Single VGG-19 Integrated Gradients may not transfer to diverse or adversarial targets: [(Adebayo et al., 2018-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8dc8f3e0127adc6985d4695e9b69d04717b2fde8), [(Ghorbani et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/96cf326c91adc96aa59382bf71fbead29ea34c52), [(Singh et al., 2019-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/05ba68950ac4ca970cdd77f02bbeff7ff9f776d3), [(Dong et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8e37a3b227b68953f8067215828dc8b8714cb21b), and [(Dohmatob et al., 2022-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/10a31b65fe2e2b82ff79e1242d653dfc0c0ebbf8). The initial epsilon=1 HSV amplitude is much larger than standard 0.05 or 8/255 bounds: [(Ilyas et al., 2018-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b3f83e8416010e9c3a705a0b6390d268e5ddf5c0), [(Bai et al., 2020-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/dac9a851d7a257869dbfc0fcb03f04daa55bea92), and [(Andriushchenko et al., 2019-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8733fe2371b615609b04e2e910b1ecfa8e77cbc2). The 10,000-query budget may be insufficient for adversarial targets: [(Dong et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8e37a3b227b68953f8067215828dc8b8714cb21b), [(Byun et al., 2021-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b3e6ba0d027c92937b12d688a81c1b888102e14b), [(Qin et al., 2021-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/07b65c99f615ad2333eb427a5c3da204725939d9), and [(Dong et al., 2021-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/486c94d035d9966cdd4078f697307faff03683df). The dual target/VGG-19 correctness filter is nonstandard and may shrink or bias the sample: [(Andriushchenko et al., 2019-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8733fe2371b615609b04e2e910b1ecfa8e77cbc2) and [(Yang et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a556a1ca23e4b2aaa7dacf928a2803d4faeaa162). HSV saturation/value-only edits may omit hue and may be less perceptually grounded than Lab/DeltaE: [(Hosseini, and Poovendran, 2018-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/48d88124dd89d988386ae452516dbba6c4a96e85), [(Wang et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4e5afc4eb4d9ad5281b94542f38ab716dc796854), and [(Zhao et al., 2019-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/54c15364b5873e39257c221112fdf22299ed9e8a). L1/L2 norms alone may not capture visual quality: [(Liu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d47b0392fce617d44fa2b681892241a69d534c9c).

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Run a small-scale pilot and ablate the core components before the full ImageNet protocol: compare TETRADAT against full-pixel PROTES, random 10% pixels, VGG-19 IG, target-specific or ensemble attribution, spatial tiling, and low-frequency priors, while sweeping TT rank r=5,10,20,40,80,150 and PROTES lambda=0.01,0.05, K=100, k=10; report ASR versus query count, median/mean queries, L0/L1/L2/L-infinity, runtime, and multiple seeds with confidence intervals, motivated by [(Batsheva et al., 2023-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c399f4724bde401589e206064e93e9663d93c675), [(Entezari, and Papalexakis, 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d17508f5038aad3cdc20fff8378027633fa243ca), [(Ilyas et al., 2018-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/10ab21b120e305b6d3cbf81c5a906d36521152f1), [(Li et al., 2020-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/16d446f22f86596d7bee3a177e5cc846fc8bc02d), and [(Yang et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a556a1ca23e4b2aaa7dacf928a2803d4faeaa162).

Make the attribution stage target-aware and statistically validated: pre-register the target-correct and VGG-19-correct intersection, report retained image counts and class balance, use target-correct-only as the primary benchmark and both-correct as sensitivity, and compare VGG-19 IG against random-label/weight VGG-19, random pixels, edge maps, target gradients where available, and ensemble/averaged IG from multiple auxiliary models; test pixel fractions 5%,10%,20%,50%,100% and IG steps 15,50,100, with dynamic mask refresh if needed, because [(Adebayo et al., 2018-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8dc8f3e0127adc6985d4695e9b69d04717b2fde8), [(Ghorbani et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/96cf326c91adc96aa59382bf71fbead29ea34c52), [(Singh et al., 2019-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/05ba68950ac4ca970cdd77f02bbeff7ff9f776d3), [(Dong et al., 2017-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8e37a3b227b68953f8067215828dc8b8714cb21b), [(Wang et al., 2021-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b18580c6746a47603955074931d4687e268781ea), and [(Zhang et al., 2022-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8f8841840339c5eabf5faddf104008eb187ffe6c) show attribution maps can be unstable, model-specific, or input-artifact-driven.

Define an explicit perturbation budget, restart schedule, and perceptual evaluation: allocate the 10,000 queries across amplitude restarts with a stopping rule, test epsilon=1 against standard 0.05 and 8/255 bounds, compare RGB additive perturbations with full HSV hue/saturation/value edits and Lab/DeltaE constraints, and report L0, L1, L2, L-infinity, PSNR, SSIM, NIQE, FSIMc, DeltaE00, and a small human/perceptual study; include robustness checks against JPEG, feature squeezing, and low-rank/TT denoising, and report query curves up to 10,000 and optionally 20,000 or 50,000 queries, motivated by [(Bai et al., 2020-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/dac9a851d7a257869dbfc0fcb03f04daa55bea92), [(Ilyas et al., 2018-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b3f83e8416010e9c3a705a0b6390d268e5ddf5c0), [(Andriushchenko et al., 2019-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8733fe2371b615609b04e2e910b1ecfa8e77cbc2), [(Hosseini, and Poovendran, 2018-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/48d88124dd89d988386ae452516dbba6c4a96e85), [(Wang et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4e5afc4eb4d9ad5281b94542f38ab716dc796854), [(Zhao et al., 2019-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/54c15364b5873e39257c221112fdf22299ed9e8a), [(Liu et al., 2020-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d47b0392fce617d44fa2b681892241a69d534c9c), and [(Cho et al., 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a27d774cfe6d427e80898f39934979620ee).
