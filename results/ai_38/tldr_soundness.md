## Strengths 
The strongest support is for the core principal-stratum idea: restricting counterfactual fairness to individuals with no individual causal effect, Y(0)=Y(1), is directly validated by [(Imai, and Jiang, 2020-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a5a271960396489b1f8708910e4e7b3488991850) and [(Nilforoshan et al., 2022-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/106e1064097a51e42a46f3f262d73a6720fadaa8), which formalize principal fairness and show that conditional principal fairness can imply counterfactual equalized odds and statistical fairness under conditional independence of the protected attribute and the principal stratum. The estimation plan is strongly supported by [(Coston et al., 2019-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b5feed60ea2c0aaaca1e93283a726390f3b7ed9b), which uses outcome regression, inverse propensity scoring, and doubly robust estimators with sample splitting and confidence intervals to evaluate counterfactual rather than observational fairness. The post-processing component is supported by [(Mishler et al., 2020-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3d8acbdd2820def27be114091b85ec39dfdc9baf), which uses a linear program over flip probabilities, doubly robust cross-fitting, and reports flip rates and loss on COMPAS. The bounds-based evaluation is supported by [(Balke, and Pearl, 1997-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7abc07c41b2bade96bc52f10c892f206fbabbe63) and [(Wu et al., 2019-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e1512a1039978bbefc9135b969f81bb5fc6ee169), which show that unobserved counterfactual quantities can be bounded by linear programming over observed probabilities and that tighter bounds can change fairness judgments.

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions concern post-processing and inference. [(Coston et al., 2019-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b5feed60ea2c0aaaca1e93283a726390f3b7ed9b) shows that post-processing to equalize observational error rates can create counterfactual imbalance, so the plan must constrain CF and PCF directly rather than relying on observed parity. [(Mishler et al., 2020-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3d8acbdd2820def27be114091b85ec39dfdc9baf) shows that exact counterfactual equalized odds can flip nearly 20% of predictions, increase loss, and that cost-sensitive variants can become degenerate all-zero or all-one classifiers. [(Balke, and Pearl, 1997-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7abc07c41b2bade96bc52f10c892f206fbabbe63) and [(Horowitz, and Manski, 2000-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/948ad7ff478d48500350278e93c6559c06f7cc2a) warn that bound-based inference can be computationally expensive, conservative, or inconclusive, with wide intervals and imperfect coverage. [(Imai, and Jiang, 2020-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a5a271960396489b1f8708910e4e7b3488991850) requires conditional independence of the protected attribute and the principal stratum given W, and [(Zhang, and Bareinboim, unknown_d)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6bb8e5c50eb219ce6344523bf9ebf0afc8b53123) and [(Chiappa, and Gillam, 2018-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8993bfbe06c0480bcb42233f058ef419278b8897) show that enforcing one counterfactual fairness target can increase other counterfactual errors or collapse accuracy. For OULAD, [(Verger et al., 2023-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cc85ccd4fb46894b38c654ae4505f481aa11fa0d) and [(Verger et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6332492f49736fdd695ff7ea1a11b3ffbcd2dd01) show course-specific, missingness-sensitive, and validation-unstable fairness behavior, so a single PC graph over all students may not capture the relevant causal structure.

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Formalize the principal fairness hierarchy and assumptions: define R_i = {Y_i(0)=Y_i(1)}, specify the conditioning set W or X, and prove or empirically test A_i independent of R_i given W, following [(Imai, and Jiang, 2020-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a5a271960396489b1f8708910e4e7b3488991850) and [(Nilforoshan et al., 2022-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/106e1064097a51e42a46f3f262d73a6720fadaa8). State how the plan's ignorability assumption A independent of (Y(1), Y(0), D(1), D(0)) given X relates to these conditions, and derive when principal parity, principal conditional fairness, principal equalized odds, and individual-level PCF imply one another or remain non-nested.

Build an uncertainty-aware bounds and feasibility diagnostic: adapt the linear-programming bound machinery of [(Balke, and Pearl, 1997-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7abc07c41b2bade96bc52f10c892f206fbabbe63) and [(Wu et al., 2019-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e1512a1039978bbefc9135b969f81bb5fc6ee169) to conditional principal strata, derive sharp bounds for P(D(0)=D(1)|Y(0)=Y(1)) and weaker variants, estimate them with OR, IPS, and DR plug-ins, and use bootstrap or influence-function confidence intervals as in [(Horowitz, and Manski, 2000-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/948ad7ff478d48500350278e93c6559c06f7cc2a) and [(Coston et al., 2019-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b5feed60ea2c0aaaca1e93283a726390f3b7ed9b). Validate on synthetic SEMs with known ground truth by reporting false-positive and false-negative rates for empty feasible regions, coverage, interval width, power, and sensitivity to nuisance misspecification, following [(Nabi, and Benkeser, 2024-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/39c98a671b47f9167cb44b7779775c7a285774b7) and [(Kallus, 2017-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d9361f57fc6a7d869ff67cfac5b5d963bdce82eb).

Implement post-processing as a flip-minimization constrained program with safeguards: use binary flip variables or flip probabilities, minimize decision changes subject to DR-estimated CF and PCF constraints, following [(Mishler et al., 2020-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3d8acbdd2820def27be114091b85ec39dfdc9baf) and [(Coston et al., 2019-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b5feed60ea2c0aaaca1e93283a726390f3b7ed9b). Add positivity, overlap, and weight-stabilization diagnostics, utility caps, fallbacks for infeasibility, and penalties against all-zero or all-one solutions, as motivated by [(Biega et al., 2018-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/630a718bead9ca7cee03bf9f1c7bd1db655d0ff7) and [(Chiappa, and Gillam, 2018-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8993bfbe06c0480bcb42233f058ef419278b8897). On OULAD, use course- or subgroup-stratified splits, report PC graph uncertainty, flip rates, accuracy loss, CF, PCF, confidence intervals, and subgroup effects, following [(Verger et al., 2023-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cc85ccd4fb46894b38c654ae4505f481aa11fa0d) and [(Glymour et al., 2019-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b068f111b4b7f436bb99b945e3191d8061fe531b).
