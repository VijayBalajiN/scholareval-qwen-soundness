## Strengths 
The strongest support is for a transcriptomics-to-target-curation workflow in F. graminearum, because [(Martins et al., 2016-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4175498ce07b5bd73a875316c29994baf43382b8) used PH-1 genome and EST data, secretome and PHI-base filters, structural accessibility, and docking to prioritize candidate proteins before validation. LC-MS metabolomics is supported by [(Genga et al., 2011-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6185c631b6b043ed076b59058825721c24230441) and [(Gunnaiah et al., 2012-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cd51931de84e7111db422558231f9387563a43cc) for differential metabolite profiling and multivariate screening in relevant biological systems. Gene deletion in F. graminearum is supported by [(Wang et al., 2024-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c825b35e476e9f1a2d424115887c5cfd63259fde) and [(Wang et al., 2023-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/69e2e73cb03a1faa61b26d3c046a41bdcf6f7429), and docking as a preliminary filter is supported by [(Martins et al., 2016-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4175498ce07b5bd73a875316c29994baf43382b8) and [(Naimuzzaman et al., 2025-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/316c2fe21afe4fbc0a4ac5345dae701cdb46edb0).

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions concern overreliance on simple thresholds and unvalidated assumptions. A single 48 h EC90 transcriptomic screen with P<0.05 and fold change >=1.5 may capture indirect stress responses and lacks FDR and target-accessibility filters, as highlighted by [(Martins et al., 2016-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4175498ce07b5bd73a875316c29994baf43382b8). PLS-DA metabolomics with P<0.05 and fold change >=1.5 is under-specified without cross-validation, permutation, VIP, FDR, QC, and validated extraction, as shown by [(Genga et al., 2011-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6185c631b6b043ed076b59058825721c24230441) and [(Gunnaiah et al., 2012-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cd51931de84e7111db422558231f9387563a43cc). PCR-only deletion confirmation and generic growth or metabolite-rescue assays are insufficient; [(Lee et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1a680a3d1713d5febdf92c001b765251e05008ca) supports CRISPR and Southern confirmation, and [(Wang et al., 2024-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c825b35e476e9f1a2d424115887c5cfd63259fde) shows pathway-specific readouts are needed. Docking alone is not proof of direct binding, and E. coli expression may fail for membrane or secreted targets, as indicated by [(Martins et al., 2016-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4175498ce07b5bd73a875316c29994baf43382b8) and [(Naimuzzaman et al., 2025-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/316c2fe21afe4fbc0a4ac5345dae701cdb46edb0). The generic ANOVA and Fisher PLSD plan is weakly supported by [(Kanchiswamy et al., 2010-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e8f6e93d4ce4907a313ed35395837e5c9231b7ac) and should be assay-specific.

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Add a qPCR validation and target-curation step after RNA-seq: validate top differentially expressed genes in at least three biological replicates with reference genes, use DESeq2 FDR<0.05 or 0.1 plus fold change >=1.5, add an early time course or sublethal dose series, and intersect hits with secretome, PHI-base, localization, PDB homology, length, and accessibility filters before docking, as in [(Martins et al., 2016-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4175498ce07b5bd73a875316c29994baf43382b8).

Make metabolomics statistically rigorous and analytically validated: use the same biological replicates, QC samples, randomized injection, XCMS or equivalent, PLS-DA/OPLS-DA with cross-validation, permutation, VIP, FDR or Bonferroni correction, define LC-MS parameters and extraction, and validate top metabolites by targeted LC-MS/MS or orthogonal GC-MS/NMR, following [(Genga et al., 2011-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6185c631b6b043ed076b59058825721c24230441) and [(Gunnaiah et al., 2012-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cd51931de84e7111db422558231f9387563a43cc).

Formalize genetic and biochemical validation: generate deletions by homologous recombination or CRISPR/Cas9 RNP with Southern blot or long-range PCR, include complementation, quantitative phenotyping, dose-response metabolite rescue, and SPR/MST with negative controls, immobilization checks, and expression feasibility; treat docking as a preliminary filter, as supported by [(Lee et al., 2022-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1a680a3d1713d5febdf92c001b765251e05008ca), [(Wang et al., 2024-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c825b35e476e9f1a2d424115887c5cfd63259fde), and [(Naimuzzaman et al., 2025-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/316c2fe21afe4fbc0a4ac5345dae701cdb46edb0).
