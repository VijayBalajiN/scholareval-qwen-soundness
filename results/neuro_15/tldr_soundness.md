## Strengths 
The best-supported methods are the optogenetic causal test and the behavioral/neural discrimination pipeline. Bilateral AAV-mediated inhibitory-opsin silencing of auditory cortex with randomized light/no-light trials and signal-detection-theory readouts is directly supported by [(Lakunina et al., 2021-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/899b45cec4747745c5fe3329adb6540462429125), which used the same architecture to show cortical inactivation impaired auditory detection, and by [(Lohse et al., 2020-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/921a54ca38f3d7d51e00acc143b8df96a75f6cca) for feasibility of auditory cortical silencing. The Go/No-Go pure-tone discrimination task with d-prime, hit rate, false-alarm rate, and response bias is supported by [(Heimburg et al., 2025-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5270415281244f7377afa2f437b90c2ccba5676c), while mouse pure-tone discrimination combined with auditory cortical electrophysiology is supported by [(Maor et al., 2018-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/839c94cb5e156c03434892f926f0c6280cd18471). Neuropixels-style single-neuron ROC and population decoding are supported by [(Angeloni et al., 2021-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8dcd1e526def9d2bfef0d1d294a8d61e5d0dca7f) and learning-related decodability by [(Rabinovich et al., 2022-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e2a5acee28afbd24515d2d3e436e29a3d6bdafdc).

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions concern the absence of direct evidence for the adolescent/adult, naive/expert, multi-region Neuropixels design and the risk of subcortical confounds. [(Maor et al., 2018-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/839c94cb5e156c03434892f926f0c6280cd18471) does not test the proposed P20-37 versus P60-77 age windows, Go/No-Go format, two difficulty levels, multi-region Neuropixels recordings, or optogenetic silencing, so it cannot validate the central age-by-learning hypothesis. [(Cruces-Solís et al., 2018-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a149aa6bb67d031b494d792fe9e4b448abf995f7) shows that predictable sound exposure can reduce spontaneous frequency discrimination acuity and produce plasticity mainly in the inferior colliculus with only minor corticofugal involvement, challenging the assumption that auditory cortical learning alone will drive discrimination changes. Many cited excerpts also lack quantitative benchmarks, sample sizes, effect sizes, statistical tests, and hardware details, leaving the proposed power, effect-size, and mixed-effects analyses underconstrained, as noted in the contradictions for [(Heimburg et al., 2025-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5270415281244f7377afa2f437b90c2ccba5676c), [(Angeloni et al., 2021-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8dcd1e526def9d2bfef0d1d294a8d61e5d0dca7f), and [(McGill et al., 2023-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/55296beb5a9bb7439a8f2eb18a123b6b62bd6d80).

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Run a small, power-analyzed pilot to validate Educage-to-head-fixed transfer and the SDT pipeline: train adolescent and adult mice in Educage, then record the same animals head-fixed; pre-register definitions of hits, false alarms, correct rejects, and misses; report d-prime, hit rate, false-alarm rate, response bias, trial variability, and the correlation between home-cage and head-fixed performance before scaling to Neuropixels recordings, as recommended by the gaps in [(Maor et al., 2018-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/839c94cb5e156c03434892f926f0c6280cd18471) and [(Heimburg et al., 2025-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5270415281244f7377afa2f437b90c2ccba5676c).

Add a pre-association passive baseline and subcortical/peripheral control subset to separate cortical learning from maturation or exposure effects: record passive tone tuning before tone association, measure spontaneous frequency discrimination acuity, and include ABR/AEP or, if feasible, inferior colliculus/cochlear nucleus recordings in a subset of adolescent and adult mice, because [(Cruces-Solís et al., 2018-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/a149aa6bb67d031b494d792fe9e4b448abf995f7) shows predictable exposure can alter subcortical tuning and discrimination acuity.

Pre-register the optogenetic and statistical analysis plan with explicit controls: specify AAV serotype, inhibitory opsin, bilateral fiber coordinates, light wavelength/intensity, control virus, and randomized 50% light trials; analyze d-prime, hit rate, false-alarm rate, and bias with linear mixed-effects models including fixed effects for age, sex, difficulty, session, virus, and interactions, and random effects for animal, repeated measurements, and co-housing, following the causal-test logic of [(Lakunina et al., 2021-06)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/899b45cec4747745c5fe3329adb6540462429125) and the repeated-measurement/sex-age cautions in [(McGill et al., 2023-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/55296beb5a9bb7439a8f2eb18a123b6b62bd6d80) and [(Ding et al., 2025-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3f4e1dba8aa5f53d6426c7514e5c4eed189116ba).
