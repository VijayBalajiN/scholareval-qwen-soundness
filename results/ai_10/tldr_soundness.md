## Strengths 
The strongest support is for the Nature Communications data-collection and subcaption pipeline, because [(Li et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e073a2fda0c4d6198ba231c8e9df10f4f2ba1c51) directly uses the same corpus, pylatexenc conversion, regular-expression subcaption extraction, ungrounded and abstract-grounded captioning, and LLaMA2 continuous pre-training, while [(Hsu et al., 2021-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/08b3c84da0da5bdc0e49a1c2f440743d829a5e1c) supports subfigure prevalence and figure-type structuring. The MMS CIQA multiple-choice design is strongly supported by [(Roberts et al., 2024-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e852785ce2acc2b79aad22f7b2585585a9c88bfc), which validates Figure-to-Caption and Caption-to-Figure accuracy, adversarial negatives, deterministic decoding, LLM parsing, and human baselines. Training-resource claims are also supported by [(Huang et al., 2023-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/fbfef4723d8c8467d7bd523e1d0b703cce0e0f9c) for interleaved pre-training and [(Lu et al., 2022-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d3135733aa39dec20ce72aa138589dda27c8406d) for scientific task-specific supervision.

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions concern the human baseline, extraction validation, metric reliability, split design, and training evidence. Using only computer science graduate students is weak for 72 disciplines because [(Hsu et al., 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f22019445f51088b064141d0934324ed73346177) used domain-specific PhD raters and [(Roberts et al., 2024-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e852785ce2acc2b79aad22f7b2585585a9c88bfc) used CS arXiv figures. The pylatexenc and regex subcaption extraction is not directly validated, and subfigure handling is error-prone in [(Hsu et al., 2021-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/08b3c84da0da5bdc0e49a1c2f440743d829a5e1c), while [(Chieh-Yang et al., 2023-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5f6c67f3111ca652e6709f5ae3b6821c13838a80) favors OCR and embedding alignment. ROUGE and METEOR may be unstable for top systems and item-level caption quality, as shown by [(Ma et al., 2019-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/37c035a8e6ad1da2a8b22d5f2951f01f1f309632) and [(Lowe et al., 2017-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/339c6e6d46836c173fb6a23b493c724896d4cc70). The 1% per-subject split may lack power and article-level leakage control, per [(Pramanick et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e8c31cdb4b8d2cd27a2cf2b1e59ff0b3457d51e5) and [(Dasigi et al., 2021-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4e3935ef7da6bcbb202ec7f8b285c313cadcd044). Finally, the MP-20 case study lacks quantitative precedent and needs contamination control, per [(Li et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e073a2fda0c4d6198ba231c8e9df10f4f2ba1c51) and [(Radford et al., 2021-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6f870f7f02a8c59c3e23f407f3ef00dd1dcf8fc4).

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Add a pre-collection validation protocol for pylatexenc and regex subcaption extraction: sample articles across all 72 disciplines, manually label subfigure boundaries and subcaptions, report precision, recall, F1, conversion failure rate, and inter-rater reliability, and combine regex candidates with OCR or embedding alignment using a high-confidence threshold, as motivated by [(Hsu et al., 2021-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/08b3c84da0da5bdc0e49a1c2f440743d829a5e1c) and [(Chieh-Yang et al., 2023-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5f6c67f3111ca652e6709f5ae3b6821c13838a80).

Replace the CS-only human baseline with a discipline-stratified human evaluation and metric-validation protocol: recruit domain-appropriate graduate students or experts, report inter-annotator agreement, calibrate G-EVAL and modified FACTSCORE against human rankings, and for MMS CIQA use five-way adversarial and random negatives, deterministic decoding, LLM answer parsing, answer-order shuffling, bootstrap confidence intervals, and CLIP or OCR baselines, following [(Roberts et al., 2024-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e852785ce2acc2b79aad22f7b2585585a9c88bfc) and [(Hsu et al., 2023-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f22019445f51088b064141d0934324ed73346177).

Make the split and training experiments reproducible and leakage-controlled: assign each article ID to exactly one split, use per-subject quotas or power analysis instead of only 1%, exclude test and validation articles from all training data, run controlled ablations for Qwen2-VL-2B supervised fine-tuning and LLaMA2-7B continuous pre-training, and add an MP-20 contamination protocol with Overlap, Clean, and All subsets, binomial tests, Clopper-Pearson intervals, and Bonferroni correction, as supported by [(Dasigi et al., 2021-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4e3935ef7da6bcbb202ec7f8b285c313cadcd044), [(Pramanick et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e8c31cdb4b8d2cd27a2cf2b1e59ff0b3457d51e5), and [(Radford et al., 2021-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6f870f7f02a8c59c3e23f407f3ef00dd1dcf8fc4).
