## Strengths 
The strongest support is for a diffusion U-Net floor-plan generator with architectural conditioning and for low-resolution generation followed by learned refinement. [(Shabani et al., 2022-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1889ae7c47137d7c8c39cc82022558c13a46f0b8) shows HouseDiffusion can generate vector floorplans from graph or bubble-diagram constraints and outperform House-GAN++ by 67% diversity and 32% compatibility, with realism -0.19 versus -0.18, supporting diffusion denoising and constraint learning. [(Engelenburg et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c5f654f500bcbcf29e8f34c658423b5c0c065efb) supports architectural-aware U-Net design through wall-cross attention, room-category and door-type embeddings, structural and zoning conditioning, and evaluation with mean IoU, graph compatibility, topology, proportions, and an architect user study. [(Ho et al., 2021-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0f183bcfe65781c06b1a48a6f56e0f3c63e8e4a4) supports cascaded low-resolution-to-high-resolution diffusion, reporting 128x128 FID 3.52/3.76 and 256x256 FID 4.88/4.63, and shows conditioning augmentation is essential for reliable refinement. [(Wang et al., 2021-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/20d319c2abc6bbd6166c6399ea8d4629ef8e175a) and [(Ronneberger et al., 2015-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6364fdaa0a0eccd823a779fcdd489173f938e91a) support U-Net spatial architectures for image-based floor-plan tasks, while [(Sun et al., 2023-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/115f8fd513c38214ea15ceb49add5b970831cd7e) supports the feasibility of low-resolution generation followed by super-resolution and of small-batch conditional diffusion training, including batch size 16 and learning rate 1e-4 in parts of the pipeline.

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions concern scale, detail, conditioning, and evaluation. [(Shabani et al., 2022-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1889ae7c47137d7c8c39cc82022558c13a46f0b8) used 60,000 plans, 256x256 direct generation, batch size 512, learning rate 1e-3 with decay, 250,000 steps, and quantitative diversity, compatibility, and realism metrics, whereas the proposal uses 12,000 images, 128x128 training, batch size 16, learning rate 1e-4, and qualitative inspection, suggesting the setup may be underpowered. [(Leng et al., 2023-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/04a0f3f39c5a6aeddcd2841457d0b500adfcb534) shows a diffusion baseline can have low floor-plan overlap, with micro IoU 12.17 and macro IoU 14.96 under warm-up plus fine-tuning, far below a structured model at 54.34 and 53.30, warning that diffusion without explicit architectural conditioning and pixel-level evaluation may be imprecise. [(Engelenburg et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c5f654f500bcbcf29e8f34c658423b5c0c065efb) contradicts the lack of structural conditioning and qualitative-only evaluation by using wall-cross attention, room and door embeddings, zoning, and metrics such as mean IoU, graph compatibility, topology, and proportions, and it also preserves original orientation rather than forcing rotation alignment. [(Zeng et al., 2019-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/99ef26d08cb55564130b168785e07ab9e33d7256) and [(Weber et al., 2022-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/615c8aae55d860f77c284faf92f485c8603f24dc) indicate that 512x512 resolution helps preserve thin walls and that low-resolution raster floor plans can be fuzzy, so 128x128 training may lose wall, door, and window detail. [(Ho et al., 2021-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0f183bcfe65781c06b1a48a6f56e0f3c63e8e4a4) and [(Khare et al., 2021-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/bc2525ac600518d71d75b0a42014564b17025a72) further warn that generic upscaling can fail if the low-resolution conditioning distribution is not modeled and that super-resolution can improve room detection while degrading icon detection or causing polygon splitting.

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Add explicit architectural conditioning and quantitative evaluation: condition the diffusion U-Net on room graphs, bubble diagrams, zoning labels, boundary/wall structure, room counts, and door/window locations using attention or embeddings, and evaluate generated plans with wall/room/door/window IoU, graph compatibility, topology, validity heuristics, FID or LPIPS, and architect pairwise or Elo ratings with confidence intervals, following [(Shabani et al., 2022-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1889ae7c47137d7c8c39cc82022558c13a46f0b8), [(Engelenburg et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c5f654f500bcbcf29e8f34c658423b5c0c065efb), [(Leng et al., 2023-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/04a0f3f39c5a6aeddcd2841457d0b500adfcb534), [(Wang et al., 2021-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/20d319c2abc6bbd6166c6399ea8d4629ef8e175a), and [(Saharia et al., 2022-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9695824d7a01fad57ba9c01d7d76a519d78d65e7).

Validate the 128x128-plus-upscaling strategy with a small ablation against direct 256x256 or 512x512 generation and multiple refinement routes, including bicubic interpolation, generic SR, diffusion SR or cascade with conditioning augmentation, and upsample guidance; report wall-edge sharpness, junction connectivity, room/door/window accuracy, FID or LPIPS, inference time, and GPU memory, as motivated by [(Ho et al., 2021-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0f183bcfe65781c06b1a48a6f56e0f3c63e8e4a4), [(Hwang et al., 2024-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ea7d472879de137bbb763eb9fb0ce6de187a90c3), [(Teng et al., 2024-05)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/48329152cc038da5e573834123c43d20654b1b02), [(Ren et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/dc7ed870fb23e1319c10553e266b3df6f8243cf3), [(Khare et al., 2021-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/bc2525ac600518d71d75b0a42014564b17025a72), and [(Engelenburg et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c5f654f500bcbcf29e8f34c658423b5c0c065efb).

Run controlled preprocessing and optimization ablations before full training: compare raw, pad/resize-only, detection/background-standardized, and rotation-aligned inputs; test batch sizes 16, 32, and 64 if memory allows, learning rates 1e-4 and 1e-3, warm-up 0, 500, and 1000 steps, fp16 on/off, and a decay schedule; document dataset curation, deduplication, splits, hardware, seeds, diffusion steps, and sampler, following [(Engelenburg et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c5f654f500bcbcf29e8f34c658423b5c0c065efb), [(Shabani et al., 2022-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/1889ae7c47137d7c8c39cc82022558c13a46f0b8), [(Kuhn, 2023-12)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ea0bec0bd97b1dc6d02411e78de56e261f64188d), [(Sun et al., 2023-08)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/115f8fd513c38214ea15ceb49add5b970831cd7e), and [(Chen, and Pan, 2023-03)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cd8e681b5137fd1835b3f93c293d092b1bf90769).
