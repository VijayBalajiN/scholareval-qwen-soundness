## Strengths 
The strongest support is for the core optogenetic photometry workflow: VTA DAT-Cre ChrimsonR stimulation can evoke striatal DA signals detectable by dLight [(Bass et al., 2013-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e83a6ca59b815258ec7bae15322c06d87b24d211), and in vivo optogenetic DA perturbation with striatal readouts is feasible [(Long et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/be9104873f956f19764c8669b4d23acc948b846d). DRN SERT-Cre optogenetics is supported for 5-HT release [(Browne et al., 2018-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/977c2fbb1cbfeaef0cd945fb4a3ec46eb310b91f) and serotonergic viral control [(Nishitani et al., 2018-10)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/5c57ca6037f7531b4f73a73eaff4243e3a922626). DAT and SERT Cre targeting is validated [(He et al., 2023-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e5c0d470c12ca480bf416e42905a2f708399cd8d) [(Dı́az-Ruiz et al., 2009-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b67ba4bce3676c8937cb93203ae1fdb44f892b48), cocaine is a dLight DA positive control in dorsal striatum [(Salinas et al., 2023-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6d2080c01166b331aa2335fba07d7254f855731b), 5-second AUC/peak analysis is supported for optogenetic DA [(Washburn et al., 2024-01)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/128be46f9ad21a520e2b8aacc5e6c730635a3e68), the 4-5 week viral/3-day habituation timeline is supported [(Saunders et al., 2018-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3a70ec784a03dcad6a2ae16b49fe9b5aee48c903), and slice opto-EPSC projection targeting is supported [(Muñoz et al., 2018-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/736fc21505a2b3ce1e644825e9a9f356f1188622) with DR glutamatergic synapses onto TH-positive neurons [(Qi et al., 2014-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/be217d5bfacb459ed03f02eac8c23f5575c90fd7).

## Weaknesses 
The strongest contradictions are that passive open-field stimulation and cocaine may not provide clean reciprocal DA-5-HT crosstalk readouts. Long et al. found reward-matched DA transients had small striatal spiking effects, so passive conditions may be underpowered [(Long et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/be9104873f956f19764c8669b4d23acc948b846d). Browne et al. found DRN 5-HT stimulation did not alter primary reinforcer responding and did not support DA involvement [(Browne et al., 2018-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/977c2fbb1cbfeaef0cd945fb4a3ec46eb310b91f). Cocaine is a reuptake blocker and its DA sensor signals can be method-dependent, so it is not a pure vesicular release positive control [(Salinas et al., 2023-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6d2080c01166b331aa2335fba07d7254f855731b). The DR glutamatergic synapse evidence is VGluT3-based rather than SERT-specific, so it cannot validate SERT-neuron glutamate co-release [(Qi et al., 2014-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/be217d5bfacb459ed03f02eac8c23f5575c90fd7). The exact 4 ms, 20 Hz, 5-pulse, 30-burst protocol is not validated and may interact with intrinsic firing and opsin kinetics [(Velden et al., 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/10b41c5beec5006c2af3d8f44c1eccc577397b86). The analysis also lacks photobleaching correction, permutation baseline tests, and sensor-specific calibration needed for long cocaine recordings [(Wei et al., 2018-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7bcd437e90823a10be7775bd25178588a0d9eafb).

## Top 3 Suggestions 
Add a validation pilot before main experiments: confirm DAT-IRES-Cre VTA and SERT-Cre DRN ChrimsonR-tdTomato specificity by TH/DAT/SERT colocalization, quantify off-target expression in Cre-negative controls, standardize ChrimsonR wavelength, optical power, 4 ms pulse width, 20 Hz burst structure, and 5-pulse/30-burst train, and include light-only, no-opsin, motion-correction, and sensor-calibration controls for dLight1.2 and 5-HT3.0, as recommended by [(Long et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/be9104873f956f19764c8669b4d23acc948b846d), [(Velden et al., 2020-02)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/10b41c5beec5006c2af3d8f44c1eccc577397b86), and [(He et al., 2023-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e5c0d470c12ca480bf416e42905a2f708399cd8d).

Rework the crosstalk paradigm to test reciprocal release in the same animals or tightly matched cohorts using dual-sensor or sequential DA and 5-HT photometry in NAc and DS, add a reward-predictive or choice task and titrate stimulation to reward-matched and supra-reward levels, and treat cocaine as a sensor/reuptake control rather than a direct release comparator by adding a 5-HT positive control such as DRN SERT stimulation or a 5-HT agonist, with event-locked analysis, photometry depth test, selectivity index, photobleaching correction, mixed-effects models, effect sizes, and multiple-comparison correction, as supported by [(Long et al., 2024-07)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/be9104873f956f19764c8669b4d23acc948b846d), [(Salinas et al., 2023-09)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6d2080c01166b331aa2335fba07d7254f855731b), and [(Wei et al., 2018-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7bcd437e90823a10be7775bd25178588a0d9eafb).

Make Experiment 3 explicitly SERT-specific and pharmacologically controlled: record opto-EPSCs in picrotoxin from identified TH-positive and non-TH neurons in DS, NAc shell, OFC, and VTA, apply NBQX/AP5 only to confirm glutamate dependence, add 5-HT receptor blockers, paired-pulse and input-output curves, and validate SERT axon VGluT3 co-expression and synaptic apposition by immunostaining or electron microscopy, with predefined sample sizes and statistical tests, addressing the VGluT3-to-SERT generalization gap in [(Qi et al., 2014-11)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/be217d5bfacb459ed03f02eac8c23f5575c90fd7) and the need for SERT-specific controls in [(Muñoz et al., 2018-04)](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/736fc21505a2b3ce1e644825e9a9f356f1188622).
